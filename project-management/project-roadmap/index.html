Project Roadmap: What It Is and How to Create One

A project roadmap is a high-level visual summary of a project's goals, major deliverables and milestones over time. It communicates direction to stakeholders without the detail of a full plan.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 21 September 2026
  • 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • A roadmap shows goals, phases and milestones at a high level.
  • It is for stakeholders; the plan is for the team.
  • Keep it visual and update it regularly.

Roadmap vs project plan

RoadmapProject plan
DetailHigh levelDetailed
AudienceExecutives and stakeholdersProject team
FocusGoals, phases, milestonesTasks, resources, dependencies

What to include

  • Goals and objectives.
  • Major phases or workstreams.
  • Key milestones.
  • Major deliverables.
  • Dependencies and risks.
  • Timeline, usually in months or quarters.

How to create one

  1. Clarify goals and success measures.
  2. Identify phases and major deliverables.
  3. Set milestones.
  4. Arrange them on a timeline.
  5. Highlight key dependencies and risks.
  6. Share and update regularly.

Agile roadmaps

Agile teams often use Now-Next-Later or outcome-based roadmaps. See the Agile roadmap.

Frequently asked questions

What is a project roadmap?

A high-level visual summary of goals, phases and milestones over time.

Who uses a project roadmap?

Executives, sponsors and stakeholders who need the big picture.

Is a project roadmap the same as a Gantt chart?

No. A Gantt chart is detailed; a roadmap is high level, though it may look similar.

How often should a project roadmap be updated?

Monthly or quarterly, and after major changes.

How is a project roadmap different from a product roadmap?

A product roadmap shows a product's evolution and is often ongoing; a project roadmap ends when the project does.

How does a project roadmap compare with an integrated master plan?

An integrated master plan is a formal, event-based plan used in large programmes, far more detailed than a project roadmap; see integrated master plan.

What tools create project roadmaps?

Most project management tools offer roadmap or timeline views.

Should a project roadmap include dates?

Usually at month or quarter level, avoiding false precision.

How is a technology roadmap different from a project roadmap?

A technology roadmap plans technology changes aligned with business goals, rather than the delivery of one project.

How is a project roadmap presented?

As a timeline, swimlane chart or Now-Next-Later board.
