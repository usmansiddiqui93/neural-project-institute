The Agile Project Manager: Role, Skills and How to Become One

Scrum has no "project manager" role, yet many organisations employ Agile project managers. They typically coordinate across teams, manage budgets and stakeholders, and keep Agile delivery aligned with business governance.

What an Agile project manager does

  • Aligns the project with business goals and secures funding.
  • Coordinates dependencies between teams, suppliers and departments.
  • Manages budget, contracts and high-level risk.
  • Reports progress to sponsors using Agile metrics.
  • Supports the Product Owner with stakeholder management.
  • Removes organisational obstacles.
  • Ensures compliance with governance requirements, often in hybrid environments.

How the role differs from traditional project management

Traditional PMAgile PM
PlanningCreates a detailed planFacilitates layered, adaptive planning
TeamAssigns tasksSupports a self-managing team
ScopeControls changes to scopeHelps balance flexible scope against fixed time and budget
ReportingTasks, milestones, earned valueIncrements, burnup, outcomes
Leadership styleCommand and controlServant leadership

Essential skills

  • Agile frameworks: Scrum, Kanban and scaling methods.
  • Stakeholder management and communication.
  • Budgeting and contract management for Agile work.
  • Risk management.
  • Facilitation and coaching.
  • Comfort with ambiguity and changing plans.

How to become an Agile project manager

  1. Learn the foundations of project management and of Agile.
  2. Join or lead work on an Agile team to gain hands-on experience.
  3. Take a recognised certification such as PMI-ACP, a Scrum certification or PRINCE2 Agile.
  4. Practise facilitation: run retrospectives, planning sessions and workshops.
  5. Build a record of outcomes delivered, not just projects managed.

Key takeaways

  • Agile project managers coordinate delivery, budget and stakeholders around Agile teams.
  • They lead through service and facilitation, not task assignment.
  • Hands-on experience plus a recognised certification is the usual path.

Frequently asked questions

Is an Agile project manager the same as a Scrum Master?

No. The Scrum Master focuses on one team's effectiveness with Scrum; an Agile project manager usually coordinates delivery, budget and stakeholders across teams.

Do Agile project managers need certification?

Not always, but certifications such as PMI-ACP or a Scrum certification help show your knowledge, especially when changing roles.
