How to Learn Agile and Get Certified: A Practical Path

You can learn the basics of Agile in a weekend, but becoming good at it takes practice. This guide sets out a practical learning path and compares the main certifications.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 26 September 2026
  • 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Learn the Manifesto, Scrum Guide and Kanban basics first.
  • Practice matters more than certificates.
  • Pick a certification that matches your role and market.

Step 1: Learn the foundations

  • Read the Agile Manifesto and its principles.
  • Read the official Scrum Guide; it is short and free.
  • Learn the basics of Kanban.
  • Work through this free guide from start to finish.

Step 2: Practise

  • Use a personal Kanban board for your own work.
  • Offer to run a retrospective for your team.
  • Join an Agile team, even in a supporting role.
  • Volunteer for a community or non-profit project run in sprints.
  • Attend local or online Agile meetups.

Step 4: Keep learning

  • Read widely: Agile, Lean, product management and facilitation.
  • Get a mentor or coach.
  • Reflect on your own practice in regular retrospectives.
  • Teach others; explaining Agile is one of the best ways to understand it.

Do you need a certification?

Not always. Many employers value demonstrated experience more. A certification helps when you are changing careers, applying to organisations that require it, or want a structured way to learn.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest Agile certification to get?

Entry-level Scrum certifications such as PSM I or CSM are the most common starting points. The right one depends on your role and local employers.

Can I learn Agile without working in IT?

Yes. Agile is used in marketing, HR, finance and many other fields. See Agile beyond software.

What is the best Agile certification for beginners?

Entry-level Scrum certifications such as PSM I or CSM are common starting points. PMAI from Neural Project Institute covers practical PM, Agile and AI skills.

What is the difference between the PSM and CSM Agile certification?

PSM I from Scrum.org is an online assessment with training optional; CSM from Scrum Alliance requires a live course with a certified trainer.

What is the PMI-ACP Agile certification?

PMI's Agile Certified Practitioner credential for project managers working in Agile settings. It has training and experience requirements.

Can I get Agile certified without experience?

Yes. Entry-level Scrum certifications do not require experience, though practical experience makes them more valuable to employers.

How long does it take to learn Agile?

The basics take a few days to learn. Becoming skilled takes months of practice on real teams.

Is the Scrum Guide a free way to learn Agile?

Yes. The official Scrum Guide is short and free to read online at scrumguides.org.

What is the best Agile certification for project managers?

PMI-ACP and PRINCE2 Agile are designed with project managers in mind; a Scrum certification also helps. See Agile project manager.

How can I learn Agile by practising without a job in Agile?

Use a personal Kanban board, run a retrospective for your team, volunteer for a community project run in sprints and attend Agile meetups.

Do Agile certifications expire?

It depends on the issuer. Some require renewal fees or continuing education; others do not expire. Check the issuer's website.
