The Agile Product Manager: Responsibilities and Skills

An Agile product manager decides what to build and why, using continuous customer feedback to guide the team. They connect strategy, customers and the delivery team.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Agile product managers decide what to build and why, guided by customer feedback.
  • The Product Owner role often overlaps with or supports the product manager.
  • Discovery and delivery run in parallel.

Responsibilities

  • Understand customers through research, interviews and data.
  • Define the product vision and strategy.
  • Own the roadmap and communicate it.
  • Set measurable outcomes and success metrics.
  • Prioritise the backlog based on value, effort and risk.
  • Work daily with the team to clarify needs.
  • Measure results after release and decide what to do next.

Product manager vs Product Owner

In Scrum, the Product Owner is accountable for maximising the product's value and managing the Product Backlog. In many organisations, one person is both product manager and Product Owner. In larger organisations, a product manager may focus on market and strategy, while one or more Product Owners work closely with delivery teams on the backlog.

Discovery and delivery

Agile product managers work in two loops at once. Discovery tests ideas cheaply, through interviews, prototypes and experiments, to learn what is worth building. Delivery builds and releases the ideas that survive discovery. Keeping both loops running reduces the risk of building features nobody uses.

Prioritisation techniques

  • MoSCoW: Must, Should, Could, Won't have this time.
  • RICE: Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.
  • Cost of delay: what does waiting cost us each week?
  • Kano model: basic needs, performance features and delighters.

Essential skills

  • Customer empathy and research.
  • Data analysis.
  • Clear writing and storytelling.
  • Negotiation and saying no.
  • Enough technical understanding to discuss trade-offs.

Frequently asked questions

Does an Agile team need a product manager?

It needs someone who owns value and priorities. In Scrum, that is the Product Owner; the job title varies.

How is an Agile product manager measured?

By outcomes: customer and business results such as adoption, retention or revenue, rather than the number of features shipped.
