Agile Portfolio Management: Aligning Strategy and Delivery

Agile portfolio management applies Agile and Lean thinking to the question of what an organisation should invest in. It connects strategy to the work of Agile teams and lets funding follow evidence, rather than being fixed in an annual plan.

Traditional vs Agile portfolio management

Traditional PPMAgile portfolio management
FundingProjects funded annually by scopeValue streams or teams funded for a period
PlanningAnnual, detailedContinuous, with regular reviews
PrioritisationBusiness cases compete onceOngoing, by cost of delay and outcomes
GovernanceStage gates and reportsLightweight, based on working results
ChangeRe-planning is expensiveExpected; funding can shift each review

Key practices

  • Lean budgets: fund long-lived value streams or teams and let them decide how to spend within guardrails.
  • Portfolio Kanban: large initiatives (epics) move through stages such as Funnel, Reviewing, Analysing, Ready, Implementing and Done, with WIP limits.
  • Lean business cases: short documents with a hypothesis, a minimum viable product and measurable outcomes.
  • Participatory budgeting: stakeholders decide together how to allocate funding.
  • Regular portfolio reviews: typically quarterly, to shift investment based on results.

Prioritising with WSJF

Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF) prioritises work that delivers the most value soonest:

WSJF = Cost of Delay ÷ Job Size

In SAFe, cost of delay combines user and business value, time criticality, and risk reduction or opportunity enablement, each scored relatively. Items with the highest WSJF go first.

Getting started

  1. Make all current initiatives visible on one portfolio board.
  2. Limit how many large initiatives are in progress at once.
  3. Define a few strategic themes and measurable outcomes.
  4. Introduce quarterly portfolio reviews.
  5. Move gradually from project funding to funding stable teams.

Key takeaways

  • Agile portfolio management funds value streams and shifts investment based on evidence.
  • Portfolio Kanban and WIP limits stop too much starting at once.
  • WSJF prioritises by cost of delay divided by job size.

Frequently asked questions

Is Agile portfolio management the same as Lean Portfolio Management?

Lean Portfolio Management is SAFe's specific version of Agile portfolio management.

Who runs Agile portfolio management?

Usually a group of business, technology and finance leaders, often supported by an Agile PMO.
