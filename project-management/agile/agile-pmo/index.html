What Is an Agile PMO? Roles and How to Transform Your PMO

An Agile PMO supports Agile delivery across an organisation. Instead of controlling projects through templates and gates, it enables teams, connects strategy to execution and helps leaders make better investment decisions.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • An Agile PMO enables and coordinates rather than controls.
  • It runs portfolio management, metrics and coaching.
  • Transform gradually, starting with willing teams.

Traditional PMO vs Agile PMO

Traditional PMOAgile PMO
Main roleControl and complianceEnablement and coordination
ReportingStatus reports and RAG ratingsWorking results, flow and outcome metrics
PlanningDetailed project plansPortfolio roadmaps and capacity
GovernanceStage gatesLightweight guardrails and regular reviews
Relationship with teamsOversightService and coaching

Key responsibilities of an Agile PMO

  • Run Agile portfolio management and capacity planning.
  • Provide consistent, lightweight metrics across teams.
  • Coordinate dependencies and release calendars.
  • Coach teams and leaders, often as part of an Agile centre of excellence.
  • Maintain shared tools and practices.
  • Support budgeting, forecasting and compliance in an Agile way.

In scaled frameworks

In SAFe, this function is sometimes called an Agile PMO or a Value Management Office. It supports Lean Portfolio Management, helps run portfolio events and coordinates Agile Release Trains.

Transforming a traditional PMO

  1. Agree a new purpose with leadership: enabling value delivery.
  2. Replace document-heavy templates with lightweight ones.
  3. Swap status reports for live boards and dashboards.
  4. Train PMO staff in Agile, Lean and coaching.
  5. Pilot the new approach with a few willing teams.
  6. Expand based on what works.

Frequently asked questions

Does an Agile organisation need a PMO?

Not always, but larger organisations usually benefit from a central function that coordinates portfolio decisions, metrics and governance.

What skills does an Agile PMO need?

Portfolio management, data and metrics, facilitation, coaching and a solid understanding of Agile and Lean.
