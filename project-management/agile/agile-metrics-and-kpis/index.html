Agile Metrics and KPIs: What to Measure and Why

Agile teams measure to learn and improve, not to rank people. The right handful of metrics shows whether the team is delivering value predictably, at good quality, in a sustainable way.

Delivery and predictability

MetricWhat it shows
VelocityStory points completed per sprint; used for forecasting
Sprint burndownWork remaining in the current sprint
Release burnupProgress towards a release, including scope changes
Sprint goal success rateShare of sprints in which the Sprint Goal was met
Planned vs completedHow reliably the team delivers what it plans

Flow

MetricWhat it shows
Cycle timeTime from starting an item to finishing it
Lead timeTime from the request to delivery
ThroughputItems finished per week
Work in progressItems started but not finished
Cumulative flow diagramWhere work piles up in the workflow

Quality

  • Escaped defects: problems found after release.
  • Defect resolution time.
  • Change failure rate and deployment frequency, two of the DORA metrics widely used by software teams.
  • Technical debt trends.

Outcomes and value

  • Customer satisfaction or Net Promoter Score.
  • Adoption and usage of new features.
  • Business results linked to the release goal, such as conversion or time saved.
  • Time to first value for new initiatives.

Team health

  • Regular team happiness or engagement surveys.
  • Overtime and unplanned work.
  • Retrospective actions completed.

Using metrics well

  • Pick a small set: one or two from each group.
  • Look at trends, not single data points.
  • Discuss metrics in retrospectives, with the team.
  • Never compare velocity between teams or use it to rate individuals.
  • Pair every speed metric with a quality metric, so one is not improved at the expense of the other.
Tip

If a metric becomes a target, people will optimise for the number rather than the result. Use metrics as questions, not verdicts.

Key takeaways

  • Measure delivery, flow, quality, outcomes and team health.
  • Use a small set of metrics and watch trends.
  • Metrics are for learning, not for ranking teams.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most important Agile metric?

There isn't one. Combine a flow or delivery metric with a quality metric and an outcome metric.

What is the difference between lead time and cycle time?

Lead time starts when the request is made; cycle time starts when work actually begins. Both end when the item is delivered.
