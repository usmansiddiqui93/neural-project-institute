What Is an Agile Release Train (ART) in SAFe?

An Agile Release Train (ART) is a long-lived team of Agile teams that plans, commits and delivers together on a shared schedule. It is the central delivery structure of the Scaled Agile Framework.

Characteristics of an ART

  • Typically 50 to 125 people in several Agile teams.
  • Organised around a flow of value, such as a product, customer journey or service.
  • Has all the skills needed to define, build, test and release its solution.
  • Shares a common cadence: all teams start and end iterations together.
  • Plans together every Program Increment (PI), usually 8 to 12 weeks.
  • Is long-lived: work is brought to the train rather than forming new teams for each project.

Key roles

RoleResponsibility
Release Train Engineer (RTE)Facilitates ART events, removes impediments, coaches leaders and teams
Product ManagementDefines and prioritises features in the ART backlog
System ArchitectProvides architectural guidance and enablers
Business OwnersSet business objectives and accept outcomes
Agile teamsDeliver stories and features in iterations

ART events

  • PI Planning: the whole train plans the next increment together.
  • ART Sync: a regular coordination meeting covering progress, dependencies and impediments.
  • System Demo: the integrated result of all teams is shown at the end of each iteration.
  • Inspect and Adapt: a workshop at the end of each PI to review results and solve problems.

Launching an ART

  1. Identify the value stream and the people needed to serve it.
  2. Train leaders and teams.
  3. Prepare the ART backlog of features.
  4. Hold the first PI Planning event.
  5. Run the PI and inspect and adapt at the end.

Key takeaways

  • An ART is a long-lived team of Agile teams, typically 50 to 125 people.
  • It plans together every PI and shares a common cadence.
  • The Release Train Engineer facilitates and coaches the ART.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it called a release train?

Like a train timetable, the ART runs on a fixed, predictable schedule. Features that are ready catch the train; those that aren't wait for the next one.

Does an ART release only at the end of a PI?

No. Planning follows the PI cadence, but teams can release whenever value is ready.
