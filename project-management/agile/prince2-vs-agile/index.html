PRINCE2 vs Agile: Key Differences and How They Combine

PRINCE2 and Agile are often presented as opposites. In practice they answer different questions. PRINCE2 is mainly about governing a project: who decides, when, and within what limits. Agile is mainly about how a team delivers the work. Many organisations use both.

Key takeaways

  • PRINCE2 governs projects; Agile delivers work.
  • They can be combined: PRINCE2 for stages and tolerances, Agile for delivery.
  • PRINCE2 Agile is the official guidance for doing this.

PRINCE2 in brief

PRINCE2 (PRojects IN Controlled Environments) is a process-based project management method that originated with the UK government and is now owned by PeopleCert. It divides a project into management stages, each approved by a project board. The project manager runs the project day to day within agreed tolerances for time, cost, scope, quality, risk and benefits, and escalates only when a tolerance is forecast to be exceeded. This is called management by exception.

Agile in brief

Agile delivers work in short iterations with continuous customer feedback, a prioritised backlog and self-organising teams. See what is Agile project management.

Key differences

When PRINCE2 fits

  • Large or public-sector projects that need clear accountability and audit trails.
  • Projects with several suppliers, where roles and approvals must be explicit.
  • Organisations that already use PRINCE2 for governance.

Combining them: PRINCE2 Agile

PRINCE2 Agile, first published in 2015, shows how to run Agile delivery inside PRINCE2 governance. The board sets tolerances; the delivery team works in sprints within them. Its key idea is to fix time and cost and let scope and quality criteria flex, which matches Agile thinking. Stages often align with a number of sprints or releases.

Tip

If your organisation insists on PRINCE2 governance, you can still run Agile teams. Agree that scope is the main tolerance that flexes, and report progress through working increments.

Frequently asked questions

PRINCE2 vs Agile: is PRINCE2 itself Agile?

Classic PRINCE2 is not an Agile method, but it is flexible enough to govern Agile delivery. PRINCE2 Agile is the official guidance for doing so.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: which certification should I take?

It depends on your market. PRINCE2 is widely requested in the UK, Europe and the public sector; Agile and Scrum certifications are in demand in technology and product roles. See how to learn Agile.

What is the main difference between PRINCE2 and Agile?

PRINCE2 focuses on governance and control through stages, a project board and tolerances; Agile focuses on delivery through short iterations, a prioritised backlog and self-organising teams.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: can PRINCE2 and Scrum be used together?

Yes. PRINCE2 can provide the governance layer, with the project board setting tolerances, while the delivery team works in Scrum Sprints within them. PRINCE2 Agile describes how.

What is PRINCE2 Agile, and how does it combine PRINCE2 and Agile?

Official guidance, first published in 2015, for running Agile delivery within PRINCE2 governance. It recommends fixing time and cost and flexing scope and quality criteria within agreed limits.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: who owns PRINCE2?

PRINCE2 originated with the UK government and is now owned by PeopleCert, which also runs its certification exams.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: what is management by exception?

The project board sets tolerances for time, cost, scope, quality, risk and benefits, and the project manager only escalates when a tolerance is forecast to be exceeded.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: is PRINCE2 a Waterfall method?

Not strictly. PRINCE2 is a governance method that can wrap sequential or Agile delivery, though it is often used alongside Waterfall-style plans.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: does PRINCE2 have a Scrum Master?

No. PRINCE2 defines roles such as executive, project manager and team manager. In PRINCE2 Agile, Scrum roles can sit within the team manager's delivery team.

PRINCE2 vs Agile: is PRINCE2 still used?

Yes, particularly in the UK, Europe, Australia and the public sector, where clear accountability and audit trails are valued.

Which is better for my career, PRINCE2 or Agile?

Look at job adverts in your target market. Many project managers hold both a PRINCE2 or PMP-style qualification and an Agile one. See how to learn Agile and get certified.
