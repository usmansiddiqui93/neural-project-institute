PMP Certification: Requirements, 2026 Exam, Cost and Prep

PMP certification (Project Management Professional) is the Project Management Institute's flagship credential for experienced project managers: you need three to five years of project leadership within the last 10 years, depending on your education, plus 35 hours of project management training, and you must pass a 180-question exam. PMI launched a new version of the exam on 9 July 2026, and from 1 December 2026 live training only counts if it comes from an approved provider. This guide explains the requirements, the new exam, the costs, the application step by step, a 12-week study plan with sample questions, and how to keep the PMP active.

Key takeaways

  • You need project leadership experience within the last 10 years: five years with a secondary diploma, four with an associate's degree, three with a bachelor's degree, or two with a degree from a PMI GAC-accredited programme.
  • You also need 35 hours of project management training or an active CAPM. From 1 December 2026, live instructor-led training only counts if it comes from a PMI Authorized Training Partner or an eligible accredited programme.
  • The exam launched on 9 July 2026 has 180 questions in 240 minutes, weighted People 33%, Process 41% and Business Environment 26%.
  • Fees are widely reported as US$425 for PMI members and US$675 for non-members in the US (checked 25 October 2026); confirm the price at checkout.
  • Most candidates study for two to three months; practise scenario questions and full timed exams, not only definitions.
  • The PMP lasts three years. Renew it by earning 60 PDUs and paying the renewal fee.

What is PMP certification?

PMP certification is a professional credential awarded by the Project Management Institute (PMI) to project managers who meet its education and experience rules and pass the PMP exam. It shows that you can lead projects using predictive, Agile and hybrid approaches, manage people and stakeholders, and connect project work to business outcomes. It is valid for three years and renewed through continuing education.

PMI is a not-for-profit professional association rather than a regulator, so the PMP is voluntary. It is, however, one of the credentials most often named in project manager job adverts. Our guide to what PMI is explains the organisation behind it and its other 12 certifications.

Who the PMP suits, and who should wait

  • Good fit: you have led projects for at least three years and want a credential recognised across industries and countries, or you hold the CAPM and now have the experience to step up.
  • Wait or choose another route: you are new to project work (consider the CAPM certification), your target employers mainly ask for PRINCE2, or your role is purely Scrum-based.

If you are still comparing options, our overview of project management certifications sets the PMP alongside PRINCE2, APM, Scrum and Agile credentials.

PMP certification requirements

According to PMI's PMP page (checked 25 October 2026), you need one combination of education and experience from the table, plus the training requirement. The experience must be professional, gained within the last 10 years, and overlapping projects cannot be counted twice.

Your highest educationProject management experience (within 10 years)Training
Secondary diploma (high school, GED or global equivalent)5 years (60 months)35 hours, or an active CAPM
Associate's degree or vocational/trade school diploma4 years (48 months)35 hours, or an active CAPM
Bachelor's degree or higher3 years (36 months)35 hours, or an active CAPM
Bachelor's or postgraduate degree from a PMI GAC-accredited programme2 years (24 months)35 hours, or an active CAPM

GAC is PMI's Global Accreditation Center, which accredits university project management degrees.

What counts as project management experience

PMI is looking for time spent leading and directing projects, not simply working on them. You do not need the job title "project manager", but your role must involve responsibility for parts of the project life cycle. Activities that usually count include:

  • Defining scope and objectives, or writing a project charter.
  • Managing schedules, budgets, risks and resources.
  • Leading a team, engaging stakeholders and managing changes.
  • Leading Agile delivery or closing a project with handover and lessons learned.

Pure technical work with no coordination role, and routine operations, usually do not count on their own.

The 35 hours of training

You need 35 hours (often called contact hours) of project management education, completed before you apply. An active CAPM meets this requirement on its own. Qualifying routes include PMI's own on-demand PMP Exam Prep course, a self-paced course from any organisation, a live course from an approved provider, or relevant modules in an accredited academic programme. Our guide to 35 contact hours for the PMP explains what counts and how to document it.

The 1 December 2026 training rule

PMI has changed which live training counts towards the 35 hours. From 1 December 2026, live instructor-led classes (in person or virtual) only count if they are delivered by a PMI Authorized Training Partner (ATP), a China Registered Education Provider (REP) or an eligible accredited higher education programme, including PMI GAC programmes. Self-paced, on-demand courses can still come from any organisation, and PMI says training completed before 1 December 2026 remains eligible under the previous rules.

Your situationDoes it count?
Self-paced online course from any provider, any dateYes
Live course from any provider, completed before 1 December 2026Yes, under the previous rules; keep the certificate
Live course from a PMI Authorized Training Partner, any dateYes
Live course from a non-ATP provider, taken on or after 1 December 2026No
Project management modules in a GAC-accredited or other eligible accredited degreeYes
An active CAPM certificationYes, it replaces the 35 hours
Tip

If you are booking a live boot camp that starts in late November and finishes in December, ask the provider whether it is a PMI Authorized Training Partner before you pay. PMI's ATP directory on pmi.org lets you check.

The rule does not change the experience rules, the exam or renewal.

The PMP exam in 2026

The current exam is based on PMI's 2026 PMP Examination Content Outline (ECO). The format, as described by PMI and the ECO:

ItemCurrent PMP exam (from 9 July 2026)
Questions180: 170 scored and 10 unscored pretest questions mixed in
Time240 minutes, with two 10-minute breaks
DomainsPeople 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26%
ApproachesAbout 40% predictive; the rest Agile/adaptive and hybrid, spread across all domains
DeliveryPearson VUE test centre (PMI recommends this) or online proctored
Languages15, including English, Arabic, French, German and Spanish
AttemptsUp to three within your one-year eligibility period

What changed on 9 July 2026

The last date to sit the previous (2021) version was 8 July 2026. The biggest change is the weight given to the business context of projects.

Domain2021 exam2026 examApproximate scored questions (2026)
People42%33%About 56
Process50%41%About 70
Business Environment8%26%About 44

The approximate counts apply the weights to the 170 scored questions; PMI notes that exact numbers vary by exam form. The exam is also 10 minutes longer and has two breaks instead of one. PMI says the update puts more emphasis on outcomes, value and business impact. If you studied from 2021 materials, add governance, compliance, change and organisational change topics.

Question types

The 2026 ECO lists two new formats: case or scenario questions (a longer scenario followed by several questions) and graphic-based questions (interpreting charts, dashboards or artefacts). These sit alongside multiple choice, multiple response, matching, point and click (hotspot) and pull-down list items.

According to the ECO, the first break comes after the case-study section and the second roughly half way through the remaining questions. Once you start a break you cannot go back to earlier questions, so review flagged items before each break.

AI and sustainability on the exam

The ECO names AI as an emerging trend PMI considered in its job analysis, but no task is dedicated to it. Expect AI to appear as project context, for example a team adopting AI tools or a stakeholder worried about data. Sustainability appears in enablers on cost of quality, compliance and risk responses. Apply normal judgement on risk, governance and stakeholders to these scenarios.

The exam is not a test of the PMBOK Guide alone. PMI lists the PMBOK Guide among its references, but questions test how you would act in a situation, drawing on predictive, Agile and hybrid practice.

How to get PMP certification step by step

The process below follows PMI's PMP page and certification handbook. Allow two to four months from starting your training to sitting the exam.

  1. Check your eligibility. Use the requirements table and count your non-overlapping months within the last 10 years. Download the current PMP Examination Content Outline from pmi.org.
  2. Complete the 35 hours (or confirm your CAPM is active). If you choose live training starting on or after 1 December 2026, use a PMI Authorized Training Partner. Save the certificate.
  3. Decide whether to join PMI. Join before you pay the exam fee if you want the member price.
  4. Create a PMI account and start the online application. Enter your education, training and each project separately, with dates, your role and a short description.
  5. Submit the application. PMI reviews it and emails you. Training providers commonly report a review of about five business days.
  6. Pay the exam fee. After payment you receive your eligibility details; some applications are selected for audit at this stage.
  7. Schedule the exam with Pearson VUE. Choose a test centre or online proctoring. You have one year from approval to take the exam, with up to three attempts.
  8. Study and sit the exam. Follow a study plan, take full-length practice exams and book a date that gives you a deadline.
  9. Maintain your PMP. Your three-year cycle starts on the day you pass. Earn and report 60 PDUs and pay the renewal fee before it ends.

Writing your project descriptions

Each project needs a short summary in your own words. A good structure is one sentence each for the objective, your role, your responsibilities and the outcome:

  • Objective: "Replace the regional CRM system for 120 sales staff within a budget of 400,000."
  • Your role: "Project lead reporting to the sales director, managing a team of six."
  • Responsibilities: "Defined scope with stakeholders, built the schedule and risk register, ran fortnightly sprint reviews with the vendor and managed change requests."
  • Outcome: "Went live on schedule; adoption reached the agreed target within three months; closed the project with a lessons-learned review."

If your application is audited

PMI selects some applications for audit at random. If yours is chosen, you cannot schedule the exam until you have sent copies of your diploma or degree, your training certificates (not needed with an active CAPM) and supervisor signatures confirming each project.

Training providers report a 90-day window to respond. Keep your evidence together from the start so you can reply quickly.

How to prepare: a 12-week PMP study plan

Most candidates study for two to three months alongside work, at roughly 8 to 10 hours a week. This plan is our suggestion, built around the 2026 domain weights; adjust it to your experience.

5Process: quality, value-based delivery, status and closure

Choosing study materials

  • PMI's resources: the free Examination Content Outline, PMI's PMP Exam Prep course and practice exam, and PMI Study Hall. Members get the PMBOK Guide free.
  • A course and simulator built on the 2026 ECO: check they cover the larger Business Environment domain and the new question formats, with full-length timed exams and explanations.
  • Background reading: our guides to Agile methodology and risk management cover topics many candidates find hardest.

How to know you are ready

PMI does not publish a pass mark. It sets the standard through psychometric analysis, and your result shows pass or fail with a performance rating for each domain. Many training providers suggest booking when you consistently score around 75% to 80% or more on full-length practice exams from a reputable simulator. That is a rule of thumb, not a PMI figure, but it is a sensible signal that you can sustain performance over four hours.

Sample PMP exam questions with explanations

These questions are our own, written in the style of the 2026 exam. The explanations matter more than the answers: the exam rewards the action a well-informed project manager would take first.

Question 1 (People)

Two senior developers on your hybrid project disagree strongly about the architecture for a new feature, and the disagreement is slowing the team. What should you do first?

  • A. Make the decision yourself so the team can move on.
  • B. Escalate the issue to the sponsor.
  • C. Meet the two developers to understand both positions and help them reach an agreed solution.
  • D. Ask the team to vote at the next stand-up.

Answer: C. The exam favours collaborative problem-solving as the first step in conflict, because it addresses the cause and keeps ownership with the team. Escalation (B) comes later if the team cannot resolve it; deciding alone (A) or voting (D) may leave the underlying disagreement unresolved.

Question 2 (Process, with a calculation)

Your project has a planned value of 200,000, an earned value of 180,000 and an actual cost of 200,000. Which statement is correct?

  • A. The project is ahead of schedule and under budget.
  • B. The project is behind schedule and over budget.
  • C. The project is on schedule and over budget.
  • D. The project is behind schedule and on budget.

Answer: B. Schedule variance is EV minus PV, so 180,000 minus 200,000 = minus 20,000 (behind schedule). Cost variance is EV minus AC, so 180,000 minus 200,000 = minus 20,000 (over budget).

Exam day: what to expect

  • At a test centre: arrive early with the identification named in your confirmation email; personal items go in a locker.
  • Online: run Pearson VUE's system test first and clear your room. A proctor watches throughout, and leaving the camera view outside scheduled breaks can end your exam.
  • Timing and breaks: you have about 80 seconds per question. Take both breaks, and review flagged questions before each one.
  • Results: a provisional pass or fail appears on screen, followed by a report rating your performance in each domain.

PMI recommends the test centre where possible.

Is PMP certification worth it?

For most experienced project managers, the PMP is worth it if employers in your market ask for it. Evidence from PMI's own research is positive: a PMI blog post from April 2026 reports that, in PMI's most recent salary survey covering 21 countries, PMP holders reported median salaries 17% higher than those without it. In the United States the gap was 24%, with a median of US$135,000 for PMP holders against US$109,157 for non-holders. The figures show association, not proof that the certificate alone causes the difference.

It is most likely to pay off when job adverts you want list it as required or preferred, when you work internationally or lead mixed predictive and Agile projects, and when your employer funds the costs. If target employers mostly ask for PRINCE2 or APM, or you have less than three years of leadership experience, another credential may serve you better first.

Preparing also fills gaps in areas you have not led before, such as procurement or governance. Our guide on how to become a project manager shows where the PMP fits in a typical career path.

Keeping your PMP: renewal and PDUs

The PMP is valid for three years from the day you pass. To renew, you earn 60 Professional Development Units (PDUs), where one PDU equals one hour of learning or giving back, report them in PMI's Continuing Certification Requirements System (CCRS), agree to PMI's code of ethics and pay the renewal fee.

RulePMP requirement
Total PDUs per three-year cycle60
Education PDUsAt least 35
Giving back PDUs (volunteering, creating content, working as a practitioner)No more than 25
Talent Triangle minimumAt least 8 PDUs in each of Ways of Working, Power Skills and Business Acumen
Renewal fee (reported)US$60 for members, US$150 for non-members

A simple approach is to earn about 20 PDUs a year from webinars, courses, chapter events and some volunteering. If you miss the deadline, training providers report that the certification is suspended for a year, during which you can still complete the requirements; after that it expires and you would need to requalify.

Common PMP mistakes to avoid

  • Counting overlapping months twice. PMI counts calendar months once, however many projects ran at the same time.
  • Listing experience older than 10 years. It will not count towards the requirement.
  • Booking an ineligible live course. From 1 December 2026, live training must come from an Authorized Training Partner or an eligible accredited programme.
  • Joining PMI after paying the exam fee. You will pay the non-member price.
  • Studying only from 2021-era materials. The Business Environment domain is now more than three times its old weight.
  • Choosing what your organisation would do. The exam asks what a well-informed project manager should do, often the first step, following good practice.

If you are building evidence of your experience, the documents you create on real projects help with both the application and the exam. Our free project charter template, risk register template and RACI matrix template are good places to start.

Frequently asked questions

What are the PMP certification requirements?

Project leadership experience gained within the last 10 years (five years with a secondary diploma, four with an associate's or vocational diploma, three with a bachelor's degree, or two with a degree from a PMI GAC-accredited programme), plus 35 hours of project management training or an active CAPM.

How much does PMP certification cost?

Independent providers report US exam fees of US$425 for PMI members and US$675 for non-members in 2026, plus your training costs. Check the price at checkout, as fees vary by country.

How many questions are on the PMP exam and how long is it?

180 questions (170 scored and 10 unscored) in 240 minutes, with two 10-minute breaks.

What changed in the PMP exam in July 2026?

The domains are now People 33%, Process 41% and Business Environment 26% (previously 42%, 50% and 8%). The exam adds case-study and graphic-based questions, 10 extra minutes and a second break.

Can I get the PMP without a degree?

Yes. With a secondary diploma you need five years of project leadership experience within the last 10 years, plus the 35 hours of training.

Does my CAPM count towards the PMP?

Yes. An active CAPM meets the PMP's 35-hour training requirement. You still need the full experience requirement.

Does my 35-hour course still count after 1 December 2026?

Yes if you completed it before that date, or if it is self-paced. Live courses taken from that date must come from a PMI Authorized Training Partner, a China REP or an eligible accredited programme.

Is the PMP exam hard? What is the passing score?

It is demanding because most questions are long scenarios that test judgement over four hours. PMI does not publish a passing score; it uses psychometric analysis and reports performance by domain.

How long does it take to get PMP certified?

Most candidates take two to four months from starting training to passing, including about 8 to 12 weeks of study. Your eligibility period lasts one year from approval.

Can I take the PMP exam online?

Yes. You can sit it online with a remote proctor or at a Pearson VUE test centre, which PMI recommends.

What happens if my application is audited?

PMI asks for copies of your education and training certificates and supervisor confirmation of your experience, and you cannot schedule the exam until the audit is complete.

Do I need to be a PMI member to take the PMP?

No. Membership is optional, but members pay less for the exam, retakes and renewal and get the PMBOK Guide free, so it usually pays for itself in the year you sit the exam.

How do I maintain my PMP?

Earn 60 PDUs every three years (at least 35 from education and at least 8 in each Talent Triangle area), report them in PMI's CCRS and pay the renewal fee.

Is the PMP worth it in 2026?

For experienced project managers in markets where employers ask for it, usually yes. PMI reports PMP holders earn a 17% higher median salary across 21 countries.
