What Is DevOps? Culture, Practices and Metrics

DevOps is a set of cultural values, practices and tools that bring software development and IT operations together, so teams can deliver changes quickly and reliably. It extends Agile and Lean thinking across the whole path from code to customer.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • DevOps unites development and operations around shared goals.
  • CALMS and the Three Ways summarise its principles.
  • Measure it with deployment frequency, lead time, change failure rate and time to restore.

Origins

The term DevOps emerged around 2009, when Patrick Debois organised the first DevOpsDays conference in Ghent, Belgium. It grew from frustration with the wall between developers, who wanted change, and operations, who wanted stability.

The CALMS model

ElementMeaning
CultureShared responsibility and collaboration between development and operations
AutomationAutomate builds, tests, infrastructure and deployments
LeanSmall batches, limited WIP, focus on flow
MeasurementMeasure flow, quality and user outcomes
SharingShare knowledge, tools and lessons learned

The Three Ways

Popularised by The Phoenix Project and The DevOps Handbook:

  1. Flow: speed up the flow of work from development to operations to the customer.
  2. Feedback: create fast feedback from operations and users back to development.
  3. Continual learning and experimentation: build a culture that learns from failure and improves.

Key practices

  • Continuous integration and continuous delivery.
  • Infrastructure as code.
  • Automated testing and monitoring.
  • Small, frequent releases with feature flags.
  • Teams that build and run their own services.
  • Blameless post-incident reviews.

Measuring DevOps performance

The DORA research programme popularised four key measures of software delivery:

  • Deployment frequency: how often you release.
  • Lead time for changes: from commit to production.
  • Change failure rate: share of releases causing problems.
  • Time to restore service: how quickly you recover from failure.

Frequently asked questions

Is DevOps a role or a culture?

Primarily a culture and set of practices. Many organisations also have "DevOps engineer" roles focused on automation and tooling.

How does DevOps relate to Kanban?

Both focus on flow, small batches and visibility. Many operations and platform teams use Kanban to manage their work.
