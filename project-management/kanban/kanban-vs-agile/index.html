Kanban vs Agile: How Kanban Fits Into Agile

Kanban and Agile aren't competitors. Agile is a set of values and principles; Kanban is one method that applies them, with roots in Lean manufacturing rather than software.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Agile is a mindset; Kanban is a method that applies it.
  • Kanban comes from Lean and focuses on flow.
  • Kanban evolves your existing process rather than replacing it.

The short answer

Agile is the mindset described in the Agile Manifesto. Kanban is a method for managing flow that is widely used to practise Agile. Kanban fits Agile's emphasis on delivering value continuously, responding to change and improving regularly.

Where Kanban aligns with Agile

  • Continuous delivery of value.
  • Welcoming changing priorities: new work can be pulled at any time.
  • Sustainable pace through WIP limits.
  • Regular reflection through cadences and reviews.
  • Transparency through visual boards.

Where Kanban is different

Using both

Kanban can be used on its own as an Agile method or combined with other frameworks, for example Scrum teams using Kanban boards and WIP limits. See Kanban vs Scrum.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kanban an Agile methodology?

Yes, Kanban is generally classed as an Agile method, although it comes from Lean manufacturing rather than the original Agile software movement.

Can you be Agile with only Kanban?

Yes. Many teams practise Agile principles using Kanban alone.

What is the difference between Kanban and Agile?

Agile is a set of values and principles for delivering work iteratively and responding to change. Kanban is a specific method, focused on visualising work and limiting WIP, that many teams use to work in an Agile way.

Is Kanban part of Agile?

Kanban is widely treated as one of the Agile approaches, alongside Scrum and XP, even though its roots are in Lean manufacturing. See types of Agile methodologies.

Is Kanban Lean or Agile?

Both. Kanban comes from Lean thinking at Toyota and shares Agile's focus on customer value, feedback and continuous improvement. See Lean Kanban.

Does Kanban follow the Agile Manifesto?

Kanban was not written from the Agile Manifesto, but it fits its values well, favouring collaboration, working results and responding to change over rigid plans.

What are the main Agile frameworks besides Kanban?

Common ones include Scrum, Extreme Programming (XP), Scrumban and scaled frameworks such as SAFe. See Kanban vs Scrum.

Is Kanban iterative?

Kanban is incremental and continuous rather than iterative in fixed cycles. Items are delivered one at a time as they are finished, and regular cadences provide feedback.

Can non-software teams use Kanban as an Agile approach?

Yes. Marketing, HR, finance and operations teams often use Kanban as their entry point to Agile because it does not require new roles or Sprints.

Is Kanban a methodology or a framework?

It is usually called a method: the Kanban Method. It provides principles and practices applied to an existing process rather than a fixed set of roles and events.

Which is better, Agile or Kanban?

They are not alternatives. Agile is the philosophy and Kanban is one way to put it into practice, so the real choice is between Kanban, Scrum or another approach.
