Kanban basics

What Kanban is, its principles, and how boards, cards, swimlanes and cadences work.

  2. Kanban Principles and Practices ExplainedThe principles and six core practices of the Kanban Method, what each means in day-to-day work, and how to apply them to your team.
  3. Kanban Boards: How to Design and Use OneWhat a Kanban board is, its key parts (columns, cards, WIP limits, swimlanes), example boards for different teams, and step-by-step design tips.
  4. Kanban Cards: What to Put on Them and WhyWhat a Kanban card is, the information each card should show, how to size work items, blocker markers, and examples of well-designed cards.
