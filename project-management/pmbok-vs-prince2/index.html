PMBOK vs PRINCE2: Differences and Which to Choose

PMBOK and PRINCE2 are often compared, but they are different kinds of thing. The PMBOK Guide is a standard describing what good project management covers; PRINCE2 is a methodology describing how to run a project.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 10 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • PMBOK is a standard; PRINCE2 is a methodology.
  • They complement each other.
  • Choose certification by region, industry and experience.

Using them together

Because PMBOK describes knowledge and PRINCE2 describes a method, they complement each other. Many project managers hold both PMP and PRINCE2 certifications and use PRINCE2's structure with PMBOK techniques.

How to choose a certification

  • Look at job adverts in your region and industry.
  • Choose PMP if you have several years' experience and want a broadly recognised credential.
  • Choose PRINCE2 Foundation for a fast, method-focused qualification, especially in the UK and Europe.
  • Consider both over time.

Frequently asked questions

Were PMBOK and PRINCE2 developed from each other?

No. They were developed independently, though they cover many of the same ideas.

PMBOK or PRINCE2: which certification is easier to get?

PRINCE2 Foundation is generally quicker to achieve; the PMBOK-based PMP has experience requirements and a longer exam.

What is the main difference between PMBOK and PRINCE2?

PMBOK is a standard describing what good project management covers; PRINCE2 is a methodology describing how to run a project through defined roles, stages and processes.

Can you use PMBOK and PRINCE2 together?

Yes. Many project managers use PRINCE2's structure for governance and PMBOK techniques for scheduling, cost and risk.

Which is more recognised, PMBOK or PRINCE2 certification?

It depends on the region. The PMBOK-based PMP is widely requested in the Americas, the Middle East and Asia; PRINCE2 is common in the UK, Europe, Australia and the public sector.

Who owns PMBOK and PRINCE2?

PMI owns the PMBOK Guide; PeopleCert owns PRINCE2.

Do PMBOK and PRINCE2 certifications require experience?

PRINCE2 Foundation has no experience requirement. The PMP requires 36 months' experience with a four-year degree or 60 months without one.

Should I study PMBOK or PRINCE2 first?

If you have several years' experience and want a broadly recognised credential, PMBOK and the PMP. For a quick, method-focused qualification in the UK or Europe, PRINCE2 Foundation.

How do PMBOK and PRINCE2 handle roles differently?

PMBOK focuses mainly on the project manager; PRINCE2 defines a project board, project manager and team managers with clear responsibilities.

Do PMBOK and PRINCE2 both use knowledge areas?

No. PRINCE2 uses seven principles, seven practices and seven processes, rather than PMBOK's knowledge areas or performance domains.

Is it worth learning both PMBOK and PRINCE2?

For many project managers, yes, especially those working internationally or with both private and public sector clients.
