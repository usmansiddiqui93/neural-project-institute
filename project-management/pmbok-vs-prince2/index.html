PMBOK vs PRINCE2: Differences and Which to Choose

PMBOK and PRINCE2 are often compared, but they are different kinds of thing. The PMBOK Guide is a standard describing what good project management covers; PRINCE2 is a methodology describing how to run a project.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • PMBOK is a standard; PRINCE2 is a methodology.
  • They complement each other.
  • Choose certification by region, industry and experience.

Using them together

Because PMBOK describes knowledge and PRINCE2 describes a method, they complement each other. Many project managers hold both PMP and PRINCE2 certifications and use PRINCE2's structure with PMBOK techniques.

How to choose a certification

  • Look at job adverts in your region and industry.
  • Choose PMP if you have several years' experience and want a broadly recognised credential.
  • Choose PRINCE2 Foundation for a fast, method-focused qualification, especially in the UK and Europe.
  • Consider both over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is PRINCE2 based on PMBOK?

No. They were developed independently, though they cover many of the same ideas.

Which is easier, PMP or PRINCE2?

PRINCE2 Foundation is generally quicker to achieve; PMP has experience requirements and a longer exam.
