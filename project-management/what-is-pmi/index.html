What Is PMI? The Project Management Institute Explained

The Project Management Institute (PMI) is a global, not-for-profit professional association for project management. Founded in the United States in 1969, it publishes standards, runs certifications and supports a worldwide community of practitioners.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • PMI is a global not-for-profit project management association founded in 1969.
  • It publishes the PMBOK Guide and awards the PMP and other certifications.
  • Membership offers discounts, standards and local networking.

What PMI does

  • Publishes standards, including the PMBOK Guide.
  • Awards certifications such as PMP, CAPM and PMI-ACP.
  • Supports local chapters for networking and events.
  • Publishes research and practice guides.
  • Runs conferences and continuing education.

Main PMI certifications

CertificationFor
CAPM (Certified Associate in Project Management)Entry-level, little or no experience
PMP (Project Management Professional)Experienced project managers
PMI-ACP (Agile Certified Practitioner)Agile practitioners
PgMP (Program Management Professional)Programme managers
PfMP (Portfolio Management Professional)Portfolio managers
PMI-RMP (Risk Management Professional)Risk specialists

Requirements and fees change; check pmi.org for current details.

Membership benefits

  • Discounts on certification exams.
  • Access to standards and practice guides.
  • Local chapter events and networking.
  • Ways to earn PDUs for certification renewal.

PMI and other bodies

PMI is one of several project management bodies. Others include PeopleCert, owner of PRINCE2; the Association for Project Management (APM) in the UK; and the International Project Management Association (IPMA). Agile bodies include Scrum.org and Scrum Alliance.

Frequently asked questions

Is PMI membership required for the PMP?

No, but members usually pay a lower exam fee.

What is a PMI chapter?

A local branch of PMI that runs events, networking and professional development in a city or region.
