PDUs Explained: How to Earn Professional Development Units

A professional development unit (PDU) is the measure PMI uses for ongoing learning and contribution to the profession. One PDU generally equals one hour of qualifying activity, and certification holders must earn a set number every three years to renew.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 3 October 2026
  • 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • PDUs measure ongoing learning and giving back to the profession.
  • PMP holders need 60 every three years; CAPM holders need 15.
  • Spread education PDUs across the Talent Triangle and log them as you go.

See PMP certification and CAPM certification.

Education and giving back

PDUs fall into two categories. Education covers courses, webinars, reading and self-study. Giving back covers work as a practitioner, volunteering, mentoring and creating content. For the PMP, at least 35 PDUs must be education, and no more than 25 can come from giving back.

Education PDUs are mapped to PMI's Talent Triangle: Ways of Working, Power Skills and Business Acumen. For the PMP you need at least 8 PDUs in each of the three areas.

Ways to earn PDUs

  • Online courses and webinars from PMI or other providers.
  • PMI chapter events and conferences.
  • Reading books and articles (self-directed learning).
  • Working as a project management practitioner.
  • Mentoring, volunteering or speaking.
  • Courses from education providers, including Neural Project Institute courses such as Agile and leadership topics.

Example PMP plan

ActivityTalent Triangle areaPDUs
Agile practitioner courseWays of Working16
Leadership and negotiation coursePower Skills12
Finance for project managers webinarsBusiness Acumen10
Self-directed readingMixed7
Working as a practitionerGiving back8
Mentoring a junior colleagueGiving back7

This meets the 35 education minimum, the 8 per area minimum and stays within the 25 giving-back maximum.

Reporting PDUs

  1. Log in to PMI's Continuing Certification Requirements System (CCRS).
  2. Choose the activity type and enter the provider, dates and hours.
  3. Allocate education PDUs to Talent Triangle areas.
  4. Keep evidence such as certificates for possible audit.
  5. Once you reach the total, pay the renewal fee.
Tip

Log PDUs as you earn them rather than at the end of the cycle. It avoids a last-minute rush and lost evidence.

Frequently asked questions

What is a PDU?

A professional development unit, PMI's measure of continuing education and contribution. One PDU is usually one hour.

How many PDUs do I need for the PMP?

60 PDUs every three years.

How many PDUs do I need for the CAPM?

15 PDUs every three years.

What is the Talent Triangle for PDUs?

PMI's framework for education PDUs: Ways of Working, Power Skills and Business Acumen.

Can I earn free PDUs?

Yes. Webinars, chapter events, reading and working as a practitioner can all count.

Can I carry over extra PDUs?

PMI allows some PDUs earned in the final year of a cycle to carry over; check PMI's current handbook for the rules.

How do I report PDUs?

Through PMI's online CCRS system.

What happens if I do not earn enough PDUs?

Your certification becomes suspended and may later expire. Check PMI's rules on reinstatement.

Can PDUs be audited?

Yes. Keep certificates and records in case PMI asks for evidence.

Are PDUs the same as contact hours?

No. Contact hours are needed to qualify for an exam; PDUs are needed to maintain a certification. See 35 contact hours.
