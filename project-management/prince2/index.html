What Is PRINCE2? Principles, Practices and Processes

PRINCE2 (PRojects IN Controlled Environments) is a structured, process-based project management method. Originating with the UK government and now owned by PeopleCert, it is widely used in the UK, Europe, Australia and the public sector worldwide.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • PRINCE2 is a stage-based method focused on governance.
  • It has seven principles, seven practices and seven processes.
  • Management by exception keeps the board focused on key decisions.

Seven principles

  • Ensure continued business justification: the project must remain worthwhile.
  • Learn from experience.
  • Define roles, responsibilities and relationships.
  • Manage by stages: plan, monitor and control stage by stage.
  • Manage by exception: set tolerances and escalate only when they will be exceeded.
  • Focus on products: define and deliver products that meet quality criteria.
  • Tailor to suit the project.

Seven practices

Called themes in earlier editions, these are aspects to manage continually: business case, organising, plans, quality, risk, issues and progress.

Seven processes

ProcessPurpose
Starting up a projectCheck the project is viable and worthwhile
Directing a projectProject board makes key decisions
Initiating a projectEstablish solid foundations and plans
Controlling a stageDay-to-day management by the project manager
Managing product deliveryTeam managers deliver work packages
Managing a stage boundaryReview the stage and plan the next
Closing a projectConfirm acceptance and close

Key roles

  • Project board: executive, senior user and senior supplier; accountable for success.
  • Project manager: runs the project day to day within tolerances.
  • Team manager: leads delivery of specialist products.
  • Project assurance and project support.

Certifications

PeopleCert offers PRINCE2 Foundation and Practitioner certifications, and PRINCE2 Agile for combining PRINCE2 with Agile delivery. See PRINCE2 vs Agile.

Frequently asked questions

Is PRINCE2 better than PMP?

They are different: PRINCE2 is a methodology; PMP certifies knowledge of PMI's standard and broader practice. Choice depends on your region and industry.

What is management by exception?

The project board sets tolerances; the project manager handles everything within them and escalates only when a tolerance is forecast to be exceeded.
