Gantt Chart Software: Features, Top Tools and How to Choose

Gantt chart software turns a list of tasks into an interactive timeline, showing durations, dependencies, milestones and progress. Unlike a static chart, it updates dates automatically when tasks move.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Gantt software adjusts dates automatically when tasks and dependencies change.
  • Look for dependencies, critical path, baselines and workload views.
  • Wrike is our top pick because its Gantt links to resource and portfolio management.

Must-have Gantt features

  • Drag-and-drop task bars and durations.
  • All four dependency types, with leads and lags.
  • Automatic rescheduling when a predecessor moves.
  • Critical path highlighting.
  • Milestones and baselines to compare plan against actual.
  • Resource assignment and workload view.
  • Export and sharing for stakeholders.

Worked example

A team plans a product launch with five tasks: research (10 days), design (15 days), build (20 days), test (10 days) and launch (2 days), each finish-to-start. In Gantt software the end date is calculated automatically as 57 working days after the start. When research overruns by 3 days, every successor shifts and the launch moves to day 60. The team sees this immediately, and decides to overlap design and build by 3 days (fast-tracking) to recover the date.

How to choose Gantt chart software

  1. Decide how complex your schedules are: tens of tasks or thousands.
  2. Check dependency types, critical path and baseline support.
  3. Check whether you need resource and workload views.
  4. Consider who else needs to see the plan and how they will access it.
  5. Test with a real schedule during a free trial.
  6. Check current prices on vendor sites; see how to choose project management software.

Tips for better Gantt charts

  • Keep tasks at a level you can track weekly; do not schedule every hour.
  • Use dependencies instead of typing fixed dates.
  • Set a baseline before work starts.
  • Update progress at a regular time each week.
  • Show a summary view to executives and detail to the team.
Tip

If your Gantt chart is too big to read, roll tasks up into phases and share a project timeline instead.

Frequently asked questions

What is Gantt chart software?

Software that creates interactive Gantt charts, automatically adjusting dates as tasks and dependencies change.

What is the best Gantt chart software?

Wrike ranks first in our review for its full interactive Gantt linked to resources and portfolios.

Is there free Gantt chart software?

Some tools include Gantt or timeline views on free or entry plans, often with limits. Check vendor pricing pages for current terms.

Can I make a Gantt chart in Excel?

Yes, with a stacked bar chart or template, but it will not handle dependencies or rescheduling automatically.

Does Trello have Gantt charts?

Trello offers a timeline view on some plans but is better for simple boards; see our Trello review.

Does Asana have Gantt charts?

Asana offers timeline and Gantt views on paid plans; see our Asana review.

Are Gantt charts used in Agile?

Sometimes, for release-level planning; see Agile Gantt charts.

What is a baseline in Gantt software?

A saved copy of the original plan so you can compare actual progress against it.

Can Gantt software show the critical path?

Many tools, including Wrike, highlight the critical path automatically.

What is the difference between a Gantt chart and a timeline?

A Gantt chart shows tasks, durations and dependencies in detail; a timeline is often a simpler, high-level view.
