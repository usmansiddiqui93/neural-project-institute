Consulting Project Management: Phases, Roles and Best Practice

Consulting project management is the planning and control of advisory engagements, where a consulting firm helps a client diagnose a problem, recommend a course of action and often support implementation. The output is usually insight and change rather than a physical product, so the craft lies in framing the question well, structuring the analysis and keeping the client engaged throughout.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 28 August 2026
  • 5 min read

Key takeaways

  • Consulting project management starts by framing the client's question clearly.
  • Hypothesis-driven workplans keep short engagements focused.
  • Regular sponsor steering meetings prevent surprises and build adoption.
  • Success means recommendations the client acts on, delivered within the fee.

What makes consulting project management different

  • The problem is not fully known at the start: scope is framed as questions to answer, and the answers shape the later work.
  • Short, intense engagements: many projects run for weeks rather than months with small teams.
  • Client-facing every day: consultants often work on the client site with client staff, so stakeholder management is constant.
  • Credibility is the deliverable: recommendations only create value if the client accepts and acts on them.
  • Commercial pressure: engagements are sold at a fee and must be delivered within it.

Consulting firms are a type of professional services business and share its utilisation and margin measures.

Tip

Hold short, regular steering meetings with the client sponsor and share emerging findings early. A final presentation with no surprises is a sign of a well-run engagement.

Methods that fit consulting

  • Hypothesis-driven workplans: break the client question into an issue tree, form hypotheses and plan analyses to prove or disprove them. This keeps work focused.
  • Phased delivery with gates: most strategy and diagnostic work follows defined phases with a steering meeting at each gate.
  • Agile sprints: used for digital, product and transformation engagements where the team builds and tests with the client; see what is Agile project management.
  • Programme management: implementation support engagements often set up or run a client PMO; see project management office.
  • Change management: recommendations that change how people work need a structured adoption plan.

Essential documents and templates

  • Project proposal template to win and frame the work.
  • Project charter template or engagement letter to confirm scope and governance.
  • Problem statement template to agree the core question.
  • Work breakdown structure template or workplan by workstream and week.
  • Gap analysis template and root cause analysis template for diagnosis.
  • RAID log template for risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies.
  • Executive summary template for the final recommendation.
  • Lessons learned template for knowledge capture.

Common challenges and how to handle them

  • Moving question: confirm the key question in writing at kick-off; if it changes, re-plan and re-price openly.
  • Slow data access: list data requests in the first week with owners and dates, and escalate through the sponsor.
  • Boiling the ocean: use hypotheses to prioritise analysis and stop work that will not change the answer.
  • Weak sponsor engagement: schedule regular steering meetings and decisions they must make.
  • Recommendations not adopted: involve the people who will implement them early and include an implementation roadmap.

Frequently asked questions

What is consulting project management?

It is planning and running advisory engagements so the consulting team answers the client's question, delivers recommendations and stays within the agreed fee.

What are the phases of consulting project management?

Proposal, scoping and mobilisation, diagnosis, analysis and solution design, recommendation, implementation support and close.

What does an engagement manager do in consulting project management?

They run the engagement day to day: the workplan, the team, client meetings, quality of analysis and delivery of findings.

What is a hypothesis-driven approach in consulting project management?

Breaking the client question into an issue tree, forming likely answers early and planning only the analyses needed to test them.

How is consulting project management different from other project management?

The problem is not fully defined at the start, the output is insight and change, and the client is involved every day.

Which KPIs are used in consulting project management?

Utilisation, realisation, engagement margin, milestones met, client satisfaction, follow-on work and recommendations adopted.

Is Agile used in consulting project management?

Yes, especially in digital and transformation engagements, while strategy work usually follows phased delivery with steering gates.

What templates help with consulting project management?

Proposal, project charter, problem statement, WBS, gap analysis, root cause analysis, RAID log and executive summary templates.

How do you handle scope change in consulting project management?

Confirm the question in writing, show the impact of any change on time and fee, and agree it with the sponsor before starting.

What software is used for consulting project management?

A work management platform such as Wrike for plans and resourcing, plus PSA or finance tools and secure document sharing.

What skills does consulting project management require?

Structured problem solving, stakeholder management, clear writing and presenting, and disciplined planning and budget control.
