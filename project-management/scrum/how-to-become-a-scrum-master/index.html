How to Become a Scrum Master: Skills, Steps and Certification

The Scrum Master is accountable for the Scrum Team's effectiveness. It's a role that rewards people skills as much as process knowledge. Here's how to build both and step into the job.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 9 September 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Scrum Masters need facilitation, coaching and influence.
  • Gain hands-on experience before or alongside certification.
  • PSM I and CSM are common entry-level certifications.

What a Scrum Master does

Scrum Masters coach the team, the Product Owner and the organisation in Scrum, remove impediments, and make sure events are productive. See what a Scrum Master does for full responsibilities.

The most useful Scrum Master skills

Steps to become a Scrum Master

  1. Learn Scrum thoroughly: read the Scrum Guide several times.
  2. Get hands-on: join a Scrum Team in any role, or facilitate events for your current team.
  3. Practise facilitation: run retrospectives and workshops.
  4. Get certified: PSM I (Scrum.org) or CSM (Scrum Alliance) are common entry points; see Scrum certification.
  5. Find a mentor among experienced Scrum Masters or Agile coaches.
  6. Apply internally first: many Scrum Masters move into the role within their own organisation.
  7. Keep learning: coaching, facilitation, Kanban and Lean.

Interview tips

  • Share concrete stories of impediments you removed.
  • Explain how you would handle a team that skips retrospectives.
  • Show you understand servant leadership; see servant leadership in Scrum.
  • Ask about the organisation's support for Scrum.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need a technical background to be a Scrum Master?

No, though understanding the team's work helps. Facilitation and coaching skills matter more.

Which Scrum Master certification is best?

PSM I and CSM are the most common. PSM I is an online assessment; CSM requires a live course. Employers in your market may prefer one.

What does a Scrum Master do day to day?

Coaches the team in self-management, helps remove impediments, makes sure events happen and are productive, and supports the Product Owner and wider organisation in using Scrum effectively.

Can I become a Scrum Master with no experience?

It is possible but difficult. Most people first gain experience on a Scrum Team, facilitating events or leading improvement work, and pair that with a certification such as PSM I or CSM.

How long does it take to become a Scrum Master?

You can earn an entry-level certification in a few days of study or a two-day course, but becoming effective in the role usually takes months of hands-on practice.

Is the Scrum Master a project manager?

No. A Scrum Master does not assign tasks or own scope and schedule; they are accountable for the team's effectiveness. Some project managers do move into the role and adapt their style.

Is a Scrum Master certification worth it?

It is often useful for getting interviews, because many job adverts ask for PSM or CSM. Practical experience and coaching skills matter more once you are in the role.

What is the difference between the PSM I and CSM Scrum Master certifications?

PSM I from Scrum.org is an online assessment with optional training. CSM from Scrum Alliance requires attending a course with a certified trainer and then passing a test. See Scrum certification.

Can a Scrum Master work with more than one team?

Yes. The Scrum Guide does not limit this, and many Scrum Masters support two teams. Supporting too many can leave little time for coaching.

What career paths follow the Scrum Master role?

Common next steps include Agile coach, Release Train Engineer in SAFe, delivery manager, Product Owner or a wider leadership role in an Agile transformation.

What questions are asked in Scrum Master interviews?

Expect scenarios such as a Daily Scrum running long, a Product Owner who is never available or a team missing its Sprint Goal. Interviewers look for practical examples and a clear grasp of the Scrum Guide.
