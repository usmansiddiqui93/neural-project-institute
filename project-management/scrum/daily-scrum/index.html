Daily Scrum (Stand-Up): Purpose, Format and Tips

The Daily Scrum is a 15-minute event for the Developers to inspect progress toward the Sprint Goal and adapt the Sprint Backlog as needed. It is held at the same time and place every working day to reduce complexity.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • The Daily Scrum is 15 minutes for the Developers.
  • Its purpose is to inspect progress towards the Sprint Goal and adapt the plan.
  • Walking the board helps avoid status reporting.

Purpose

  • Inspect progress towards the Sprint Goal.
  • Adapt the plan for the next day of work.
  • Identify impediments early.
  • Improve communication and reduce the need for other meetings.

Who attends

The Daily Scrum is for the Developers. If the Product Owner or Scrum Master are actively working on items in the Sprint Backlog, they take part as Developers. Others may observe but should not interrupt.

Formats

The Scrum Guide does not prescribe a format. Common options:

  • Three questions: What did I do yesterday towards the goal? What will I do today? Do I see any impediments? Simple, but can drift into status reporting.
  • Walk the board: review items from right to left (closest to done first), asking what is needed to finish each.
  • Goal-focused: start with "Are we on track for the Sprint Goal?" and discuss only what affects it.

Tips

  • Stand up, or keep cameras on for remote teams, to keep it short.
  • Start on time even if someone is missing.
  • Take detailed discussions offline, right after the meeting, with only the people needed.
  • Update the board during the meeting.
  • Rotate who facilitates.

Warning signs

  • People report to the Scrum Master or a manager.
  • It regularly runs longer than 15 minutes.
  • Nobody mentions the Sprint Goal.
  • Impediments are raised but never resolved.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Daily Scrum the same as a stand-up?

Many teams call it a stand-up. The Daily Scrum is Scrum's specific version, focused on the Sprint Goal.

Can the Daily Scrum be skipped?

Not in Scrum. If it feels pointless, use the Retrospective to change its format rather than dropping it.
