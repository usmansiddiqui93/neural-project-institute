Scrum Ceremonies: The 5 Scrum Events Explained

Scrum's events, often called ceremonies or meetings, create a regular rhythm for planning, inspecting and adapting. Each has a clear purpose and a maximum duration, and together they replace most other meetings.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Scrum has five events: the Sprint plus four inside it.
  • Each event has a purpose and a maximum time-box.
  • Backlog refinement is ongoing, not a formal event.

The five events at a glance

EventPurposeWhoTime-box (1-month Sprint)
The SprintContainer for all other eventsScrum TeamOne month or less
Sprint PlanningPlan the Sprint's goal and workScrum TeamUp to 8 hours
Daily ScrumInspect progress and adapt the planDevelopers15 minutes
Sprint ReviewInspect the outcome and adapt the backlogScrum Team and stakeholdersUp to 4 hours
Sprint RetrospectiveImprove quality and effectivenessScrum TeamUp to 3 hours

For shorter Sprints, the events are usually shorter.

Why time-boxes matter

A time-box is a maximum duration. It keeps events focused, limits waste and creates a predictable rhythm. If an event regularly needs more time, that is a signal to investigate, for example poorly refined backlog items.

What about backlog refinement?

Product Backlog refinement is an ongoing activity, not a formal event. Teams typically spend some time each Sprint clarifying, splitting and estimating upcoming items, often in a regular session.

Making events effective

  • Hold them at the same time and place every Sprint.
  • Prepare inputs in advance, such as a refined backlog for planning.
  • Keep the focus on the event's purpose.
  • Respect time-boxes.
  • Use outcomes: every event should change something, such as a plan, the backlog or the way of working.

Frequently asked questions

Are Scrum ceremonies mandatory?

Yes. The Scrum Guide says each event is a formal opportunity to inspect and adapt, and skipping events loses those opportunities.

Is the Daily Scrum a status meeting?

No. It is a planning meeting for the Developers, focused on progress towards the Sprint Goal.
