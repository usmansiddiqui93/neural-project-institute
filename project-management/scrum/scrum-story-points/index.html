Story Points in Scrum: How to Estimate Product Backlog Items

The Scrum Guide doesn't require any particular estimation technique, but many Scrum teams use story points: relative units that capture the size, complexity and uncertainty of backlog items.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 15 September 2026
  • 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Story points measure relative size, complexity and uncertainty.
  • Developers estimate, usually with planning poker.
  • Points feed velocity and forecasting.

How story points work

  • Pick a small, well-understood item as a reference, for example a 2.
  • Estimate other items relative to it: twice as big, half as big.
  • Use a Fibonacci-like scale: 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20.
  • Split anything too large to finish within a Sprint.

Planning poker in Scrum

During refinement or Sprint Planning, Developers privately choose a card, reveal together, discuss differences and re-vote. The Product Owner explains items but does not estimate. See Agile estimation for detail.

From points to planning

The total points completed per Sprint is the team's velocity. Average velocity guides how much to select in Sprint Planning and supports release forecasts.

Alternatives

  • T-shirt sizes for rough roadmap sizing.
  • Counting items when items are similarly sized.
  • Probabilistic forecasting from throughput.

Frequently asked questions

Are story points required in Scrum?

No. Scrum only requires that the Developers size work in some way; story points are one popular option.

Who estimates story points?

The Developers who will do the work.

What are story points?

Relative units that estimate the size, complexity and uncertainty of a Product Backlog item compared with other items. They are not hours or days.

Why use the Fibonacci sequence for story points?

The growing gaps between numbers, such as 5, 8 and 13, reflect the greater uncertainty of larger items and stop teams arguing over false precision.

How many hours is a story point?

There is no fixed conversion. Story points are relative, and turning them into hours undermines their purpose.

How does planning poker work for story points?

An estimation technique in which each Developer chooses a card privately, all reveal at once, the highest and lowest explain their reasoning, and the team re-votes until estimates converge.

What is a good story point size for a Sprint?

Items should be small enough to finish within a Sprint, ideally in a few days. Many teams split anything estimated at 13 points or more.

Can story points be compared between teams?

No. Each team's scale is relative to its own reference items, so comparing points across teams is meaningless.

When should story points be estimated?

Usually during backlog refinement, so items are sized before Sprint Planning. Teams may re-estimate during planning if understanding has changed.

What is the difference between story points and T-shirt sizing?

T-shirt sizes (S, M, L, XL) are a coarser relative scale often used for early or roadmap-level sizing; story points give finer numbers used for Sprint planning and velocity.

Is #NoEstimates an alternative to story points?

Yes. #NoEstimates is a movement that questions detailed estimation and instead splits work into small, similar items and forecasts from throughput. See Kanban planning.
