CAPM Certification: Requirements, Exam and Is It Worth It?

The Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM) is PMI's entry-level certification. It shows you understand project management fundamentals and terminology, and it does not require project experience.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 3 October 2026
  • 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • The CAPM is PMI's entry-level credential with no experience requirement.
  • You need 23 contact hours of project management education.
  • It satisfies the PMP's education requirement later on.

Requirements

  • A secondary diploma or equivalent.
  • 23 contact hours of project management education completed before the exam.
  • No project management experience required.

Confirm current eligibility on PMI's website before applying.

What the exam covers

  • Project management fundamentals and core concepts.
  • Predictive (plan-based) methods.
  • Agile frameworks and methods.
  • Business analysis frameworks.

Study the PMBOK Guide, basic Agile and Scrum concepts, and common terms.

CAPM vs PMP

CAPMPMP
LevelEntryExperienced practitioner
ExperienceNone36 or 60 months leading projects
Education23 contact hours35 contact hours (or CAPM)
Renewal15 PDUs every 3 years60 PDUs every 3 years
Typical holderCoordinators, students, career changersProject managers

Holding a CAPM also satisfies the PMP's education requirement; see PMP certification.

Preparation plan

  1. Complete a 23-hour course from a reputable provider.
  2. Read the relevant PMI standard and an Agile primer.
  3. Make flashcards for key terms and formulas.
  4. Take at least two timed practice exams.
  5. Review every wrong answer and the concept behind it.
  6. Book the exam once you score consistently well in practice.
Tip

Exam fees differ for PMI members and non-members. Check PMI's current price list to see whether membership saves you money overall.

Who should take it

  • Students and recent graduates.
  • Project coordinators and team members.
  • Career changers moving into project work.
  • Anyone planning to take the PMP later.

Frequently asked questions

What is the CAPM?

PMI's entry-level certification for people who want to show knowledge of project management fundamentals.

What are the CAPM requirements?

A secondary diploma and 23 contact hours of project management education. No experience is needed.

How much does the CAPM cost?

PMI charges different fees for members and non-members and these change over time; check PMI's website for current figures.

Is the CAPM worth it?

It is useful for beginners who want a recognised credential and a foundation for the PMP, especially where employers value PMI certifications.

How do I maintain the CAPM?

Earn 15 PDUs every three years and complete PMI's renewal process.

Is the CAPM easier than the PMP?

Yes. It tests knowledge rather than experienced judgement and has no experience requirement.

Does the CAPM count towards the PMP?

Yes. A current CAPM meets the PMP's 35 contact hour education requirement.

Can I take the CAPM online?

PMI offers online proctored and test centre options; check current availability when booking.

How long should I study for the CAPM?

Many people prepare in four to eight weeks alongside work.

Should I take the CAPM or PRINCE2 Foundation?

CAPM suits PMI-focused markets; PRINCE2 Foundation is more common in the UK, Europe and the public sector.
