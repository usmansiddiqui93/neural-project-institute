What Is a Project Baseline? Scope, Schedule and Cost

A baseline is the approved version of a plan, frozen so that actual performance can be compared against it. Projects usually have three: scope, schedule and cost.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Baselines are approved plans used to measure performance.
  • Projects have scope, schedule and cost baselines.
  • Change them only through change control.

The three baselines

BaselineContents
Scope baselineScope statement, WBS and WBS dictionary
Schedule baselineApproved start and finish dates for activities and milestones
Cost baselineTime-phased budget, excluding management reserve

Together they form the performance measurement baseline, used for earned value management.

Setting a baseline

  1. Complete planning and get the plan approved.
  2. Save the baseline in your scheduling tool.
  3. Communicate it to the team and stakeholders.

Using the baseline

  • Compare actual progress with baseline dates.
  • Calculate schedule and cost variance; see schedule variance and cost variance.
  • Show baseline bars on Gantt charts.
  • Plot planned vs actual on an S-curve.

Re-baselining

Change the baseline only through formal change control, for example after a major approved scope change. Keep earlier baselines so the history remains visible.

Frequently asked questions

What is a baseline in project management?

The approved version of the scope, schedule or cost plan used to measure performance.

Why is a baseline important?

Without it you cannot measure variance or progress objectively.

When is the baseline set?

At the end of planning, once the plan is approved.

What is re-baselining?

Formally changing the baseline after an approved change.

Should you re-baseline often?

No. Frequent re-baselining hides performance problems.

What is the performance measurement baseline?

The integrated scope, schedule and cost baselines.

Is management reserve in the cost baseline?

No. It sits outside the cost baseline in the overall budget.

How is a baseline shown on a Gantt chart?

As a second, usually grey, bar beneath each task.

Do Agile projects have baselines?

Not in the classic sense; release plans and burnup charts play a similar role.

What tools support baselines?

Most scheduling tools such as Wrike, Smartsheet and Microsoft Planner premium plans.
