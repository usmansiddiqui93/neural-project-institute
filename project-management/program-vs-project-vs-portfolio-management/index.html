Project vs Program vs Portfolio Management: Differences

Projects, programmes and portfolios are three levels of managing change in an organisation. Each has a different purpose, and each is judged by different measures of success.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways

  • Projects deliver outputs; programmes deliver benefits; portfolios deliver strategy.
  • Each level has different success measures and leaders.
  • PPM decides which projects and programmes to fund.

Example

A hospital's portfolio includes all its change initiatives. Its digital health programme coordinates several related projects: a patient app, an electronic records upgrade and staff training. Each project has its own manager and deliverables; the programme makes sure together they deliver better patient care.

Project portfolio management (PPM)

PPM selects, prioritises and balances projects and programmes against strategy, capacity and risk. It decides what to start, stop or change. See Agile portfolio management for adaptive approaches.

Enterprise project management

Enterprise project management (EPM) applies consistent project, programme and portfolio practices across a whole organisation, often supported by a PMO and shared tools.

Frequently asked questions

What does a program manager do?

Coordinates related projects, manages dependencies between them and ensures their combined benefits are realised.

Is a portfolio the same as a programme?

No. A programme groups related projects for shared benefits; a portfolio groups all initiatives, related or not, to meet strategic goals.

What is the difference between project, programme and portfolio management?

Project management delivers a specific result; programme management coordinates related projects to realise shared benefits; portfolio management selects and balances all initiatives to meet strategic goals.

What is a programme in project management?

A group of related projects managed together to achieve benefits that none could deliver alone.

What is a project portfolio?

A collection of projects, programmes and other work managed together to meet strategic objectives.

What does a portfolio manager do?

Selects, prioritises and balances initiatives against strategy, capacity and risk, deciding what to start, stop or change.

Is it program or programme?

Both are correct. Programme is the usual British spelling for a set of related projects; program is used in American English and by PMI.

What is project portfolio management (PPM)?

The practice of selecting, prioritising and balancing projects and programmes against strategy, capacity and risk.

What certifications exist for programme and portfolio managers?

PMI offers PgMP for programme managers and PfMP for portfolio managers. See what is PMI.

What is enterprise project management?

Applying consistent project, programme and portfolio practices across a whole organisation, often supported by a PMO and shared tools.

How is a programme manager different from a project manager?

A project manager focuses on deliverables; a programme manager focuses on benefits across several projects. See project manager vs program manager.
