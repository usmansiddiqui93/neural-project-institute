## Fact-Checking Notes
- OUSD(R&E), *Integrated Master Plan and Integrated Master Schedule Preparation and Use Guide*, May 2023 (Change 1, 1 October 2024 per the DAF MPM listing): IMP = Events, Accomplishments, Criteria; IMS adds Tasks with dates, durations and logic; approved contractor IMP generally becomes part of the contract, IMS typically not contractually binding; IMP items use past participles, IMS tasks start with imperative verbs, verbs defined in an Event and Action Dictionary; normally two or more Accomplishments per Event and generally at least two Criteria per Accomplishment; entry and exit criteria; numbering examples A / A01 / A01a and tasks A01a01; IMP sections: introduction, IMP table, narrative, glossary; narratives contractually binding so keep concise; most IMP dates for information only; IMS good practice: avoid leads, prefer finish-to-start, avoid start-to-finish, hard constraints highly discouraged, tasks over 44 days considered long by DCMA, one critical path; schedule risk analysis needs sound logic.
- Earlier guide: DoD IMP/IMS Preparation and Use Guide, 21 October 2005 (AcqNotes).
- Humphreys & Associates: IMS data item DI-MGMT-81650 (March 2005) superseded by the IPMR and then the IPMDAR data item (DI-MGMT-81861).
- Wikipedia/AcqNotes: IMP top-down and event-based, IMS bottom-up and time-based; WBS per MIL-STD-881; "integrated master plan" also used for unrelated municipal public works plans.
- Tool facts from our reviews: Wrike Gantt (Team plan up) with critical path, float and baselines; Smartsheet Gantt with dependencies and critical path; Microsoft Planner Plan 3 baselines and critical path.
- The worked example is illustrative; durations and logic checked by hand.
