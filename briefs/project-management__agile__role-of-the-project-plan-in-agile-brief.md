## Fact-checking notes

- Agile Manifesto value "Responding to change over following a plan", with the clause that the items on the right still have value: well established.
- Scrum Guide 2020 (current): Product Goal is a target for the Scrum Team to plan against; Sprint Backlog is "a plan by and for the Developers"; Daily Scrum produces an actionable plan for the next day of work; burn-downs, burn-ups and cumulative flow are useful forecasting practices but do not replace empiricism. Paraphrase except the short quote.
- Planning onion: popularised by Mike Cohn ("Agile Estimating and Planning"). Different authors label the layers differently (strategy, portfolio, product, release, iteration, day; or vision, roadmap, release, iteration, daily). Attribute without dates or layer counts.
- PRINCE2 has project, stage and team plans: established on our PRINCE2 pages.
- All example numbers are illustrative.
