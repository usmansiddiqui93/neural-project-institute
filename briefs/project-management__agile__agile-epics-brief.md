## Fact-checking notes

- 2020 Scrum Guide does not mention epics (only Product Backlog items); consistent with the sibling page.
- SAFe: an epic is a container for a significant solution development initiative; business and enabler epics; epic hypothesis statement; MVP used to prove or disprove the hypothesis; Lean business case reviewed for a go/no-go decision; Epic Owner; epics managed in the Portfolio Kanban; SAFe states epics are not a synonym for projects (scaledagileframework.com epic article, checked 4 Nov 2026). Avoid naming exact Kanban state labels, which differ between SAFe versions.
- Epic length: no standard. Vendors suggest from "weeks or longer" (Atlassian) to "1 to 4 months" (Asana). Present as guidance: several Sprints, often within a quarter.
- Jira: epics sit at the top of the default hierarchy; levels above epics can be added (our Jira review). Wrike, monday.com, Asana, ClickUp: describe generally, consistent with our reviews.
- All example numbers are illustrative.
