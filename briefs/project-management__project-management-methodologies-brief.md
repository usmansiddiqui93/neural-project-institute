## Search Intent

Informational with a strong decision-making element. Searchers want a list of the main methodologies with plain explanations, and then help choosing one for a real project. Students and PMP/CAPM candidates also want the vocabulary straight (approach, framework, methodology, standard). The page should define the term in the first sentence, give a scannable comparison table, explain each method briefly with when to use it, then go beyond competitors with a step-by-step selection process, a scoring worked example, hybrid patterns and implementation steps.

## Target Audience

- New and accidental project managers deciding how to run a project
- Experienced PMs and PMO leads standardising or tailoring approaches
- Team leads moving from Waterfall to Agile or hybrid
- PMP, CAPM and PRINCE2 students
- Business owners and sponsors who want to understand the options
