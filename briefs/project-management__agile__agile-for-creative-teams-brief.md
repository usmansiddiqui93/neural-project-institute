## Facts to use (verified)

- GV design sprint: five-day process developed by Jake Knapp, who began running design sprints at Google in 2010 and brought them to GV in 2012; days: map and target, sketch, decide and storyboard, prototype, test with customers (gv.com/sprint). Book *Sprint* (2016).
- Scrum Guide 2020: Sprints one month or less; Scrum Team typically 10 or fewer; three accountabilities and five events.
- Kanban classes of service (expedite, fixed date, standard, intangible) come from the Kanban Method; present as common practice.
- Agile Marketing Manifesto current version finalised in 2021 (consistent with our Agile marketing page); do not restate in detail, link instead.
- Tool facts from our review pages: Wrike #1 (9.4/10), strong request forms and proofing for marketing and creative teams, takes time to set up; Asana (9.0) proofing and approvals on Advanced; ClickUp (8.9) forms, content calendars and proofing; Trello (7.8) simple Kanban boards for small teams.
