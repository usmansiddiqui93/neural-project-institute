## Search Intent

Mixed: commercial investigation plus informational. The reader wants to:

- Know what a Scrum tool must do (features mapped to Scrum artifacts and events)
- See the main options and how they differ, with honest strengths and trade-offs
- Find free or low-cost options
- Get a practical process for choosing, trialling and setting up a tool
- Avoid letting a tool distort Scrum (tool-driven process, over-configuration)

This page is a guide, not a ranking roundup. It should send buyers to our review pages for detail and keep every tool fact consistent with them.

## Target Audience

- Scrum Masters and Product Owners setting up or replacing a tool
- Team leads and delivery managers choosing software for one or several Scrum teams
- Mixed organisations where software and non-software teams share a tool
- Learners on Agile/Scrum courses (PMAI Sprintcraft)
