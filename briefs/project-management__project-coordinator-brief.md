## Fact-checking notes
- CAPM: secondary diploma plus 23 hours of project management education, no experience required; exam 150 questions in 180 minutes; fees widely reported as US$225 members / US$300 non-members (consistent with our CAPM guide, checked 18 October 2026). Use only the requirement facts on this page.
- PMP: 3/4/5 years of experience leading projects within the last 10 years by education level (2 with GAC degree) plus 35 contact hours or CAPM; from 1 December 2026 live training must come from a PMI Authorized Training Partner (site facts). Whether coordinator time counts depends on whether it involved leading and directing project work; do not promise it counts.
- Salary: competitor figures are either unsourced or from aggregators (Glassdoor). Do not publish figures; explain the factors and point to job adverts, national statistics and PMI's salary survey.
- Day-in-the-life schedule, status update and CV bullets are illustrative.
