## Search Intent

Informational and practical. The reader has an actual project to run (often their first Agile one) and wants a sequence of steps, what to produce at each step, how long things take, how to forecast and report, and an example of a project from start to finish. They do not need a definition of Agile or a change programme.

### Fact checks

- Scrum Guide 2020 (current): Sprint ≤ one month; Sprint Planning ≤ 8 hours, Sprint Review ≤ 4 hours, Retrospective ≤ 3 hours for a one-month Sprint, usually shorter for shorter Sprints; Daily Scrum 15 minutes; team of 10 or fewer; Product Goal, Sprint Goal, Definition of Done as commitments. Verified via Scrum.org.
- The Scrum Guide does not define a "Sprint 0"; present inception/Sprint 0 as a common practice, not part of Scrum.
- Discovery / alpha / beta / live phases: describe as the approach used in UK public-sector service manuals (verified on the Welsh Digital Public Services service manual).
- PMP exam (July 2026) covers Agile and hybrid approaches; keep to site-established facts.
- Worked examples are illustrative.

## Outline

**Intro:** To run an Agile project: set a vision, form a small cross-functional team with a Product Owner, build and order a backlog, plan an MVP and a forecast range, deliver in short sprints, review with stakeholders every sprint, release early and close or continue. Note the sister page for organisation-wide adoption.
