- What the role is and how it differs from a project manager and a programme manager

## Fact-Checking Notes

- PMP eligibility and exam (site-established): 3/4/5 years' experience within the last 10 years by education level (2 with a GAC-accredited degree) plus 35 contact hours or CAPM; exam updated July 2026 (People 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26%); from 1 December 2026 live training must come from a PMI Authorized Training Partner.
- PMI Earning Power salary survey, 14th edition (published November 2025, 14,628 respondents in 21 countries): PMP holders report median salaries 17% higher than non-holders. Matches our PMP page.
- Competitor experience claims (5–7 or 5–10 years) are not standards; present as "job adverts often ask for", no fixed rule.
- No salary figures for senior PMs specifically: they vary by country and sector, and published averages conflict (e.g. ProjectManager.com and The Digital Project Manager give different unsourced/own-survey US averages). Point readers to current adverts and surveys.
- PRINCE2 is run by PeopleCert (consistent with our PRINCE2 page).
- Worked examples and the weekly schedule must be labelled illustrative.
