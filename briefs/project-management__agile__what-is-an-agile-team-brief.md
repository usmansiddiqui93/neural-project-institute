## Facts to use (verified)

- 2020 Scrum Guide (current; consistent with our Scrum team roles page checked 26 October 2026): one Product Owner, one Scrum Master and Developers; typically 10 or fewer; cross-functional; self-managing; no sub-teams or hierarchies.
- SAFe: Agile teams of ten or fewer, include a Product Owner and Scrum Master/Team Coach (SAFe Agile Teams page).
- Agile Manifesto (2001) principles: self-organising teams; build projects around motivated individuals; regular reflection.
- Communication channels: n(n−1)/2.
- Tuckman's stages (forming, storming, norming, performing; adjourning added later).
- Google's Project Aristotle studied 180 teams and identified psychological safety as the most important of five team dynamics (psychological safety, dependability, structure and clarity, meaning, impact). Attribute to Google.
- Team Topologies (Skelton and Pais) names four team types: stream-aligned, enabling, complicated-subsystem, platform. Attribute.
- No invented statistics on Agile team performance.
