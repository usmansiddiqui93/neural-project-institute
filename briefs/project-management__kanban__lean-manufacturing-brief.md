## Search Intent

Informational, with a secondary "how to" layer. The bulk of searchers want a clear definition, the principles, the wastes and the main tools (students, new supervisors, people preparing for Lean Six Sigma Yellow/Green Belt study). A second group (operations and project managers) wants to know how to start and what results look like in practice. The page should define lean manufacturing in the first sentence, give compact reference tables (principles, wastes, tools) and then add what generic glossary pages lack: worked numbers and a step-by-step rollout.
