## Search Intent

Informational, often with a decision behind it. Searchers are usually managers or team members outside IT who have been told to "go Agile" (or are curious whether it would help), plus students meeting the "Agile is only for software" myth. They want a direct answer (no), evidence that it works elsewhere, an honest view of where it does not fit, and practical guidance on translating software terms and getting started.

## Target Audience

- Managers and team leads in marketing, HR, finance, operations, legal and education
- Project managers in non-IT organisations considering Agile or hybrid approaches
- Product and engineering leaders in hardware and manufacturing
- Public sector delivery teams
- Students and certification candidates (PMI-ACP, Scrum, PMP)
