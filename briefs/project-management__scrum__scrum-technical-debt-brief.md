## Fact-check notes

- Ward Cunningham introduced the debt metaphor in 1992 (OOPSLA experience report on the WyCash portfolio system).
- Martin Fowler's Technical Debt Quadrant was published 14 October 2009 (reckless/prudent × deliberate/inadvertent).
- The 2020 Scrum Guide does not use the term "technical debt" (Scrum.org blog confirms the Guide does not mention it).
- No authoritative standard for the share of Sprint capacity spent on debt; present percentages only as common practice, not a rule.
- Worked example and numbers are illustrative and labelled as such.
