## Facts to use (verified)

- PRINCE2 7 published 2023 by PeopleCert (which acquired AXELOS in 2021); 7 principles, 7 practices (formerly themes), 7 processes, plus people and project context (consistent with our PRINCE2 and PMBOK vs PRINCE2 pages).
- Tolerances can be set for seven performance targets: time, cost, quality, scope, benefits, risk and sustainability (Knowledge Train exception management page).
- PRINCE2 Agile first published 2015; PeopleCert released a new version (Version 2) in 2025, aligned with PRINCE2 7, adding performance targets, agile leadership, people/mindset content and guidance on Scrum, Kanban and compatibility with SAFe (PeopleCert PRINCE2 Agile page; ILX July 2025).
- Fix and flex: time and cost fixed; scope and quality criteria flex; benefits and risk can be either (Knowledge Train). Present as the core PRINCE2 Agile idea; do not claim exact Version 2 wording.
- Scrum Guide 2020 is current.
- Do not state exam prices or formats (they change and were not confirmed on PeopleCert's page).
