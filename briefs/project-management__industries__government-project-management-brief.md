## Search Intent

Informational, with a practical "how do I do this" layer. Searchers are mostly people who already manage or are about to manage projects in the public sector (central, state or local government, agencies) and want to know how it differs from private sector work, which methods, roles and documents apply, and how to deal with procurement, funding years and scrutiny. A second group is suppliers and consultants delivering to government, and a third is students and PMP/PRINCE2 candidates. The page must define the term early, then give a usable life cycle, a step-by-step process and worked examples, which the current SERP mostly lacks.

## Target Audience

- Senior responsible owners, sponsors and project board members
