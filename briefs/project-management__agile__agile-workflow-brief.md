## Search Intent

Informational and practical. The reader wants to understand what an Agile workflow is and then design or fix one for their team. Many ranking pages blur two meanings: (1) the lifecycle of an Agile product or project (ideation to retirement) and (2) the states a work item moves through on a board. The page should explain both briefly and focus on (2), which is what teams actually configure.

## Fact-checking notes

- Kanban Guide (Coleman and Vacanti), edition 2025.5 (May 2025): Definition of Workflow elements (units of value; when started and finished; one or more defined states; how WIP is controlled; explicit policies; service level expectation); three practices; four measures (WIP, throughput, work item age, cycle time). Checked on kanbanguides.org, 3 Nov 2026.
- Scrum Guide 2020: no prescribed board; the Sprint Backlog is owned by the Developers.
- Little's Law: average cycle time = average WIP / average throughput (holds for stable systems; use as a sense check, not a forecast).
- All example numbers illustrative. Tool facts must match our review pages.
