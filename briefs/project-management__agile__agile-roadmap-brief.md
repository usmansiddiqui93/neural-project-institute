Informational and practical ("how to"). Readers want a clear definition, to understand how an Agile roadmap differs from a traditional plan and from a release plan or backlog, to see real formats and an example, and to follow steps to build one. Many also want a template.

## Fact-checking notes

- Scrum Guide 2020: Product Goal in the Product Backlog; Product Owner accountable for the Product Backlog; no roadmap artifact.
- Now-Next-Later: Janna Bastow (ProdPad co-founder) says she invented it (ProdPad, Product Hunt AMA). Attribute as "popularised by".
- GO product roadmap: Roman Pichler; five elements date, name, goal, features, metrics (Scrum Alliance article drawing on Pichler).
- RICE: Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort (developed at Intercom); worked numbers illustrative.
- Tool facts from our review pages: Jira timeline on every plan, Plans (previously Advanced Roadmaps) on Premium and Enterprise; monday.com sells monday dev for sprints and roadmaps; Asana goals and portfolios on Advanced; Wrike #1 (9.4).
