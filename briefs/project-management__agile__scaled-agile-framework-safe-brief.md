## Fact checks (framework.scaledagile.com and scaledagile.com unless stated)
- Framework home (modified 25 Sep 2026): two operating models, Core SAFe and AI-Native SAFe; competencies grouped by discipline.
- Knowledge Base (modified 5 Oct 2026): five disciplines: Leadership and Culture; Team and Technical Agility; Product Development Flow; Large Solution Integration and Delivery; Lean Portfolio Management (27 competencies, four community contributions). Roles list incl. Scrum Master/Team Coach, RTE, STE, System/Solution/Enterprise Architect, Epic Owner, Business Owners, VMO. ART events: PI Planning, System Demo, Inspect and Adapt, ART Sync; team events: Iteration Planning, Iteration Review, Iteration Retrospective, Team Backlog Refinement, Team Sync.
- Posters page: Big Picture posters for Essential, Large Solution, Portfolio and Full SAFe (file names 6.0), plus AI-Native SAFe Big Picture.
- Core values (Lean-Agile mindset page): alignment, transparency, respect for people, relentless improvement.
- Ten Lean-Agile principles (exact names from the principles page, last update 4 May 2023).
- Planning Interval page (scaledagileframework.com/program-increment, 7 Feb 2023): PI 8–12 weeks, bias to shorter; common pattern four iterations + IP iteration; no fixed rule.
- PI Planning page (15 Mar 2026): every 8–12 weeks, typically two days, held in IP iteration; outputs committed PI objectives and ART planning board.
- What's new in SAFe 6.0: "Program" replaced by "ART"; APMO → VMO; SAFe Scrum and SAFe Team Kanban; Measure and Grow with outcomes, flow, competency; Scrum Master/Team Coach; core values as above; flow accelerators.
- AI-Native SAFe: blog 23 Jun 2026 (unveiled 16 Jun 2026; "does not replace Core SAFe"); PR Newswire 23 Jun 2026 (AI Value Architect role; smaller AI-augmented teams; shorter cycles); official release at SAFe Summit San Diego 15 Sep 2026 (secondary: Substack; Pretty Agile). Framework articles: AI-Native ART (14 Aug 2026), AI-Native Teams (3–7 people; capabilities Product, Builder, Domain Expert, AI; 14 Jul 2026), AI Value Architect (14 Jul 2026), PI Outcome Planning (one day, replaces two-day PI Planning in AI-Native SAFe; 28 Jul 2026), Sense and Respond (two hours, at least once per iteration; replaces Inspect and Adapt and absorbs System Demo; 28 Jul 2026).
- History: first released 2011, Dean Leffingwell; 6.0 March 2023 (Wikipedia; Leffingwell's 2007 book *Scaling Software Agility*).
- Criticism: Ken Schwaber "unSAFe at any speed" (2013), Thoughtworks Technology Radar, Jeff Gothelf "SAFe is not Agile" (2021) as cited by Wikipedia (originals not opened).
- Certifications: SA exam (study guide, 20 Oct 2026): 45 questions, 90 minutes, 80%, closed book, first two attempts within 60 days included, retake $50 unproctored / $495 proctored. Tiers: Foundational $195/yr (SA, SSM, POPM), Advanced $295/yr (RTE, LPM, SASM...), Expert $995/yr (SPC). Renewal annual. AI-Native certifications renew individually ($195–$1,500/yr).

## Worked example (illustrative)
Bank technology group, 420 people in four value streams: retail digital banking 120, business banking 105, payments 110, data platform 85 (all within 50–125; combining the two digital banking groups would give 225, above 125). Four ARTs, independent solutions → Portfolio SAFe, not Large Solution/Full. Teams of about ten → 8 to 12 teams per train. Cadence arithmetic: 10-week PI = 400 working hours per person; PI planning 16 h + Inspect and Adapt 4 h = 20 h = 5.0% (12-week PI: 4.2%); IP iteration is 2/10 = 20% of PI calendar but not overhead. 10-week PIs = 5.2 per year, 12-week = 4.33.
