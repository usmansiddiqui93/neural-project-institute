### Fact checks

- US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook, project management specialists: median annual pay $102,320 (May 2025); employment projected to grow 7% from 2025 to 2035; typical entry education bachelor's degree. This is a broad category that includes experienced project managers, not a figure for assistant roles. (Consistent with the site's construction PM page, which uses May 2025 BLS data.)
- PMI Earning Power salary survey 14th edition (November 2025, 21 countries): PMP holders reported median salaries 17% higher (site-established on the senior PM page).
- CAPM: secondary diploma plus 23 hours of project management education, no experience; 150 questions (135 scored) in 180 minutes (site-established on CAPM page).
- PMP eligibility (site-established): 3/4/5 years' experience within the last 10 years by education level (2 with a GAC degree) plus 35 contact hours or CAPM; from 1 December 2026 live training must come from a PMI Authorized Training Partner.
- APM Project Fundamentals Qualification (PFQ): UK entry-level qualification, no prior experience required (training provider listings, 2026).
- No invented salary figures; worked example and job description are illustrative.
