## Fact-check notes

- BIID Interior Design Project Book: RIBA Publishing with BIID, reflects RIBA Plan of Work 2020 (biid.org.uk). BIID and RIBA based their interior design contracts on the Plan of Work; RIBA/BIID Domestic Professional Services Contract 2020 referenced by BIID.
- UK domestic upholstered furniture: Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988 remain the framework; government announced plans to amend them (GOV.UK news, January 2025). Do not state a date for new rules.
- BS 7176: specification for resistance to ignition of upholstered furniture for non-domestic seating (FIRA).
- No fee percentages or salary figures: none verified; all worked examples labelled illustrative.
