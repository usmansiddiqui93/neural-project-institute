## Facts verified

- DORA (dora.dev history page): five metrics: change lead time, deployment frequency, failed deployment recovery time (throughput); change fail rate, deployment rework rate (instability). MTTR renamed in 2023; rework rate added 2024.
- Kanban Guide flow metrics (Vacanti and Coleman, via ProKanban.org): WIP, cycle time, work item age, throughput, with definitions.
- SAFe 6.0 flow metrics (framework.scaledagile.com, Accelerating Flow with SAFe): flow distribution, velocity, time, load, efficiency, predictability. SAFe metrics domains: outcomes, flow, competency (SAFe "Measure and Grow").
- SAFe predictability guideline of 80–100% based on business value of committed PI objectives (third-party SAFe guides; attribute as commonly cited guidance).
- EBM: four Key Value Areas, EBM Guide last updated 2024 (as on our Scrum metrics guide).
- Tool facts consistent with reviews: Jira has sprint, burndown/burnup, velocity, CFD and control charts; ClickUp sprint reporting includes burndown, burnup, velocity and CFD; Wrike #1 (9.4) with dashboards and portfolio reporting.
- Worked examples are illustrative and labelled as such.
