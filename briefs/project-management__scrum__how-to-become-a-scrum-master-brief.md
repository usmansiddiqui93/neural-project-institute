## Certification facts (must match scrum-certification-and-training and scrum-master-role)
PSM I: US$200/attempt, 80 Q, 60 min, 85%, lifetime, course optional, class includes free attempt. CSM: 16 h course, 50 Q, 60 min, 37 correct (74%), two attempts within 90 days, trainer-set fee ($250–$2,495 as of Oct 2023 per Scrum Alliance), renewal 2 years 20 SEUs + $100. A-CSM: CSM or PSM I/II + 12 months validated in past 5 years. CSP-SM: A-CSM + 24 months in past 5 years.
