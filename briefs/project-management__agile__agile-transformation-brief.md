## Search Intent

Informational, with planning intent. Two reader groups: (1) people who need to understand what an Agile transformation is and how it differs from adopting Agile in a few teams or from digital transformation; (2) leaders and change leads who have been asked to plan or rescue a transformation and want a roadmap, the dimensions that must change, how to fund and govern it, how to measure it and how to avoid the well-known failure patterns. They want a practical, honest guide, not vendor marketing or inflated statistics.

### Content gaps we can fill

- Competitors list stages but rarely give **exit criteria** for each phase or say what decision ends each phase.
- **Funding**: almost nobody explains project funding vs persistent product/value-stream funding with a worked comparison.
- **HR and performance management** changes are mentioned in passing, never tabulated.
- **Operating model options** (keep functional structure with Agile teams; product/value-stream teams; full squads-and-tribes) with trade-offs are missing.
- **Organisation-level scorecard** with leading and lagging indicators is missing; most list team metrics.
- **Real case** with verified facts is rare or unsourced; we use ING (McKinsey Quarterly interview, January 2017) with exact facts.
- **Early warning signs** of a stalling transformation, with responses.
- Honest timescale: "years, not months", without inventing a precise range.

### Fact checks

- ING (McKinsey Quarterly, "ING's agile transformation", 10 January 2017; interview with Peter Jacobs, CIO ING Netherlands, and Bart Schlatmann, former COO): strategy work from late 2014; new way of working introduced June 2015; about 350 squads of nine people in 13 tribes; initial scope about 3,500 headquarters staff, about 2,500 after selection; all headquarters staff asked to reapply for roles and nearly 40% ended up in a different position; ambition to release every two weeks instead of five to six big launches a year.
- Spotify model: 2012 paper by Henrik Kniberg and Anders Ivarsson (site-established on the squads, tribes, chapters and guilds page).
- SAFe: say it is updated regularly; do not name a version (site convention).
- Scrum Guide 2020 is current; PMBOK 8th edition (November 2025) only if referenced.
- No adoption or success-rate statistics: the figures on competitor pages (for example vendor surveys and percentage uplifts) could not be traced to primary sources with clear methods, so none are used.

## Outline

**Intro:** Agile transformation = organisation-wide change in how work is prioritised, funded, organised, governed and led so the organisation can deliver in small increments and adapt. Distinguish from implementing Agile in teams (link). Summarise: start with business outcomes, change the system, transform in waves over years.
