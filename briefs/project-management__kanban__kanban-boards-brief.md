## Facts to keep consistent / verified

- Kanban Guide edition 2025.5 (1 May 2025), John Coleman and Daniel Vacanti: board = visualisation of the Definition of Workflow; minimum elements; four flow measures; three practices (fetched).
- Origins: Toyota Production System under Taiichi Ohno; Anderson at Microsoft and Corbis in the 2000s (as on our what-is-Kanban guide).
- Little's Law: average cycle time = average WIP ÷ average throughput (as on our WIP limits guide).
- Tools (from our reviews): Wrike board view on every plan including Free, swimlanes via custom fields; ClickUp WIP limits per column, Board view on Free Forever; Jira WIP limits and swimlanes by assignee, epic or priority, Free up to 10 users; Trello no native WIP limits or swimlanes on the base board; Asana Board view, swimlanes since July 2025 on paid plans; monday.com Kanban view, Free plan up to 2 seats; Microsoft Planner Board view with buckets.
