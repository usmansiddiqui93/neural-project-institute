## Fact-Checking Notes
- Microsoft Support, "Add lead or lag time to a task": lead entered as a negative number or negative percentage (e.g. -50%), lag as a positive number or percentage (e.g. 2d), in the Lag column of the Predecessors tab.
- GAO Schedule Assessment Guide (gaoinnovations.gov, "Using Lags", "Using Leads"): lags must be justified and documented; should not represent effort, procurement/external work, mandated dates (use a constraint) or risk buffers; leads should generally be avoided, can create logic problems, replace with SS links or split activities.
- DCMA 14-point assessment (as described in Deltek Acumen documentation): target of zero leads; lags on no more than 5% of relationships.
- Wrike: our review page states its Gantt charts (Team plan up) support all four dependency types with lead and lag time, automatic rescheduling, critical path and baselines.
- PMI glossary wording paraphrased (lag: delay to the successor; lead: successor advanced). Do not quote PMBOK 8th edition glossary (not verified).
- All schedule examples are illustrative.
