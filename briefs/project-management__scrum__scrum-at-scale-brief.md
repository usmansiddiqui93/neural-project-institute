## Fact-Checking Notes

- Official guide: The Scrum@Scale Guide, version 2.1, February 2022, Jeff Sutherland and Scrum Inc., CC BY-SA 4.0 (checked 3 November 2026 via scrumatscale.com and the guide PDF).
- Guide figures: average optimal team size 4.6 (citing Hackman), therefore 4–5 teams per SoS; Scaled Daily Scrum 15 minutes or less with a representative of each team; Executive MetaScrum at least once per Sprint, facilitated by the CPO.
- Guide does not set EAT meeting frequency: do not invent one.
- Certification name varies by provider (Registered Scrum@Scale Practitioner, RS@SP); tell readers to check the official site.
- Nexus: 3–9 Scrum Teams (Nexus Guide); LeSS: up to about eight teams, LeSS Huge beyond. Keep consistent with our LeSS and SAFe pages.
- Worked example must be labelled illustrative.
