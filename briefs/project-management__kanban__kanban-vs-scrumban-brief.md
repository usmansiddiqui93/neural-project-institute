## Facts verified

- Corey Ladas, *Scrumban: Essays on Kanban Systems for Lean Software Development* (Modus Cooperandi Press, 2009, ISBN 9780578002149). He framed Scrumban as a way for Scrum teams to evolve towards Kanban. No official Scrumban guide exists (some vendors publish their own guides).
- Kanban Guide (Edition 2025.5): three practices, four flow measures; no roles or cadences prescribed. Kanban Method: seven cadences, optional roles (Service Request Manager, Service Delivery Manager). Consistent with site pages.
- 2020 Scrum Guide: Sprints of one month or less; three accountabilities; five events.
- Bucket-size planning, feature freeze and triage appear in many Scrumban descriptions (e.g. TechRepublic mentions bucket-size planning) but are not part of any formal standard; present them as common optional practices.
- Tools (from our reviews): Wrike #1 (9.4), board view on all plans with custom statuses; Jira #6, Scrum and Kanban boards with WIP limits and CFD; ClickUp #4, Sprints plus per-column WIP limits.
