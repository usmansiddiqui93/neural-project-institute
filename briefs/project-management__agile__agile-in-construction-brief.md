## H1: Agile in Construction: Where It Works and How to Use It

Intro: answer directly in the first sentence (Agile in construction means applying short planning cycles, frequent feedback, visual management and continuous improvement to the parts of a building project that benefit from them, mostly design, preconstruction and site coordination, while the overall build stays predictive).
