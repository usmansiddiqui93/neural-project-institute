## Search Intent

Informational with a strong practical and commercial element. Searchers are owners, operations managers and team leads in small companies who want a simple way to keep projects on track without corporate process, and many are also deciding which tool to use. They want a short, usable process, examples they can copy, help prioritising and cash planning, and a fair view of tools and prices. The page must answer the query in the first sentence, give a step-by-step process early and back it with worked examples.

## Target Audience

- Small business owners and managing directors who run projects themselves
- Office, operations and general managers who coordinate projects alongside day-to-day work
- Trades, studios, agencies, consultancies, retailers and hospitality businesses running client or internal projects
- Start-up founders and small teams choosing their first project management tool
- Freelancers who coordinate other freelancers on client projects
