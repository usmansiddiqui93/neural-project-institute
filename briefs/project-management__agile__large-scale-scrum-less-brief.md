## Outline

**Intro (lead):** LeSS is a framework for applying Scrum to many teams on one product with one Product Owner, one Product Backlog and one Sprint; created by Larman and Vodde; this guide covers rules, principles, roles, events, LeSS Huge, an example and adoption.

## Fact-Checking Notes (verified 4 November 2026)

- less.works Rules page is titled "LeSS Rules (November 2024)". Basic LeSS: 2–8 teams; LeSS Huge: 8+ teams; Requirement Areas 4–8 teams each, one Area PO each; Scrum Master 1–3 teams, dedicated full-time; managers optional; one PO and one Product Backlog; one DoD, teams may strengthen not weaken; one product-level Sprint; SP1 common, SP2 usually per team; own Sprint Backlog and Daily Scrum per team; PBR preferably multi-team; one product-level Sprint Review; team retros then Overall Retrospective (PO, Scrum Masters, team representatives, managers if any); LeSS Huge adoption incremental, can take months or years.
- less.works Principles: 10 principles (LeSS is Scrum; empirical process control; transparency; more with less; whole-product focus; customer-centric; continuous improvement towards perfection; systems thinking; lean thinking; queuing theory).
- less.works Introduction: Larman and Vodde began working together on scaling Scrum in 2005; books 2008 (Scaling Lean & Agile Development), 2010 (Practices for Scaling…), Large-Scale Scrum: More with LeSS (third book; published 2016, Addison-Wesley — widely cited, not restated on the fetched page, so the page omits the year); eight teams described as an empirical limit; Area POs form a Product Owner Team with the PO.
- less.works Overall Retrospective: PO, Scrum Masters, rotating team representatives; max 45 minutes per week of Sprint. Sprint Planning One has no fixed timebox on the framework page, so do not state one.
- Certifications (less.works courses, Scrum Alliance page): Certified LeSS Basics (1 day), Certified LeSS Practitioner (3 days in person; online provisional version), Certified LeSS for Executives; issued by The LeSS Company B.V.; Scrum Alliance lists them and they count towards SEUs. No exam is described.
- Nexus Guide (January 2021): approximately three to nine Scrum Teams; Nexus Integration Team (PO, a Scrum Master, NIT members).
- Keep consistent with our SAFe page (ART typically 50–125 people) and Scrum@Scale rewrite (groups of 4–5 teams, EAT, MetaScrum).
- Worked examples must be labelled illustrative; do not invent case-study results.
