## Search Intent

Informational with a strong practical "how to" element. The reader wants:

- The formula and a clear definition
- A step-by-step method they can apply to a real business case
- A worked example with real-looking numbers (costs, benefits, years)
- How to interpret the result (good, bad, negative ROI)
- How ROI relates to NPV, IRR and payback, and when to use each
- How to measure actual ROI after delivery

### Content gaps we can fill

- No competitor shows a **multi-year example** with a year-by-year cash flow table, then derives ROI, annualised ROI, payback, NPV, discounted ROI and IRR from the same numbers.
- Few explain that **the denominator convention changes the answer** (total costs vs initial investment) and that you must state it.
- None shows **annualised ROI** properly or a **project comparison** where simple ROI, annualised ROI and NPV rank projects differently.
- Little on **sensitivity and risk-adjusted (expected-value) ROI**.
- Little on **hard vs soft benefits** with a worked effect on the result.
- No **Excel formulas** section.
- Weak coverage of **measuring actual ROI** via benefits realisation.
