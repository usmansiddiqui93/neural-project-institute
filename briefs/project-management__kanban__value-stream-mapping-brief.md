Fact checks:
- *Learning to See* (Mike Rother and John Shook, Lean Enterprise Institute) is commonly dated 1998 (first release; widely cited 1999 printing), Shingo Research award 1999 (LEI store and lexicon).
- Karen Martin and Mike Osterling, *Value Stream Mapping: How to Visualize Work and Align Leadership for Organizational Transformation* (McGraw-Hill, 2013). Their worked examples use process time (PT), lead time (LT), %C&A, rolled %C&A (product of step values) and activity ratio (total PT ÷ total LT).
- Terminology clash: in manufacturing VSM, "C/T" (cycle time) is the time an operator takes to complete their work elements; in Kanban, cycle time is the elapsed time an item takes from start to finish. Must explain to stay consistent with our cycle time and flow efficiency guides.

## H2: What is value stream mapping?
- 40–60 word definition paragraph.
- What a value stream is (all steps, value-adding or not, for one product, service or request type).
- Three things that make a VSM different: whole flow from customer to customer, information flow as well as work flow, a timeline of process time vs waiting.
- Current state vs future state (and an implementation plan).
