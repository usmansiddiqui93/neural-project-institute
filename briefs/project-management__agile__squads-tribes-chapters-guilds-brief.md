2. **Kniberg and Ivarsson, "Scaling Agile @ Spotify with Tribes, Squads, Chapters & Guilds" (October 2012)**: squad like a Scrum team, "mini-startup", product owner and agile coach, no formal squad leader; tribes kept under about 100 (Dunbar); tribe lead; chapter lead is line manager and also works in a squad; guilds voluntary with a guild coordinator; matrix weighted towards delivery (vertical = what, horizontal = how), "professor and entrepreneur" analogy; system owners (often pairs) and a chief architect whose advice is not binding; dependency surveys; scrum of scrums only on demand; caveats: journey in progress, introduced gradually over about a year, tech grew from about 30 to 250 people in three years.
3. **Mersino (Medium)**: argues there is no Spotify model to adopt; cites Kniberg's later statements that it was never meant as a framework and an Ivarsson remark (in Hesselberg's Unlocking Agility) to the same effect.
