## Search Intent

Informational, with a strong "how to calculate" element. Most searchers want the formula and a worked example; a significant minority (PMP candidates, project controls staff, PMO analysts) want interpretation: what a negative or positive value means, how SV relates to SPI and cost variance, its limitations and what to do about it. The page should answer the formula in the first sentence, then go deeper than competitors on interpretation, limitations and action.
