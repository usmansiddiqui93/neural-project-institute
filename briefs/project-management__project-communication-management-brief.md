## Fact-Checking Notes
- PMI, Pulse of the Profession in-depth report "The High Cost of Low Performance: The Essential Role of Communications" (May 2013): US$135 million at risk per US$1 billion spent; 56% (US$75 million) of that at risk due to ineffective communications; one in five projects unsuccessful due to ineffective communications; 55% of project managers agree effective communication to all stakeholders is the most critical success factor. Present as 2013 data.
- PMBOK 6th edition: Project Communications Management knowledge area with three processes (Plan Communications Management, Manage Communications, Monitor Communications).
- PMBOK 8th edition (November 2025; 6 principles, 7 performance domains): published summaries (Ricardo Vargas podcast; Project Management Compass "Inside PMBOK 8: Stakeholders Performance Domain") place communications in the Stakeholders performance domain, which contains Identify Stakeholders, Plan Stakeholder Engagement, Plan Communications Management, Manage Stakeholder Engagement, Manage Communications, Monitor Stakeholder Engagement, Monitor Communications. Attribute to published summaries.
- "90% of a project manager's time is spent communicating" is widely repeated but could not be traced to a primary study; do not state it as fact.
- Tool descriptions consistent with our review pages (Wrike #1 9.4; monday.com 9.1; Asana 9.0; Microsoft Planner 8.1).
- All project examples are illustrative.
