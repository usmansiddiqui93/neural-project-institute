## Worked example (illustrative)
WMS replacement: go-live end of week 26; freeze from start of week 29; time tolerance +2 weeks; budget £240,000, cost tolerance +5% (£12,000); 4 developers × 5 days × 0.8 = 16 person-days/week; run rate £5,500/week; change of 48 person-days; Could-haves 30 + 18 person-days. Options: A extend 3 weeks (£16,500, breaches freeze); B contractor 60 days × £550 = £33,000; C defer 48 person-days; D +2 weeks (£11,000) + defer 18. All figures verified in Python.
