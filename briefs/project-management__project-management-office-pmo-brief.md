Informational, with a practical "how do I build or improve one" strand. Readers want a clear definition, the main types, what a PMO actually does day to day, the roles in it, and how to set one up or justify it. A secondary group is career-focused (PMO analyst, PMO manager, certification).

## Facts to verify / keep consistent
- PMBOK Guide 8th edition, November 2025, Appendix X2 "Project Management Offices" (verified via O'Reilly table of contents preview: sections on PMO type, model and structure, and PMO maturity models).
- PMI-PMOCP: PMI Project Management Office Certified Professional; secondary degree + 3 years project-related experience in last 8 years or PMP; 10 hours PMO education; 120 questions, 165 minutes (pmi.org). Builds on PMO-CP acquired from PMO Global Alliance in 2023 (PMI ATP page).
- P3O: Portfolio, Programme and Project Offices guidance, trademark of the PeopleCert group; describes portfolio, programme and project offices and a centre of excellence.
- PM Solutions 2022 State of the PMO research, as cited by CIO.com: nearly three-quarters of midsize and large companies report having a PMO.
- Tool facts from our review pages only.
