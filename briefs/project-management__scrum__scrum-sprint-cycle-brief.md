## Search Intent

Informational with a practical "how to run it" slant. The reader wants to know what happens in a Scrum Sprint from start to finish, in what order, how long each part takes, which rules apply and how to set up a cycle for their own team. Many are new Scrum Masters, team leads or project managers setting up a cadence.

## Target Audience

- New Scrum Masters and Product Owners setting up Sprints
- Team leads and project managers moving a team to Scrum
- Developers and stakeholders who want to understand the rhythm
- Certification learners (PSM, CSM, PMAI Sprintcraft)
