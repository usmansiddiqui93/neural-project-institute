## Search Intent

Mainly informational, with a strong "how to" element. Two groups search this term:

1. Students and PMP/CAPM candidates who want the formal process view (the PMBOK knowledge area, its six processes, inputs and outputs, and how the name changed to schedule management).
2. Working project managers and team leads who want a practical method for planning a realistic schedule, keeping it on track and managing their own time.

The page must serve both: a clear definition and the formal processes first, then a practical step-by-step method with worked numbers. Most ranking pages are tool vendors' blogs that blur project scheduling with personal productivity and timesheets; a structured, numerical guide is the differentiator.
