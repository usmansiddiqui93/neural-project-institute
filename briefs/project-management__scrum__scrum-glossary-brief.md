## Search Intent

Informational / reference. The reader wants a fast, trustworthy definition of a Scrum term, usually while onboarding, sitting in a Scrum event or revising for a certification such as PSM I or CSM. Secondary intent: know which terms are official Scrum Guide language and which are practices or retired terms, and see how the terms fit together in a real Sprint.

## Target Audience

- New members of Scrum Teams (developers, testers, designers, analysts, marketers)
- Project managers moving to Scrum and stakeholders who attend Sprint Reviews
- Certification learners (PSM, PSPO, CSM, CSPO, PMAI Sprintcraft)
- Scrum Masters and coaches who want a link to share with their teams
