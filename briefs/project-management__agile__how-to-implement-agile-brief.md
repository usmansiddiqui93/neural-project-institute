## Search Intent

Informational with strong practical ("how to") intent. The reader has decided, or is close to deciding, that their team, department or organisation should work in an Agile way and wants a concrete plan: where to start, who needs to be involved, what to change first, how to choose a pilot, which framework to start with, how to tell whether it is working and how to spread it. They want steps, checklists and an example they can adapt, not a definition of Agile.

## Target Audience

- Heads of department, delivery and engineering managers asked to "introduce Agile"
- PMO leads and portfolio managers
- Project managers who will become Scrum Masters, Product Owners or Agile project managers
- Agile coaches and change leads building a rollout plan
- Leaders outside software (marketing, operations, HR, public sector) adopting Agile
- PMAI Sprintcraft learners

## Current Page (what to keep)

Live page: about 1,000 words, seven steps (why, leadership, pilot, framework and training, basics, run and adapt, scale), pitfalls, 11 FAQs. Everything is accurate and the sequence is sound. Keep: start with a clear reason, leadership support, willing pilot volunteers, empowered Product Owner, avoid the highest-pressure project first, one framework as written, train teams and managers together, run five or six iterations before judging, scale team by team, change HR/budgeting/governance, scaling framework only when needed. Expand heavily: readiness check, pilot scoring matrix, framework comparison, role mapping, training plan, governance changes, metrics, 90-day worked example, people side of change, tools.
