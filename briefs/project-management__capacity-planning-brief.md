## Search Intent

Informational with a strong "how to" element. Searchers want a clear definition, then a method they can use: how to calculate capacity, how to estimate demand, how to compare the two and what to do about a gap. A second group (PMO analysts, resource managers, heads of delivery) want portfolio-level guidance: strategies, cadence, ownership and tools. Most top-ranking pages are vendor blogs with lists of benefits and best practices but no calculations, so the page should win on worked numbers.
