## Fact-Checking Notes
- IRS (irs.gov, "Determining if an employer is an applicable large employer"): FTE employees = non-full-time employees' hours for the month, max 120 per employee, divided by 120; 50+ full-time plus FTE employees = ALE. Full-time = 30 hours a week on average or 130 hours a month.
- nidirect (UK government): no specific number of hours makes someone full time; usually 35 hours or more.
- All costs and team figures in the rewrite are illustrative and must be labelled as such.
