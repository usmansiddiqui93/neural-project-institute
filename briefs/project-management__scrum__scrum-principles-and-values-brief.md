## Fact-Checking Notes

- 2020 Scrum Guide (Schwaber and Sutherland, November 2020), checked via scrumguides.org PDF: "Scrum is founded on empiricism and lean thinking"; "Transparency enables inspection. Inspection without transparency is misleading and wasteful"; "Inspection enables adaptation. Inspection without adaptation is considered pointless"; "Adaptation becomes more difficult when the people involved are not empowered or self-managing"; five values Commitment, Focus, Openness, Respect, Courage; Scrum Team typically 10 or fewer; self-managing; each artifact contains a commitment (Product Goal, Sprint Goal, Definition of Done).
- Values wording per Guide (via PSM study guide quoting the Guide): team commits to achieving its goals and supporting each other; focus on the work of the Sprint; open about the work and challenges; respect each other as capable, independent people; courage to do the right thing and work on tough problems.
- Values added to the Scrum Guide: update announced 6 July 2016 (DevOps.com press coverage). Retained in 2020.
- Earlier origin of the values (Schwaber and Beedle, 2001 book) is widely repeated but only weakly sourced: mention with "generally traced to" or omit.
- SBOK six principles (SCRUMstudy): empirical process control, self-organisation, collaboration, value-based prioritisation, time-boxing, iterative development; not part of the Scrum Guide.
- XP values (communication, simplicity, feedback, courage, respect) used only as a brief comparison.
- No statistics used. Worked example labelled illustrative.
