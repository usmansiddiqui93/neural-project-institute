## Fact Check (primary sources, checked 4 November 2026)

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| PSM I: $200 per attempt, 80 questions, 60 minutes, 85%, MC/MA/TF, English and Japanese (Simplified Chinese via scrum.org.cn), no course required, lifetime, passwords single-use and do not expire | scrum.org PSM I page |
| PSM class: two days, includes one PSM I attempt; second free attempt if taken within 14 days and score below 85%; 40% discount on PSM II for PSM class students after passing PSM I | scrum.org PSM training page |
| PSM II: $250, 30 questions (partial credit), 90 minutes, 85%, lifetime | scrum.org |
| PSM III: $500, 24 essay questions, 150 minutes, expert-graded pass/did not pass, lifetime | scrum.org |
| PSM-AI Essentials: $200, 40 questions, 60 minutes, 85% | scrum.org |
| PSPO I: $200, 80/60/85%; PSPO II: $250, 40 questions, 60 minutes, 85%; PSPO class two days with same free/second attempt rule | scrum.org |
| PSD $200 80q 60m; PSK I $200 45q 60m; PAL I $200 36q 60m; SPS $250 40q 60m; all 85%, lifetime | scrum.org |
| Certified counts (updated 1 Nov 2026): PSM I 778,506; PSPO I 279,802; total over 1,231,400 | scrum.org count page |
| CSM: 16 hours of training, most classes 2–3 days; 50 questions, 60 minutes, 37/50 (74%); two attempts within 90 days included; $25 per attempt after; public course prices $250–$2,495 (as of October 2023); renewal every two years | scrumalliance.org CSM page and support centre |
| CSPO: 16-hour class, no test | Scrum Alliance support |
| Renewal: Foundational (CSM, CSPO, CSD...) 20 SEUs + $100; Advanced (A-CSM, A-CSPO...) 30 SEUs + $175; Professional (CSP-SM, CSP-PO...) 40 SEUs + $250; two-year term; highest cert renewed at full rate, others at half SEUs and no fee; earning a new SA cert renews others | scrumalliance.org renewing certifications page |
| A-CSM: CSM (or Scrum.org PSM I/PSM II), 16-hour course, 12 months SM experience in past five years. CSP-SM: A-CSM, course, 24 months SM experience in past five years | Scrum Alliance support |
| AI-Empowered SAFe Scrum Master exam: 45 questions, 90 minutes, 80%; course not required; proctored exam $495 + tax (two attempts in 60 days); two attempts included with course within 60 days; unproctored retake $50; renewal annual, SAFe Foundational tier $195/year + tax; 24 CEUs per two-year cycle | scaledagile.com / support.scaledagile.com |
| PMI-ACP: 120 questions, 180 minutes; $435 member / $495 non-member; 30 PDUs every three years; Nov 2024 exam update FAQ: 28 contact hours of agile training, 2 years of agile experience in last 5 (reductions for third-party agile cert, PMP, GAC) | pmi.org page, handbook, 2024 update FAQ (pmi.org page still shows 21 hours: flag) |
| ICAgile: accredited class, no central exam; ICP knowledge-based, ICE competency-based | icagile.com |
