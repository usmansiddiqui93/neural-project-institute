## Search Intent

Informational, with strong study intent. Two groups search this term: practitioners who need to buy goods or services for a project and want a step-by-step process, documents and contract advice; and PMP/CAPM candidates who need the PMBOK processes, contract types and formulas (make-or-buy, incentive fees, point of total assumption). The page must define the term in the first sentence, give the process in a scannable form, then go deeper than competitors with worked numbers.
