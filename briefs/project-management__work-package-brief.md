## Fact-check Notes
- PMI defines a work package as the lowest level of the WBS for which cost and duration are estimated and managed (paraphrase; consistent with the old page).
- 8/80 and reporting-period rules are heuristics, not standards (consistent with our WBS guide).
- PRINCE2: project manager authorises work packages (Controlling a stage); team manager accepts and delivers them (Managing product delivery). Contents per prince2.wiki: work description, product descriptions, techniques, tolerances, date of agreement, reporting arrangements, quality responsibilities.
- No statistics are used.
