## Search Intent

Mainly informational with a practical "how to write one" element. Readers want the meaning, what goes into an SLA, examples of real-looking targets, the difference between SLA/SLO/SLI/OLA, and how to calculate availability and credits. Our angle is the project manager's view: SLAs as procurement terms for services a project buys, and as acceptance criteria for services a project hands over to operations.
