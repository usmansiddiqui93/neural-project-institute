## Search Intent

Informational with a strong "how to" layer. The SERP mixes two meanings:

1. The project-management meaning (culture, structure, leadership, workspace and tools that let Agile teams work well), which is our focus.
2. The workplace-design meaning ("agile working": flexible desks, activity-based offices), served mainly by office-booking and furniture vendors.

The reader wants a definition, the components, a way to assess their own environment and a practical plan to improve it, including for remote and hybrid teams.

## Target Audience

- Team leads and managers introducing Agile ways of working
- Scrum Masters, Agile coaches and Agile project managers
- PMO and transformation leads
- Non-software teams (marketing, operations, HR) adopting Agile
- Students preparing for Agile certifications

## Recommended Length

3,600–4,000 words (sections + FAQ), with tables, a self-assessment, a step-by-step process and an illustrative worked example.
