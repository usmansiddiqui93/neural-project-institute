## Search Intent

Informational and career research. Readers want to know what the role is, what the job involves day to day, how it differs from similar titles, what skills, certifications and pay to expect, and how to move into it. A secondary group are hiring managers writing a job description. The page should answer "what is it" quickly, then give practical, example-led depth on the work itself, which most ranking pages treat superficially.

## Target Audience

- Software developers, engineers, QA, data and infrastructure specialists considering a move into project management
- Project managers moving into technical domains
- Junior PMs and coordinators planning their career path
- Hiring managers defining the role
