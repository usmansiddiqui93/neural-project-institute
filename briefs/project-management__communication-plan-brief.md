## Search Intent

Mostly "how to" with a template-seeking element. Searchers want a definition, a list of what goes into the plan, a step-by-step process and, above all, a filled-in example they can copy. Many arrive needing the plan for a real project this week, so practical examples, a column-by-column template and rules for escalation matter more than theory.

This page is the practical "write the document" guide. It must stay distinct from `/project-management/project-communication-management/` (rewritten 4 November 2026), which covers the three PMI processes, push/pull/interactive methods, the channels formula, the sender-receiver model, an ERP worked example, measuring effectiveness and barriers. Here we only summarise those ideas in a line and link to that guide.
