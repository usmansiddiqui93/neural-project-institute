## Facts to keep consistent / verified

- PMBOK Guide 8th edition published November 2025: 6 principles, 7 performance domains (site fact; principle titles per O'Reilly TOC, recorded in the PMBOK brief).
- 7th edition (2021): 12 principles with official titles (verified via Edworking list; titles well known).
- PRINCE2 7 (2023, PeopleCert): seven principles, roles principle now "define roles, responsibilities and relationships" (Wikipedia, Purple Griffon).
- PMP exam updated July 2026: People 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26% (site fact).
- No public official PMI crosswalk from 12 to 6 found: mapping is labelled as our interpretation.
