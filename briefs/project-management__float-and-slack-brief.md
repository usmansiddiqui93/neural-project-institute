## Fact-Checking Notes
- Microsoft Support, "Show slack in your project in Project desktop": Detail Gantt view, Schedule table shows Free Slack and Total Slack; tasks with zero slack are critical by default; threshold set in options ("Tasks are critical if slack is less than or equal to").
- GAO Schedule Assessment Guide, Best Practice 7 "Ensuring Reasonable Total Float": unreasonably high total float may indicate missing or invalid logic; float distorted by incomplete logic, unrealistic durations and unstatused work; customer and contractor managers should proactively manage total float.
- DCMA 14-point assessment (Deltek): high float = incomplete tasks with total float > 44 working days, should not exceed 5% of incomplete tasks; negative float threshold zero; CPLI = (critical path length + total float) / critical path length, 1.00 = on track.
- Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer, "Legal terms explained: float in construction contracts" (2026): JCT and FIDIC do not expressly define float or its ownership; SCL Delay and Disruption Protocol (2nd edition): float not for the exclusive use of either party (absent express terms); NEC4: terminal float cannot be used to mitigate compensation events, so it may be said the contractor owns it.
- Our tool reviews: Wrike Gantt (Team plan up) shows the critical path and float; ClickUp Gantt shows critical path and slack time; Microsoft Planner Plan 3 has baselines and critical path.
- All schedule examples are illustrative; numbers checked by calculation.
