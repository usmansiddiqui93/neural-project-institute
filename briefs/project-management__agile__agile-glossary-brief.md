## Search Intent

Informational / reference. The reader wants a quick, trustworthy definition of one or more Agile terms, often while in a meeting, onboarding, or studying for a certification. Secondary intent: understand how terms relate (which are official Scrum terms, which are practices, which belong to Kanban or SAFe) and which terms are commonly confused.

## Facts to verify / keep consistent

- Agile Manifesto: 17 people, Snowbird, Utah, 11–13 February 2001; 4 values, 12 principles (matches our Manifesto guide).
- 2020 Scrum Guide is current: Developers, Product Goal, commitments, self-managing, Sprint ≤ one month, Daily Scrum 15 minutes, Scrum Team typically 10 or fewer.
- SAFe 6.0 (2023): PI = Planning Interval, typically 8–12 weeks; ART typically 50–125 people (matches our SAFe/PI Planning guides).
- Kanban Guide (Vacanti and Coleman) flow metrics: WIP, throughput, work item age, cycle time.
- DORA: five metrics since 2024 (deployment rework rate added; MTTR renamed failed deployment recovery time in 2023) — dora.dev.
- Nexus: 3–9 Scrum Teams; LeSS: 2–8 teams, LeSS Huge above that (matches site).
- Attributions kept only where well established: INVEST (Bill Wake), Three Cs (Ron Jeffries), technical debt metaphor (Ward Cunningham), Little's Law (John Little), Kanban Method (David J. Anderson).
