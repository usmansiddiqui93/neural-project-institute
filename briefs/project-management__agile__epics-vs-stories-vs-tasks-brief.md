## Fact-checking notes

- Jira default hierarchy and "levels above epic in Premium": Atlassian "Jira work items overview" (checked 3 Nov 2026). Jira now says "work items" (previously "issues"), consistent with our Jira review.
- Scrum Guide 2020: no mention of epics, stories or tasks; Sprint Planning text: decomposing Product Backlog items into smaller work items of one day or less ("often", not a rule).
- INVEST: Bill Wake (2003). 3 Cs: Ron Jeffries. Attribute only by name, no dates needed.
- All example numbers are illustrative.
