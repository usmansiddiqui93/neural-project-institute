## Facts to verify / verified

- CDM 2015 duty holders (client, principal designer, designers, principal contractor, contractors) and HSE notification thresholds (more than 30 working days with more than 20 workers at the same time, or more than 500 person-days): consistent with our construction project manager guide, which cites HSE; also confirmed by Designing Buildings Wiki F10 entries.
- Building Safety Act 2022: higher-risk buildings in England need Building Safety Regulator approval before construction (Gateway 2) and before occupation (Gateway 3): consistent with sibling guides.
- RIBA Plan of Work 2020 stages 0–7; AIA B101–2017 five phases: consistent with architecture guide.
- Last Planner System: developed by Glenn Ballard and Greg Howell (Lean Construction Institute, 1990s); levels: master, phase (pull) planning, lookahead/make-ready, weekly work plan, learning; PPC = completed commitments / commitments made: verified (Mastt, LCI materials).
- ISO 19650: international standard for information management using BIM across the asset life cycle: verified (BSI).
- CMAR works to a guaranteed maximum price: verified (Smartsheet).
- NEC4 early warnings: consistent with infrastructure guide.
- All numbers in worked examples are illustrative and labelled as such. No industry statistics used.
- Tool facts (Wrike #1 9.4, Gantt with dependencies, critical path and baselines; Smartsheet #5 8.6) taken from our review pages.
