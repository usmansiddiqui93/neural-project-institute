## Facts verified (primary or near-primary sources)

- 2020 Scrum Guide (current; site already notes the June 2025 Expansion Pack does not change the rules): Sprints of one month or less; Scrum Team typically 10 or fewer; three accountabilities (Product Owner, Scrum Master, Developers); five events (Sprint, Sprint Planning max 8 h, Daily Scrum 15 min, Sprint Review max 4 h, Sprint Retrospective max 3 h for a one-month Sprint); three artifacts with commitments (Product Goal, Sprint Goal, Definition of Done). Scope may be clarified and renegotiated with the Product Owner during the Sprint; no changes that endanger the Sprint Goal. Velocity, story points and burndown are common practices, not part of the Guide.
- The Kanban Guide (Edition 2025.5, May 2025, Coleman and Vacanti): three practices (define and visualise the workflow, actively manage items in the workflow, improve the workflow) and four flow measures (WIP, throughput, work item age, cycle time). No roles or cadences prescribed.
- Kanban University's Kanban Method: foundational principles plus six general practices; seven cadences (daily Kanban meeting, replenishment, delivery planning, service delivery review, operations review, risk review, strategy review). Optional roles Service Request Manager and Service Delivery Manager. (Consistent with `/project-management/kanban/what-is-kanban/` and `/kanban-ceremonies/`.)
- Scrum.org publishes a Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams (current edition 2021) that adds Kanban flow practices to Scrum without changing Scrum.
- Tool facts from our reviews: Wrike #1 (9.4), board view on the Free plan, custom statuses; Jira #6 (8.6), Scrum and Kanban boards, WIP limits, CFD and control charts, free up to 10 users; ClickUp #4 (8.9), sprints plus per-column WIP limits; Trello #9 (7.8), simple Kanban boards.
