## Search Intent

Informational with commercial investigation. The reader wants a clear list of the features project management software offers, which ones matter for their situation, and how to judge whether a tool's version of a feature is good enough. Many are early in a buying process: they want a checklist they can take into demos and trials, and they want to know which plan tier usually unlocks each feature. The page should answer "what are the key features" in the first sentence and then go deeper than competitors on what good looks like, how to test each feature and how to score tools.
