## Facts to verify / verified

- UKRI (EPSRC page, updated 12 March 2026): funds 80% of fEC; equipment funded at 80% fEC and capital threshold raised to £25,000 from 1 April 2025 (Research Live, March 2025).
- NIH: one no-cost extension of up to 12 months without additional funds under expanded authorities, subject to conditions; further extensions need prior approval (university guidance, August 2025).
- NIH Data Management and Sharing Policy effective 25 January 2023.
- Horizon Europe: DMP mandatory for projects generating/reusing data, full DMP due by month 6 (CASRAI guide).
- RACC credentials: CRA, CPRA, CFRA.
- Site facts: PMP eligibility and ATP rule; PMBOK 8th edition.
- Do not invent success rates or statistics; worked example labelled illustrative.
