What's included

  • Side-by-side comparison grid
  • Weighted scoring model with automatic scores
  • Comparison: Factor, Your company, Competitor A, Competitor B, Competitor C
  • Scoring: Criterion, Weight (%), Your company (1-5), Competitor A (1-5), Competitor B (1-5), Competitor C (1-5)

How to use the competitor analysis template

  1. Choose three to five direct and indirect competitors.
  2. Research each one and fill in the Comparison tab.
  3. Set weights for what customers value most on the Scoring tab.
  4. Score each company from 1 to 5; weighted scores calculate automatically.
  5. Summarise gaps and opportunities and act on them.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Use customer reviews, pricing pages and sales calls as sources.
  • Include indirect competitors and substitutes.
  • Be honest about your own weaknesses.
  • Refresh the analysis every six months.

Frequently asked questions

What is a competitor analysis template?

A competitor analysis template is a structured grid for comparing competitors' products, pricing, audiences, strengths and weaknesses.

Is this competitor analysis template free?

Yes. The competitor analysis template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

What should a competitor analysis template include?

Target customers, offer, pricing, strengths, weaknesses, positioning, channels, market share and recent moves.

How many competitors should I include in the competitor analysis template?

Three to five main competitors is usually enough.

How does the scoring in the competitor analysis template work?

Each criterion has a weight; scores of 1 to 5 are multiplied by the weights to give a weighted score.

Where do I find data for the competitor analysis template?

Websites, pricing pages, reviews, analyst reports, social media and conversations with customers.

Can I use the competitor analysis template for a business plan?

Yes. Summarise it in the competition section of your business plan.

Is a SWOT the same as a competitor analysis template?

No. SWOT looks at your own strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats; a competitor analysis compares rivals.

How often should I update the competitor analysis template?

Every six months, or when a competitor makes a major move.

Can I use the competitor analysis template for competitive intelligence?

Yes. Add a 'Recent moves' log to track competitive intelligence over time.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert competitor analysis template?

The beginner competitor analysis template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions and revision history.
