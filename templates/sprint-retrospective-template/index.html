What's included

  • Observation board with categories and votes
  • Action items with owners, dates, measures and status
  • Retrospective: Category, Observation, Votes, Discussion notes
  • Action items: Action, Owner, Due, Success measure, Status

How to use the sprint retrospective template

  1. Set the stage: remind everyone of the Sprint Goal and keep it blameless.
  2. Gather observations under Went well, To improve and Ideas.
  3. Vote on the most important items.
  4. Discuss root causes and agree one to three actions with owners on the Action items tab.
  5. Start the next retrospective by reviewing last Sprint's actions.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Change the format occasionally; see retrospective games.
  • Limit actions so they actually happen.
  • Add improvement actions to the next Sprint Backlog.
  • Celebrate wins as well as fixing problems.

Frequently asked questions

What is a sprint retrospective template?

A sprint retrospective template is a structured board for reflecting on a Sprint and agreeing improvement actions.

Is this sprint retrospective template free?

Yes. The sprint retrospective template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

What are the sections of a sprint retrospective template?

Typically what went well, what to improve, ideas and action items with owners.

How long should a sprint retrospective using this sprint retrospective template take?

Up to three hours for a one-month Sprint, usually shorter for shorter Sprints.

Who attends the meeting that uses the sprint retrospective template?

The whole Scrum Team: Developers, Product Owner and Scrum Master.

How many action items should a sprint retrospective template have?

One to three focused improvements are usually enough.

Can I use the sprint retrospective template for remote teams?

Yes. Share the Google Sheets sprint retrospective template and let people add notes before voting.

What is the difference between a sprint retrospective template and a lessons learned template?

A sprint retrospective is per Sprint; a lessons learned template usually covers a phase or project.

What retrospective formats work with this sprint retrospective template?

Start-Stop-Continue, Mad-Sad-Glad and 4Ls all fit by renaming the categories.

Should the sprint retrospective template be shared outside the team?

Keep detailed notes within the team to keep it safe; share only the agreed actions if needed.
