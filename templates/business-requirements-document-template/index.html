What's included

  • BRD overview with ten sections

  • BRD overview: Section, Guidance, Your content
  • Requirements: ID, Requirement, Type, Rationale, Priority (MoSCoW), Source, Acceptance criteria, Status

How to use the business requirements document template

  1. Complete the overview: objectives, scope and stakeholders.
  2. Run workshops and interviews to gather requirements.
  3. Write each requirement as a clear, testable statement with a rationale and source.
  4. Prioritise with MoSCoW and add acceptance criteria.
  5. Review with stakeholders and get sign-off before design starts.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Describe what is needed, not how to build it.
  • One requirement per row.
  • Trace every requirement to an objective.
  • Control changes through change requests.

Frequently asked questions

What is a business requirements document template?

A business requirements document template is a structure for recording a project's business objectives, scope, stakeholders and requirements with acceptance criteria.

Is this business requirements document template free?

Yes. The business requirements document template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

What is a BRD template?

A BRD template is the same thing: a business requirements document template.

What should a business requirements document template include?

Summary, objectives, current state, scope, stakeholders, assumptions, constraints, requirements, success measures and sign-off.

Who writes the business requirements document template?

Usually a business analyst, working with stakeholders and the project manager.

What is the difference between a business requirements document template and a functional specification?

A BRD states what the business needs; a functional specification describes how the system will meet those needs.

How do I prioritise in the business requirements document template?

Use MoSCoW: Must, Should, Could and Won't have.

Is a requirements document template the same as a business requirements document template?

A general requirements document template may include technical requirements; a BRD focuses on business needs.

Can Agile teams use a business requirements document template?

Yes, at a high level; detail then lives in user stories.

Who signs off the business requirements document template?

The sponsor and key business stakeholders sign off the BRD.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert business requirements document template?

The beginner business requirements document template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions, a dashboard that summarises your data automatically, status colours and a revision history.
