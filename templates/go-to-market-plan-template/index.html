How to use the go to market plan template

  1. Define the target market, ideal customer and value proposition.
  2. Agree positioning, messaging and pricing.
  3. Choose channels and the sales motion.
  4. Build the launch checklist across product, marketing, sales and support.
  5. Set launch metrics and review them 30 days after launch.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Interview target customers before launch.
  • Launch to a focused segment first.
  • Enable sales and support before marketing goes live.
  • Plan the post-launch review up front.

Frequently asked questions

What is a go to market plan template?

A go to market plan template sets out the target market, positioning, pricing, channels, launch tasks and metrics for a product launch.

Is this go to market plan template free?

Yes. The go to market plan template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

Is the go to market plan template the same as a go to market strategy template?

Yes. It includes both the go to market strategy and the launch plan.

Can I use the go to market plan template as a product launch plan template?

Yes. The Launch plan tab is a product launch plan checklist.

What should a go to market plan template include?

Product, target market, value proposition, positioning, pricing, channels, launch tasks, owners and metrics.

Who owns the go to market plan template?

Usually product marketing, with product, sales and support owning their workstreams.

When should I start the go to market plan template?

Two to three months before launch for most products.

How do I measure success with the go to market plan template?

Set launch goals such as leads, sign-ups or revenue and review them at 30 and 90 days.

What is the difference between a go to market plan template and a marketing plan template?

A go to market plan is for one launch or market entry; a marketing plan covers all marketing for a year.

Can I download the go to market plan template as a PDF?

Yes. Use the PDF button for a printable go to market plan template.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert go to market plan template?

The beginner go to market plan template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions, a dashboard that summarises your data automatically, status colours and a revision history.
