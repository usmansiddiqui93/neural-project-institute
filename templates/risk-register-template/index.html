What's included

  • Risk description, category, probability, impact, auto-calculated score, response, action, owner, review date and status
  • Response and category dropdowns
  • Risk register: ID, Risk (cause → event → effect), Category, Probability (1-5), Impact (1-5), Score, Response, Action, Owner, Review date, Status

How to use the risk register template

  1. Identify risks with the team and write each as cause → event → effect.
  2. Score probability and impact from 1 to 5; the score calculates automatically.
  3. Sort by score and choose a response for the highest risks.
  4. Assign an owner and an action with a review date.
  5. Review the register regularly and record residual risk.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Focus effort on the top 10 risks.
  • Include opportunities as positive risks.
  • Keep scoring definitions consistent.
  • Link the register to your contingency plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is a risk register template?

A risk register template is a log of project risks with their probability, impact, score, owner and planned response.

Is this risk register template free?

Yes. The risk register template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

How is the risk score calculated in the risk register template?

Risk score = probability x impact, each rated 1 to 5, giving a score from 1 to 25.

Can I use the risk register template as a risk assessment template in Excel?

Yes. The risk register template doubles as a risk assessment template in Excel with automatic scoring.

What columns should a risk register template have?

ID, description, category, probability, impact, score, response, action, owner, review date and status.

What risk responses are in the risk register template?

Avoid, mitigate, transfer and accept for threats, and exploit, enhance and share for opportunities.

How often should a risk register template be reviewed?

Review the risk register at least every two weeks and at each phase gate.

What is the difference between a risk register template and a RAID log template?

A risk register tracks risks only; a RAID log template also covers assumptions, issues and dependencies.

Who owns the risk register template?

The project manager maintains the risk register; each risk has a named owner who manages the response.

Can I use the risk register template for risk analysis?

Yes. Sorting by score gives a qualitative risk analysis; use Monte Carlo simulation for quantitative analysis.
