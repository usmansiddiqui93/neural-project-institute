Frequently asked questions

Are these project management templates free?

Yes. Every template is free to download in Google Sheets, Excel (.xlsx) and PDF formats, with no sign-up.

Which project management templates should I start with?

Most projects start with a project charter template, a project plan template and a RACI matrix template, then add a schedule, risk register and status tracker.

Can I use the project management templates in Google Sheets?

Yes. Use the Google Sheets button to make your own copy, or import the .xlsx file into Google Sheets with File > Import.

Do the project management templates work in Excel?

Yes. The .xlsx files open in Microsoft Excel 2010 or later, Excel for the web, LibreOffice and Numbers.

Can I print the project management templates?

Yes. Each template has a print-ready PDF in landscape A4 that also prints well on US Letter.

Can I edit and rebrand the project management templates?

Yes. You may edit, rebrand and use them in your own projects, including commercial work. Please don't resell the templates themselves.

What is the difference between a project plan template and a project schedule template?

A project plan template covers scope, deliverables, owners and milestones; a project schedule template focuses on tasks, dates, durations and dependencies.

Are there Agile and Scrum project management templates?

Yes: see the Agile & Scrum section, including sprint planning, product backlog, user story, Kanban board and sprint retrospective templates.

Should I use project management templates or project management software?

Templates are ideal for small projects and quick starts. As projects grow, tools such as Wrike add real-time collaboration and automation; see our best project management tools.

How often are the project management templates updated?

We review the templates regularly and add new ones based on what project teams search for most.
