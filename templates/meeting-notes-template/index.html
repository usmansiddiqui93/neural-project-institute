What's included

  • Discussion points, decisions and action items with owners, due dates and status
  • Works as a minutes of meeting template
  • Meeting notes: Agenda item, Key discussion points, Decision, Action item, Owner, Due date, Status

How to use the meeting notes template

  1. Fill in the meeting name, date, attendees and apologies.
  2. For each agenda item, note key points briefly, not word for word.
  3. Record decisions clearly in their own column.
  4. Capture every action with one owner and a due date.
  5. Send the notes within 24 hours and review actions at the next meeting.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Write actions as verbs: 'Send', 'Draft', 'Approve'.
  • Separate decisions from discussion.
  • Keep notes factual and neutral.
  • Track open actions in an action plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is a meeting notes template?

A meeting notes template is a structured format for recording attendees, discussion points, decisions and action items from a meeting.

Is this meeting notes template free?

Yes. The meeting notes template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

Does the meeting notes template include action items?

Yes. Each row has an action item, owner, due date and status.

Is the meeting notes template the same as a minutes of meeting template?

It can be used as a minutes of meeting template; formal minutes may also record motions and votes.

Can I use the meeting notes template for project meetings?

Yes. It works as a project meeting notes template; link actions to your project tracker.

What should meeting notes include in the meeting notes template?

Date, attendees, agenda items, key points, decisions, actions, owners and due dates.

Who should fill in the meeting notes template?

A named note-taker, agreed before the meeting starts.

How soon should I share the meeting notes template after a meeting?

Within 24 hours, while details are fresh.

Can I use the meeting notes template in Google Sheets?

Yes. Make a copy and share the meeting notes with attendees.

What is the difference between meeting notes and a meeting agenda template?

The meeting agenda template plans the meeting; meeting notes record outcomes.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert meeting notes template?

The beginner meeting notes template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions, a dashboard that summarises your data automatically, status colours and a revision history.
