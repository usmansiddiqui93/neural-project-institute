What's included

  • Dates, channels, content type, topic, campaign, keyword, owner, status and link
  • Channel and status dropdowns
  • Content calendar: Publish date, Channel, Content type, Topic / title, Campaign, Target keyword, Owner, Status, Link

How to use the content calendar template

  1. Set goals and the channels you will use.
  2. Plan themes or campaigns for the month or quarter.
  3. Add each piece with a publish date, channel, topic, owner and status.
  4. Use the target keyword column for SEO content.
  5. Review weekly and record links once published.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Batch similar content to save time.
  • Mix formats: guides, short posts, video.
  • Leave gaps for timely content.
  • Repurpose one big piece across channels.

Frequently asked questions

What is a content calendar template?

A content calendar template is a schedule of the content you plan to publish, with dates, channels, topics, owners and status.

Is this content calendar template free?

Yes. The content calendar template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

Can I use the content calendar template as a social media calendar template?

Yes. Filter by social channels to use it as a social media content calendar.

Is the content calendar template the same as an editorial calendar template?

Yes. It works as an editorial calendar template for blogs and publications.

Can I use the content calendar template as a marketing calendar template?

Yes. Add campaigns, events and launches to use it as a marketing calendar.

How far ahead should I plan in the content calendar template?

One month in detail and one quarter at theme level.

What columns should a content calendar template have?

Publish date, channel, content type, topic, campaign, keyword, owner, status and link.

Can teams collaborate on the content calendar template?

Yes. Share the Google Sheets content calendar so writers update their own rows.

Can I use the content calendar template for a blog editorial calendar?

Yes. Filter to the Blog channel and use the keyword column for SEO planning.

When should I move from a content calendar template to a tool?

When you need approvals, asset proofing and automation, consider a work management tool such as Wrike.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert content calendar template?

The beginner content calendar template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions, a dashboard that summarises your data automatically, status colours and a revision history.
