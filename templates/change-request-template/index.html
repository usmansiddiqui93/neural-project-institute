What's included

  • A change log with impacts, priority, decision and approver
  • A one-page change request form
  • Change log: CR #, Date raised, Raised by, Change description, Reason, Impact: scope, Impact: time (days), Impact: cost ($), Priority, Decision, Approved by, Decision date
  • Change request form: Field, Details

How to use the change request template

  1. Complete the form for each proposed change.
  2. Assess the impact on scope, time, cost, quality and risk.
  3. Log it in the change log and present it to the approver or change control board.
  4. Record the decision, approver and date.
  5. Update the plan, baseline and stakeholders when a change is approved.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • No change is too small to log.
  • Agree approval thresholds in advance.
  • Consider the combined impact of many small changes.
  • See change control for the full process.

Frequently asked questions

What is a change request template?

A change request template is a form for describing a proposed change to a project, its reasons and impact, and recording the decision.

Is this change request template free?

Yes. The change request template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

What should a change request template include?

Description, reason, impact on scope, schedule, cost, quality and risk, options, recommendation, decision and approver.

Who approves a change request template?

The sponsor or a change control board, depending on the size of the change.

Is a change control template the same as a change request template?

A change control template usually includes the change log and process; this change request template includes both the form and the log.

How do I assess impact in a change request template?

Estimate extra effort, time and cost, and consider effects on quality, risk and other deliverables.

When should I use a change request template?

Use a change request template whenever someone wants to change agreed scope, schedule or budget.

Does Agile need a change request template?

Agile teams handle most change through the backlog, but contracts and fixed budgets may still need a change request.

What is a change log in the change request template?

The change log lists every request with its impact and decision, giving an audit trail.

Can I download the change request template as a PDF?

Yes. Use the PDF button for a printable change request template.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert change request template?

The beginner change request template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions, a dashboard that summarises your data automatically, status colours and a revision history.
