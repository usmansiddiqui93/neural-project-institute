What's included

  • Timed agenda with owners, desired outcomes and pre-reading
  • Works for weekly, staff and formal meetings
  • Agenda: Time, Minutes, Agenda item, Owner, Desired outcome, Pre-reading

How to use the meeting agenda template

  1. Write the meeting's objective in one sentence.
  2. List topics in priority order with an owner and the outcome needed (decision, input or information).
  3. Time-box each item so the total fits the meeting length.
  4. Attach pre-reading and send the agenda at least 24 hours ahead.
  5. Use the meeting notes template to record decisions and actions.

Tips for getting the most from it

  • Put decisions early, while energy is high.
  • If an item has no outcome, it may not need a meeting.
  • Keep 5 minutes to summarise actions.
  • End on time.

Frequently asked questions

What is a meeting agenda template?

A meeting agenda template is a structured list of topics, times, owners and desired outcomes for a meeting.

Is this meeting agenda template free?

Yes. The meeting agenda template is free in Google Sheets, Excel and PDF.

What should a meeting agenda template include?

Objective, date and attendees, timed agenda items, owners, desired outcomes and pre-reading.

Can I use the meeting agenda template for staff meetings?

Yes. It works as a staff meeting agenda template; add a standing item for team updates.

Is there a weekly meeting agenda template?

Yes. Save this meeting agenda template as your weekly meeting agenda and reuse it each week.

Can I use the meeting agenda template for formal meetings?

Yes. For a formal meeting agenda, add apologies, approval of previous minutes and matters arising.

Is there a meeting agenda template in Word?

Download the PDF or Excel meeting agenda template; you can paste it into Word or Google Docs.

How far in advance should I send the meeting agenda template?

At least 24 hours before the meeting, with any pre-reading.

How long should a meeting be with this meeting agenda template?

As short as the objective allows; most regular meetings need 30 to 60 minutes.

What is the difference between a meeting agenda template and a meeting notes template?

The agenda plans the meeting; the meeting notes template records what happened.

What is the difference between the beginner, advanced and expert meeting agenda template?

The beginner meeting agenda template keeps only the essential columns on one tab; the advanced version is the full template with every tab, dropdown and formula; the expert version adds instructions, a dashboard that summarises your data automatically, status colours and a revision history.
