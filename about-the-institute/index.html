About the Neural Project Institute

The Neural Project Institute is an online institute dedicated to project delivery. We write the free Project Management, Agile, Scrum and Kanban guides on this site and award the PMAI certification.

How we write our guides

Every guide is written and reviewed by the Neural Project Institute editorial team. We base our content on widely recognised sources such as the Agile Manifesto, the Scrum Guide and established project management practice, and we explain them in plain, practical language. Some content is prepared with the help of AI tools; all of it is checked before publication, and we correct errors reported to us.

Independence

We are independent and not affiliated with PMI, PeopleCert, Scrum.org, Scrum Alliance, Scaled Agile, Inc. or any software vendor. Product and framework names belong to their owners.
