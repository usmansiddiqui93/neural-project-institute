What is Smartsheet?

Smartsheet was founded in 2005 and is widely used by large enterprises, PMOs and operations teams. Work is organised in sheets that look like spreadsheets but behave like project plans, with dependencies, Gantt charts, forms and automation.

Enterprise features such as Control Center and Dynamic View help organisations standardise and scale project portfolios.

Key features

Grid and Gantt

Spreadsheet-style sheets with dependencies, Gantt and critical path.

Reports and dashboards

Roll up data across sheets into reports and dashboards.

Automation and forms

Workflow automation and forms for intake.

Portfolio management

Portfolios and scenario planning on Enterprise; Control Center on Advanced Work Management.

Proofing

Review and approve images and documents in context.

AI

Conversational AI features on paid plans.

Frequently asked questions

What is Smartsheet?

Smartsheet is a project and work management tool from Smartsheet Inc., launched in 2005. Smartsheet combines a familiar spreadsheet grid with Gantt charts, automation and enterprise portfolio tools. It is a natural step up from Excel, though it lost its free plan in 2025 and advanced capabilities are quote-based.

How much does Smartsheet cost?

From $9/member/month (no free plan). Plans: Pro: $9/member/month annual ($12 monthly); Business: $19/member/month annual ($24 monthly); Enterprise: Contact sales; Advanced Work Management: Contact sales. Prices checked 5 October 2026.

Does Smartsheet have a free plan?

No (30-day free trial).

Does Smartsheet offer a free trial?

30-day free trial.

Is Smartsheet good for small teams?

Yes. It suits small teams willing to invest some setup time; simpler options include Trello and Basecamp.

Is Smartsheet good for large enterprises?

Yes, it offers enterprise plans with advanced security and administration.

Does Smartsheet have Gantt charts?

Yes, Gantt or timeline views are available (plan-dependent).

Does Smartsheet support Agile and Scrum?

It supports Agile boards and some sprint features, but dedicated tools like Jira go further.

Does Smartsheet have time tracking?

Limited, or via integrations.

What are the best Smartsheet alternatives?

Top alternatives include Wrike (for a more collaborative all-in-one platform), monday.com (for a more visual interface), Microsoft Planner (for Microsoft 365 integration), Asana (for simpler task coordination). See our full list of Smartsheet alternatives.

How is Smartsheet rated by users?

4.4/5 on G2 from 22,766 reviews and 4.5/5 on Capterra from 3,557 reviews (checked 5 October 2026).

Who should not use Smartsheet?

Small teams wanting a free plan; Creative teams wanting a visual, board-first tool.
