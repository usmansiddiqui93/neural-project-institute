What is ClickUp?

ClickUp launched in 2017 with the ambition of being "one app to replace them all". It combines task management, docs, whiteboards, chat, goals, time tracking and Agile sprint tools in a single workspace.

Its pricing is among the most competitive for the feature set, which makes it popular with start-ups and growing teams.

Key features

Views

List, board, Gantt, calendar, table, timeline, workload and mind map views.

Docs and whiteboards

Built-in documents and whiteboards linked to tasks.

Agile tools

Sprints, sprint points and reporting on Business.

Time tracking and goals

Native time tracking and goal tracking on Unlimited and above.

Automations

Large automation library, with monthly limits by plan.

AI

Brain AI and Everything AI are paid add-ons.

Frequently asked questions

What is ClickUp?

ClickUp is a project and work management tool from ClickUp, launched in 2017. ClickUp packs an enormous range of features into low-priced plans: tasks, docs, whiteboards, goals, sprints, time tracking and dashboards. The trade-off is complexity, and the best AI features are paid add-ons.

How much does ClickUp cost?

Free; paid from $7/user/month. Plans: Free Forever: $0; Unlimited: $7/user/month annual ($10 monthly); Business: $12/user/month annual ($19 monthly); Enterprise: Contact sales; AI add-ons: $9 or $28/user/month. Prices checked 5 October 2026.
