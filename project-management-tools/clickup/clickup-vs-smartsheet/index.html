ClickUp vs Smartsheet (2026): Which Is Better?

ClickUp is best value for feature-rich teams; Smartsheet is best for spreadsheet-style enterprise planning. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

By Neural Project InstituteUpdated 5 October 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: ClickUp (8.9/10 vs 8.6/10), winning 3 of 7 categories.
  • Choose ClickUp for budget-conscious teams that want tasks, docs, goals, sprints and dashboards in one tool.
  • Choose Smartsheet for organisations comfortable with spreadsheets that need enterprise-grade planning and reporting.
  • Price: ClickUp Free; paid from $7/user/month; Smartsheet From $9/member/month (no free plan).

Quick verdict

ClickUp offers the most features for the money. Wrike remains the stronger choice for structured resource, portfolio and approval management at scale.

Smartsheet is excellent for spreadsheet-minded enterprises. Wrike offers a broader all-in-one experience with stronger collaboration and proofing.

Which should you choose?

Choose ClickUp if…

  • Start-ups and SMBs wanting maximum features per dollar
  • Software and product teams wanting sprints and docs together
  • Teams consolidating several tools
  • Very strong value for money matters to you

Choose Smartsheet if…

  • Enterprises and PMOs standardising project portfolios
  • Operations and finance teams who think in spreadsheets
  • Familiar spreadsheet interface for Excel users matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, ClickUp or Smartsheet?

In our review, ClickUp scores 8.9/10 and Smartsheet 8.6/10. ClickUp wins on feature depth, agile & software teams, value for money; Smartsheet is better for planning & resource management, scalability & enterprise readiness.

Is ClickUp cheaper than Smartsheet?

ClickUp: Free; paid from $7/user/month. Smartsheet: From $9/member/month (no free plan). Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do ClickUp and Smartsheet have free plans?

ClickUp: Yes. Smartsheet: No (30-day free trial).

Which is easier to use, ClickUp or Smartsheet?

Neither (tie) scores higher for ease of use in our review (ClickUp 7/10, Smartsheet 7/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, ClickUp or Smartsheet?

ClickUp is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Which has better resource and portfolio management?

Smartsheet scores higher for planning and resource management (ClickUp 8/10, Smartsheet 9/10).

Which is better for large enterprises?

Smartsheet scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do ClickUp and Smartsheet compare on G2 and Capterra?

ClickUp: 4.6/5 on G2 (14,714 reviews) and 4.6/5 on Capterra (4,634). Smartsheet: 4.4/5 on G2 (22,766) and 4.5/5 on Capterra (3,557). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do ClickUp and Smartsheet have Gantt charts?

ClickUp: yes. Smartsheet: yes (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Smartsheet to ClickUp (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
