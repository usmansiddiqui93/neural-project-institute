Best ClickUp Alternatives in 2026

Thinking of switching from ClickUp? We compared 9 leading alternatives on features, pricing, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings, so you can find the right fit for your team.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 11 September 2026
  • Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Choose Wrike if you want more mature enterprise resource and portfolio management.
  • Choose monday.com if you want a simpler, more polished interface.
  • Choose Asana if you want a cleaner, more focused task experience.
  • Choose Jira if you want dedicated software development tooling.

Why look for ClickUp alternatives?

ClickUp is best value for feature-rich teams. Teams usually look elsewhere for these reasons:

  • Can feel overwhelming; setup takes effort
  • Some users report performance lags
  • Best AI features cost extra
  • Frequent updates can change familiar workflows

Wrike

Best all-in-one work management platform

9.4/10

Choose Wrike if you want more mature enterprise resource and portfolio management. Wrike is a full-featured work management platform that brings project planning, resource management, portfolio views, creative proofing and business intelligence into one place. It takes longer to learn than lighter tools, but few competitors match its depth once a team scales.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to ClickUp?

It depends on why you are switching. Choose Wrike if you want more mature enterprise resource and portfolio management. Choose monday.com if you want a simpler, more polished interface.

Why do teams look for ClickUp alternatives?

The most common reasons are: can feel overwhelming; setup takes effort; some users report performance lags; best ai features cost extra.

Is there a free alternative to ClickUp?

Yes: Wrike, monday.com, Asana, Jira, Notion, Trello, Basecamp have free plans.

What is a cheaper alternative to ClickUp?

Trello (from $5/user/month) and ClickUp (from $7/user/month, annual) are among the lowest-priced paid options; Basecamp's flat pricing can be cheaper for large teams.

What is an easier alternative to ClickUp?

Trello and monday.com are the easiest tools to learn, followed by Asana and Basecamp.

What is the best ClickUp alternative for enterprises?

Wrike, Smartsheet and monday.com offer the strongest enterprise features in our review.

What is the best ClickUp alternative for software teams?

Jira is the standard for software teams; ClickUp is a flexible, lower-cost option.

Which ClickUp alternative has the best user ratings?

On G2, monday.com (4.7), ClickUp (4.6) and Notion (4.6) have some of the highest ratings among the tools we reviewed (checked 5 October 2026).

Can I migrate from ClickUp to another tool?

Most tools offer CSV import and many provide direct importers from popular competitors. Plan a pilot project, map fields and statuses, and migrate active work first.

How did you choose these ClickUp alternatives?

We compared the other nine tools in our top 10 on features, pricing, ease of use, scalability and published G2 and Capterra ratings, and ordered them by how well they address common reasons for switching.

How we review. Rankings and scores are the editorial opinion of the Neural Project Institute, based on features, pricing, ease of use, scalability and published user ratings. Prices were checked on vendors' pricing pages and ratings on G2 and Capterra on 5 October 2026; both change often, so confirm on the vendor's site before buying.
