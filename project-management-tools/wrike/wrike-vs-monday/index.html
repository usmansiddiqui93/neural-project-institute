Wrike vs monday.com (2026): Which Is Better?

Wrike is best all-in-one work management platform; monday.com is best for ease of use and visual workflows. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

By Neural Project InstituteUpdated 5 October 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: Wrike (9.4/10 vs 9.1/10), winning 4 of 7 categories.
  • Choose Wrike for growing teams and enterprises that want one platform for projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting.
  • Choose monday.com for teams that want a colourful, intuitive platform they can shape into almost any workflow.
  • Price: Wrike Free; paid from $10/user/month; monday.com Free for up to 2 seats; paid from $9/seat/month.

Quick verdict

Wrike is the most complete all-in-one choice in this list. If you need projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting in one place, and can invest a little time in setup, it is our top recommendation.

monday.com is the easiest powerful platform to roll out. Choose it when adoption and flexibility matter most; choose Wrike if you need deeper resource and portfolio control.

Which should you choose?

Choose Wrike if…

  • PMOs and professional services firms managing many projects and resources
  • Marketing and creative teams that need proofing and request intake
  • Organisations replacing several point tools with one platform
  • One of the most complete feature sets in the category matters to you

Choose monday.com if…

  • Non-technical teams that need fast adoption
  • Marketing, operations and agencies with varied workflows
  • Teams that value visual dashboards
  • Very easy to learn and adopt matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Wrike or monday.com?

In our review, Wrike scores 9.4/10 and monday.com 9.1/10. Wrike wins on feature depth, planning & resource management, agile & software teams, scalability & enterprise readiness; monday.com is better for ease of use.

Is Wrike cheaper than monday.com?

Wrike: Free; paid from $10/user/month. monday.com: Free for up to 2 seats; paid from $9/seat/month. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do Wrike and monday.com have free plans?

Wrike: Yes. monday.com: Yes (up to 2 seats).

Which is easier to use, Wrike or monday.com?

monday.com scores higher for ease of use in our review (Wrike 7/10, monday.com 9/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, Wrike or monday.com?

Wrike is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Which has better resource and portfolio management?

Wrike scores higher for planning and resource management (Wrike 10/10, monday.com 8/10).

Which is better for large enterprises?

Wrike scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do Wrike and monday.com compare on G2 and Capterra?

Wrike: 4.2/5 on G2 (4,537 reviews) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (3,041). monday.com: 4.7/5 on G2 (17,248) and 4.6/5 on Capterra (6,120). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do Wrike and monday.com have Gantt charts?

Wrike: yes. monday.com: yes (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from monday.com to Wrike (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
