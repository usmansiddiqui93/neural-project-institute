Wrike vs Jira (2026): Which Is Better?

Wrike is best all-in-one work management platform; Jira is best for software and agile teams. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

By Neural Project InstituteUpdated 5 October 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: Wrike (9.4/10 vs 8.6/10), winning 5 of 7 categories.
  • Choose Wrike for growing teams and enterprises that want one platform for projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting.
  • Choose Jira for software development and IT teams running Scrum or Kanban.
  • Price: Wrike Free; paid from $10/user/month; Jira Free for up to 10 users; Standard roughly $7.91/user/month.

Quick verdict

Wrike is the most complete all-in-one choice in this list. If you need projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting in one place, and can invest a little time in setup, it is our top recommendation.

Jira is the strongest choice for software teams. For organisations that need one platform across engineering, marketing and operations, Wrike or monday.com are easier to roll out widely.

Which should you choose?

Choose Wrike if…

  • PMOs and professional services firms managing many projects and resources
  • Marketing and creative teams that need proofing and request intake
  • Organisations replacing several point tools with one platform
  • One of the most complete feature sets in the category matters to you

Choose Jira if…

  • Software engineering and IT teams
  • Organisations already using Atlassian tools
  • Best-in-class Agile and Scrum tooling matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Wrike or Jira?

In our review, Wrike scores 9.4/10 and Jira 8.6/10. Wrike wins on ease of use, feature depth, planning & resource management, collaboration, scalability & enterprise readiness; Jira is better for agile & software teams, value for money.

Is Wrike cheaper than Jira?

Wrike: Free; paid from $10/user/month. Jira: Free for up to 10 users; Standard roughly $7.91/user/month. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do Wrike and Jira have free plans?

Wrike: Yes. Jira: Yes (up to 10 users).

Which is easier to use, Wrike or Jira?

Wrike scores higher for ease of use in our review (Wrike 7/10, Jira 6/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, Wrike or Jira?

Jira is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Which has better resource and portfolio management?

Wrike scores higher for planning and resource management (Wrike 10/10, Jira 7/10).

Which is better for large enterprises?

Wrike scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do Wrike and Jira compare on G2 and Capterra?

Wrike: 4.2/5 on G2 (4,537 reviews) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (3,041). Jira: 4.3/5 on G2 (7,988) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (15,493). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do Wrike and Jira have Gantt charts?

Wrike: yes. Jira: yes (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Jira to Wrike (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
