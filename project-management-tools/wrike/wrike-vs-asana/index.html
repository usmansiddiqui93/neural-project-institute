Wrike vs Asana (2026): Which Is Better?

Wrike is best all-in-one work management platform; Asana is best for cross-team task and goal management. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 15 September 2026
  • Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: Wrike (9.4/10 vs 9.0/10), winning 4 of 7 categories.
  • Choose Wrike for growing teams and enterprises that want one platform for projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting.
  • Choose Asana for organisations that want clean, reliable task management connected to goals and portfolios.
  • Price: Wrike Free; paid from $10/user/month; Asana Free (Personal); paid from $10.99/user/month.

Quick verdict

Wrike is the most complete all-in-one choice in this list. If you need projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting in one place, and can invest a little time in setup, it is our top recommendation.

Asana is a superb, easy choice for coordinating work across teams. For deeper resource, budget and approval management in one platform, Wrike goes further.

Which should you choose?

Choose Wrike if…

  • PMOs and professional services firms managing many projects and resources
  • Marketing and creative teams that need proofing and request intake
  • Organisations replacing several point tools with one platform
  • One of the most complete feature sets in the category matters to you

Choose Asana if…

  • Cross-functional teams in mid-size and large organisations
  • Companies that manage by goals and OKRs
  • Marketing and operations teams
  • Clean, intuitive interface matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Wrike or Asana?

In our review, Wrike scores 9.4/10 and Asana 9.0/10. Wrike wins on feature depth, planning & resource management, agile & software teams, scalability & enterprise readiness; Asana is better for ease of use.

Is Wrike cheaper than Asana?

Wrike: Free; paid from $10/user/month. Asana: Free (Personal); paid from $10.99/user/month. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do Wrike and Asana have free plans?

Wrike: Yes. Asana: Yes (Personal plan).

Which is easier to use, Wrike or Asana?

Asana scores higher for ease of use in our review (Wrike 7/10, Asana 9/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, Wrike or Asana?

Wrike is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Wrike vs Asana: which has better resource and portfolio management?

Wrike scores higher for planning and resource management (Wrike 10/10, Asana 8/10).

Wrike vs Asana: which is better for large enterprises?

Wrike scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do Wrike and Asana compare on G2 and Capterra?

Wrike: 4.2/5 on G2 (4,537 reviews) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (3,041). Asana: 4.4/5 on G2 (14,008) and 4.5/5 on Capterra (13,731). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do Wrike and Asana have Gantt charts?

Wrike: yes. Asana: yes (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Asana to Wrike (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
