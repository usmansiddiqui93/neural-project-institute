Brief reviews of the top 10 tools

#1

Wrike

Best all-in-one work management platform

9.4/10

Wrike is a full-featured work management platform that brings project planning, resource management, portfolio views, creative proofing and business intelligence into one place. It takes longer to learn than lighter tools, but few competitors match its depth once a team scales.

Starting priceFree; paid from $10/user/month
Free planYes
User ratingsG2 4.2/5 (4,537)
Capterra 4.4/5 (3,041)

Pros

  • One of the most complete feature sets in the category
  • Strong resource, workload and portfolio management
  • Built-in proofing and approvals for creative work
  • Highly configurable custom item types, fields and workflows

Cons

  • Steeper learning curve than simpler tools
  • Interface can feel dense for new users
  • Advanced resource, budgeting and BI features sit on higher, quote-based tiers

How to choose a project management tool

  1. Match the tool to your method. Predictive projects need Gantt charts and baselines; Agile teams need backlogs and sprints; continuous work needs Kanban and WIP limits.
  2. Decide how much you need in one place. If you want projects, resources, portfolios and approvals together, an all-in-one platform like Wrike avoids stitching tools together.
  3. Check who needs access. Guests, clients and viewers can change the cost dramatically.
  4. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.
  5. Trial on a real project for two weeks with the people who will use it daily.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best project management tool in 2026?

Our top pick is Wrike for most growing teams and enterprises because it combines projects, resources, portfolios, approvals and reporting in one platform. monday.com is best for ease of use, Asana for cross-team coordination, ClickUp for value and Jira for software teams.

What is the best free project management tool?

ClickUp, Trello, Wrike, Notion and Jira (up to 10 users) all have free plans. Trello is the simplest; ClickUp offers the most features; Wrike's free plan is a good start if you expect to grow into a full work management platform.

What is the cheapest project management software?

Among per-user tools, Trello Standard ($5/user/month annual) and ClickUp Unlimited ($7/user/month annual) are the cheapest paid plans. Basecamp's flat pricing can be cheapest for large teams with few projects.

Which project management tool is best for small teams?

Trello, ClickUp, Asana and Basecamp suit small teams. Wrike's Team plan (2–15 users) is a good choice if the team needs Gantt charts and dashboards.

Which project management tool is best for enterprises?

Wrike, Smartsheet, monday.com, Asana and Jira all offer enterprise plans. Wrike and Smartsheet are particularly strong for PMOs needing portfolio and resource management.

Which project management tool is best for Agile and Scrum?

Jira is the standard for software teams. ClickUp and Wrike also support sprints and Agile boards; see our Scrum guide.

Which project management tool is easiest to use?

Trello and monday.com are the easiest to learn, followed by Asana and Basecamp.

Do I need Gantt charts in a project management tool?

If your projects have dependencies and fixed dates, yes. Wrike, Smartsheet, monday.com, Asana, ClickUp and Microsoft Planner's paid plans include Gantt or timeline views. See Gantt charts.

Is Microsoft Project still available as a project management tool?

Microsoft retired Project for the web in 2025 and moved its features into Microsoft Planner's premium plans, and has announced the retirement of Project Online. See our Microsoft Planner review.

How did you choose and rank these tools?

We evaluated features, planning and resource depth, collaboration, Agile support, ease of use, value for money and scalability, and considered published user ratings on G2 and Capterra. Rankings are our editorial opinion.

How often are project management tool prices and ratings updated?

We checked them on 5 October 2026. Vendors change prices and plans frequently, so always confirm on the vendor's pricing page.

Can project management software use AI?

Yes. All ten tools now offer AI features, from summaries and writing help to automated workflows. Some include AI in plans; others, like ClickUp and Microsoft 365 Copilot, sell it as an add-on.
