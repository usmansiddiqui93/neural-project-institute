What is monday.com?

monday.com launched in 2012 and has grown into a broad work platform with separate products for work management, CRM, service and software development (monday dev). Its board-based interface is colourful and approachable, which helps adoption in non-technical teams.

Boards can be shaped into project plans, trackers, content calendars or intake processes, and switched between table, Kanban, timeline, Gantt and calendar views.

Key features

Boards and views

Flexible boards with many column types and views, including Kanban, timeline, Gantt, calendar and charts.

Automations and integrations

Recipe-style automations and integrations, with monthly action limits by plan.

Dashboards

Combine data from several boards into dashboards for status and workload.

Docs

monday docs for collaborative documents connected to boards.

AI

AI tools and credits included on paid plans for summarising, writing and automating work.

Product family

Separate CRM, service and dev products share the same platform.

Pros and cons

Pros

  • Very easy to learn and adopt
  • Highly customisable boards and views
  • Attractive, visual interface
  • Strong dashboards and automation recipes
  • Large template library

Cons

  • Seats are sold in bundles, which can raise costs for small teams
  • Automation and integration actions are capped by plan
  • Time tracking and some advanced features require Pro or higher
  • Advanced resource and portfolio features are on Enterprise

Look elsewhere if

  • Teams needing advanced scheduling with critical path and baselines on a budget
  • Very cost-sensitive small teams
