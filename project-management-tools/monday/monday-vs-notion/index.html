monday.com vs Notion (2026): Which Is Better?

monday.com is best for ease of use and visual workflows; Notion is best for docs and projects in one workspace. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 21 September 2026
  • Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: monday.com (9.1/10 vs 8.0/10), winning 5 of 7 categories.
  • Choose monday.com for teams that want a colourful, intuitive platform they can shape into almost any workflow.
  • Choose Notion for teams that want documents, wikis and lightweight project tracking in one flexible workspace.
  • Price: monday.com Free for up to 2 seats; paid from $9/seat/month; Notion Free; paid from $10/member/month.

Quick verdict

monday.com is the easiest powerful platform to roll out. Choose it when adoption and flexibility matter most; choose Wrike if you need deeper resource and portfolio control.

Notion is the best docs-first workspace. For structured project, resource and portfolio management, Wrike, monday.com or Asana are stronger.

Which should you choose?

Choose monday.com if…

  • Non-technical teams that need fast adoption
  • Marketing, operations and agencies with varied workflows
  • Teams that value visual dashboards
  • Very easy to learn and adopt matters to you

Choose Notion if…

  • Start-ups and small teams wanting docs and tasks together
  • Product teams managing specs and roadmaps
  • Excellent docs and knowledge base matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, monday.com or Notion?

In our review, monday.com scores 9.1/10 and Notion 8.0/10. monday.com wins on ease of use, feature depth, planning & resource management, agile & software teams, scalability & enterprise readiness; Notion is better for value for money.

Is monday.com cheaper than Notion?

monday.com: Free for up to 2 seats; paid from $9/seat/month. Notion: Free; paid from $10/member/month. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do monday.com and Notion have free plans?

monday.com: Yes (up to 2 seats). Notion: Yes.

Which is easier to use, monday.com or Notion?

monday.com scores higher for ease of use in our review (monday.com 9/10, Notion 7/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, monday.com or Notion?

monday.com is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

monday.com vs Notion: which has better resource and portfolio management?

monday.com scores higher for planning and resource management (monday.com 8/10, Notion 5/10).

monday.com vs Notion: which is better for large enterprises?

monday.com scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do monday.com and Notion compare on G2 and Capterra?

monday.com: 4.7/5 on G2 (17,248 reviews) and 4.6/5 on Capterra (6,120). Notion: 4.6/5 on G2 (13,862) and 4.7/5 on Capterra (2,829). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do monday.com and Notion have Gantt charts?

monday.com: yes. Notion: yes (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Notion to monday.com (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
