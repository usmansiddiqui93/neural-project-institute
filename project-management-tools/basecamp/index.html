What is Basecamp?

Basecamp, from 37signals, has been around since 2004. It focuses on calm, organised communication rather than detailed scheduling or resource management.

Its flat monthly pricing with unlimited users on higher tiers makes it attractive for agencies working with many clients.

Key features

Message boards and chat

Centralised project discussions instead of scattered email.

To-dos and Card Table

Task lists and a Kanban-style Card Table.

Schedules and Lineup

Project schedules and a cross-project timeline.

Docs and files

Documents and file storage per project.

Client access

Invite clients with controlled visibility.

Frequently asked questions

What is Basecamp?

Basecamp is a project and work management tool from 37signals, launched in 2004. Basecamp keeps projects simple: message boards, to-dos, schedules, docs, chat and client access in one place, with flat pricing and no per-user fees. It deliberately avoids complex PM features.

How much does Basecamp cost?

Free (1 project); paid from $25/month flat. Plans: Free: $0; Freelancer: $25/month; Studio: $59/month; Pro: $99/month; Unlimited: $299/month (annual). Prices checked 5 October 2026.

Does Basecamp have a free plan?

Yes (1 project).

Does Basecamp offer a free trial?

Free trial; 45 days on Unlimited.

Is Basecamp good for small teams?

Yes. Agencies and consultancies working with clients

Is Basecamp good for large enterprises?

It can work for larger organisations, but tools such as Wrike or Smartsheet offer more enterprise depth.

Does Basecamp have Gantt charts?

It offers a basic timeline, but not full Gantt scheduling with dependencies on all plans.

Does Basecamp support Agile and Scrum?

Not really; it is not designed for sprints.

Does Basecamp have time tracking?

Yes, native time tracking is available (plan-dependent).

What are the best Basecamp alternatives?

Top alternatives include Asana (for structured task management), Wrike (for full project, resource and approval management), monday.com (for flexible, visual workflows), Trello (for simple Kanban at per-user pricing). See our full list of Basecamp alternatives.

How is Basecamp rated by users?

4.1/5 on G2 from 5,489 reviews and 4.4/5 on Capterra from 14,447 reviews (checked 5 October 2026).

Who should not use Basecamp?

Teams needing scheduling, resource or portfolio controls; Software teams running sprints.
