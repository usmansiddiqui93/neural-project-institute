What is Asana?

Asana was founded in 2008 by Dustin Moskovitz and Justin Rosenstein. It is known for a clean interface and dependable task management, and has grown to include goals, portfolios, workload management and AI features.

Asana works well when many teams need to coordinate work and see how it ladders up to company objectives.

Key features

Projects and tasks

List, board, calendar, timeline and Gantt views with dependencies and milestones.

Goals and portfolios

Connect projects to company goals and monitor many projects in portfolios (Advanced).

Workload

Balance team capacity with workload views (Advanced).

Rules and forms

Automate routine steps and standardise intake with forms.

Approvals and proofing

Review and approve work in context on Advanced.

AI

AI teammates and AI Studio for building AI-powered workflows, with allowances by plan.

Pros

Cons

  • Portfolios, workload and time tracking require Advanced

Who should use Asana?

Great for

  • Cross-functional teams in mid-size and large organisations
  • Companies that manage by goals and OKRs
  • Marketing and operations teams

Look elsewhere if

  • Teams needing detailed budgeting and financial tracking
  • Very small teams on a tight budget needing advanced features

Frequently asked questions

What is Asana?

Asana is a project and work management tool from Asana, Inc., launched in 2008. Asana is a polished, easy-to-use work management tool that excels at coordinating tasks across teams and linking them to company goals. Advanced capabilities such as portfolios and resource management are reserved for higher plans.

How much does Asana cost?

Free (Personal); paid from $10.99/user/month. Plans: Personal: $0; Starter: $10.99/user/month annual ($13.49 monthly); Advanced: $24.99/user/month annual ($30.49 monthly); Enterprise: Contact sales. Prices checked 5 October 2026.

Does Asana have a free plan?

Yes (Personal plan).

Does Asana offer a free trial?

Free trial available.

Is Asana good for small teams?

Yes. Cross-functional teams in mid-size and large organisations

Is Asana good for large enterprises?

Yes, it offers enterprise plans with advanced security and administration.

Does Asana have Gantt charts?

Yes, Gantt or timeline views are available (plan-dependent).

Does Asana support Agile and Scrum?

It supports Agile boards and some sprint features, but dedicated tools like Jira go further.

Does Asana have time tracking?

Yes, native time tracking is available (plan-dependent).
