What is Jira?

Atlassian launched Jira in 2002 as an issue tracker. It has become the default Agile tool for many software organisations, with backlogs, sprints, boards, roadmaps, releases and deep integrations with developer tools.

Jira's per-user price falls as team size grows, and a free plan covers up to 10 users. Pricing shown is a third-party estimate for small teams; check Atlassian for your team size.

Key features

Scrum and Kanban boards

Backlogs, sprints, story points, velocity and burndown reports.

Issue tracking

Highly configurable issue types, fields and workflows.

Timelines and planning

Timeline view on all plans; advanced cross-team planning on Premium.

Automation

Rule-based automation across projects.

Developer integrations

Connects with code repositories, CI/CD and incident tools.

Atlassian ecosystem

Pairs with Confluence for docs and a large app marketplace.

Verdict

Jira is the strongest choice for software teams. For organisations that need one platform across engineering, marketing and operations, Wrike or monday.com are easier to roll out widely.

Frequently asked questions

What is Jira?

Jira is a project and work management tool from Atlassian, launched in 2002. Jira is the industry standard for Agile software teams, with deep Scrum and Kanban support, issue tracking and developer integrations. It is less suited to non-technical teams and has a steep learning curve.

How much does Jira cost?

Free for up to 10 users; Standard roughly $7.91/user/month. Plans: Free: $0; Standard: About $7.91/user/month (monthly, small teams); Premium: About $14.54/user/month (monthly, small teams); Enterprise: Contact sales. Prices checked 5 October 2026.

Does Jira have a free plan?

Yes (up to 10 users).

Does Jira offer a free trial?

Free trial of paid plans.

Is Jira good for small teams?

Yes. It suits small teams willing to invest some setup time; simpler options include Trello and Basecamp.

Is Jira good for large enterprises?

Yes, it offers enterprise plans with advanced security and administration.

Does Jira have Gantt charts?

Yes, Gantt or timeline views are available (plan-dependent).

Does Jira support Agile and Scrum?

Yes, it includes sprint and Agile tooling.

Does Jira have time tracking?

Limited, or via integrations.

What are the best Jira alternatives?

Top alternatives include ClickUp (for Agile features with docs and lower complexity), Wrike (for an all-in-one platform for non-technical teams too), Asana (for simpler cross-team task management), Trello (for simple Kanban boards). See our full list of Jira alternatives.

How is Jira rated by users?

4.3/5 on G2 from 7,988 reviews and 4.4/5 on Capterra from 15,493 reviews (checked 5 October 2026).

Who should not use Jira?

Marketing, creative or business teams needing a simple tool; Teams without someone to administer it.
