Best Jira Alternatives in 2026

Thinking of switching from Jira? We compared 9 leading alternatives on features, pricing, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings, so you can find the right fit for your team.

  • Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Choose Wrike if you want an all-in-one platform for non-technical teams too.
  • Choose monday.com if you want monday dev with a friendlier interface.
  • Choose Asana if you want simpler cross-team task management.
  • Choose ClickUp if you want Agile features with docs and lower complexity.

Why look for Jira alternatives?

Jira is best for software and agile teams. Teams usually look elsewhere for these reasons:

  • Steep learning curve and complex admin
  • Not designed for non-technical teams
  • Docs require Confluence
  • No proofing or creative workflows

Wrike

Best all-in-one work management platform

9.4/10

Choose Wrike if you want an all-in-one platform for non-technical teams too. Wrike is a full-featured work management platform that brings project planning, resource management, portfolio views, creative proofing and business intelligence into one place. It takes longer to learn than lighter tools, but few competitors match its depth once a team scales.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Jira?

It depends on why you are switching. Choose Wrike if you want an all-in-one platform for non-technical teams too. Choose monday.com if you want monday dev with a friendlier interface.

Why do teams look for Jira alternatives?

The most common reasons are: steep learning curve and complex admin; not designed for non-technical teams; docs require confluence.

Is there a free alternative to Jira?

Yes: Wrike, monday.com, Asana, ClickUp, Trello, Notion, Basecamp have free plans.

What is a cheaper alternative to Jira?

Trello (from $5/user/month) and ClickUp (from $7/user/month, annual) are among the lowest-priced paid options; Basecamp's flat pricing can be cheaper for large teams.

What is an easier alternative to Jira?

Trello and monday.com are the easiest tools to learn, followed by Asana and Basecamp.

What is the best Jira alternative for enterprises?

Wrike, Smartsheet and monday.com offer the strongest enterprise features in our review.

What is the best Jira alternative for software teams?

ClickUp and Wrike offer Agile features with broader business use.

Which Jira alternative has the best user ratings?

On G2, monday.com (4.7), ClickUp (4.6) and Notion (4.6) have some of the highest ratings among the tools we reviewed (checked 5 October 2026).

Can I migrate from Jira to another tool?

Most tools offer CSV import and many provide direct importers from popular competitors. Plan a pilot project, map fields and statuses, and migrate active work first.

How did you choose these Jira alternatives?

We compared the other nine tools in our top 10 on features, pricing, ease of use, scalability and published G2 and Capterra ratings, and ordered them by how well they address common reasons for switching.

How we review. Rankings and scores are the editorial opinion of the Neural Project Institute, based on features, pricing, ease of use, scalability and published user ratings. Prices were checked on vendors' pricing pages and ratings on G2 and Capterra on 5 October 2026; both change often, so confirm on the vendor's site before buying.
