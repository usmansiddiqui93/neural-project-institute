Jira vs Trello (2026): Which Is Better?

Jira is best for software and agile teams; Trello is best for simple kanban boards. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

By Neural Project InstituteUpdated 5 October 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: Jira (8.6/10 vs 7.8/10), winning 4 of 7 categories.
  • Choose Jira for software development and IT teams running Scrum or Kanban.
  • Choose Trello for individuals and small teams that want simple, visual Kanban boards.
  • Price: Jira Free for up to 10 users; Standard roughly $7.91/user/month; Trello Free; paid from $5/user/month.

Quick verdict

Jira is the strongest choice for software teams. For organisations that need one platform across engineering, marketing and operations, Wrike or monday.com are easier to roll out widely.

Trello is perfect for simple boards. When projects grow in complexity, teams typically move to Wrike, Asana or monday.com.

Which should you choose?

Choose Jira if…

  • Software engineering and IT teams
  • Organisations already using Atlassian tools
  • Best-in-class Agile and Scrum tooling matters to you

Choose Trello if…

  • Individuals and small teams
  • Simple workflows such as content calendars or recruiting pipelines
  • Extremely easy to learn matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Jira or Trello?

In our review, Jira scores 8.6/10 and Trello 7.8/10. Jira wins on feature depth, planning & resource management, agile & software teams, scalability & enterprise readiness; Trello is better for ease of use, value for money.

Is Jira cheaper than Trello?

Jira: Free for up to 10 users; Standard roughly $7.91/user/month. Trello: Free; paid from $5/user/month. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do Jira and Trello have free plans?

Jira: Yes (up to 10 users). Trello: Yes (up to 10 collaborators per workspace).

Which is easier to use, Jira or Trello?

Trello scores higher for ease of use in our review (Jira 6/10, Trello 10/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, Jira or Trello?

Jira is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Which has better resource and portfolio management?

Jira scores higher for planning and resource management (Jira 7/10, Trello 4/10).

Which is better for large enterprises?

Jira scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do Jira and Trello compare on G2 and Capterra?

Jira: 4.3/5 on G2 (7,988 reviews) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (15,493). Trello: 4.4/5 on G2 (14,063) and 4.5/5 on Capterra (23,624). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do Jira and Trello have Gantt charts?

Jira: yes. Trello: partial (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Trello to Jira (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
