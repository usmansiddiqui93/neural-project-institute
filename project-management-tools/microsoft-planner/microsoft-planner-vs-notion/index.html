Microsoft Planner vs Notion (2026): Which Is Better?

Microsoft Planner is best for microsoft 365 organisations; Notion is best for docs and projects in one workspace. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

By Neural Project InstituteUpdated 5 October 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: Microsoft Planner (8.1/10 vs 8.0/10), winning 3 of 7 categories.
  • Choose Microsoft Planner for organisations standardised on Microsoft 365 and Teams.
  • Choose Notion for teams that want documents, wikis and lightweight project tracking in one flexible workspace.
  • Price: Microsoft Planner Basic Planner included in Microsoft 365; Plan 1 from $10/user/month; Notion Free; paid from $10/member/month.

Quick verdict

Planner is the convenient default for Microsoft 365 shops. For richer collaboration, resource and portfolio management, Wrike integrates with Microsoft tools while offering far more depth.

Notion is the best docs-first workspace. For structured project, resource and portfolio management, Wrike, monday.com or Asana are stronger.

Which should you choose?

Choose Microsoft Planner if…

  • Organisations already on Microsoft 365 and Teams
  • Teams needing simple, integrated task management
  • Included or low-cost for Microsoft 365 customers matters to you

Choose Notion if…

  • Start-ups and small teams wanting docs and tasks together
  • Product teams managing specs and roadmaps
  • Excellent docs and knowledge base matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Microsoft Planner or Notion?

In our review, Microsoft Planner scores 8.1/10 and Notion 8.0/10. Microsoft Planner wins on ease of use, planning & resource management, scalability & enterprise readiness; Notion is better for feature depth, collaboration, value for money.

Is Microsoft Planner cheaper than Notion?

Microsoft Planner: Basic Planner included in Microsoft 365; Plan 1 from $10/user/month. Notion: Free; paid from $10/member/month. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do Microsoft Planner and Notion have free plans?

Microsoft Planner: Basic Planner included with Microsoft 365. Notion: Yes.

Which is easier to use, Microsoft Planner or Notion?

Microsoft Planner scores higher for ease of use in our review (Microsoft Planner 8/10, Notion 7/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, Microsoft Planner or Notion?

Neither (tie) is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Which has better resource and portfolio management?

Microsoft Planner scores higher for planning and resource management (Microsoft Planner 7/10, Notion 5/10).

Which is better for large enterprises?

Microsoft Planner scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do Microsoft Planner and Notion compare on G2 and Capterra?

Microsoft Planner: 4.2/5 on G2 (237 reviews) and 4.3/5 on Capterra (298). Notion: 4.6/5 on G2 (13,862) and 4.7/5 on Capterra (2,829). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do Microsoft Planner and Notion have Gantt charts?

Microsoft Planner: yes. Notion: yes (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Notion to Microsoft Planner (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
