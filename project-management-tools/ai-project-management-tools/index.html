Best AI Project Management Tools in 2026

AI is now built into most project management software, from assistants that summarise work to agents that triage requests and flag risks. Here are the 9 best AI project management tools, what their AI actually does, and how it is priced.

Written byNeural Project InstituteProject management editorial team
  • Published 26 August 2026
  • Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Best overall: Wrike, with agents and Copilot working on full project, resource and portfolio data.
  • Easiest AI agents: monday.com. Most AI models: ClickUp. Best for software teams: Jira with Rovo.
  • AI included in paid plans: Smartsheet, Jira and Wrike (AI Essentials); ClickUp charges a per-user AI add-on.
  • Check credit and action limits: agent-heavy use can cost more than the base plan.

The 9 best AI project management tools

#1

Wrike

Best overall AI work management platform

Wrike combines a full work management platform with AI at two levels: everyday AI for every paid user, and autonomous agents plus a Copilot assistant for teams that want AI to run parts of their process.

Key AI features

  • AI Essentials on paid plans from Team: content creation and editing, comment and work-item summaries, Board AI, mobile inbox prioritisation and automation rules written in plain language.
  • Wrike AI Agents (AI Elite): configurable agents for intake, triage, auto-assignment and risk identification.
  • Wrike Copilot (AI Elite): an assistant inside your projects that answers questions and summarises work.
  • MCP Server: connect Wrike to external AI assistants securely.
How AI is pricedAI Essentials is included from the Team plan. AI Elite actions are included from Business (starter allowance), with more on Pinnacle and Apex; extra action packs are sold at custom pricing.

AI pros

  • Agents act on real project, resource and portfolio data
  • Everyday AI included in paid plans
  • Strong enterprise security and governance

AI cons

  • Agents and Copilot need Business or higher
  • Heavier AI use means buying action packs

Best fit: PMOs, marketing and professional services teams that want AI to triage, assign and flag risks across many projects.

#2

monday.com

Best for easy AI agents and no-code AI apps

monday.com's AI spans a personal assistant (Sidekick), ready-made and custom agents, AI blocks in automations and Vibe, which turns a prompt into a working app on your board data.

Key AI features

  • Sidekick: a context-aware assistant across boards, docs and items that drafts content, dashboards and workflows.
  • Agents: ready-made agents for sales, marketing, IT, HR and operations, or build your own.
  • AI blocks in automations: summarise, categorise, extract and translate inside workflows.
  • Vibe: build custom apps such as trackers and calculators from a prompt.
  • MCP: connect external AI assistants to your workspace.
How AI is pricedAccounts get a monthly allowance of AI credits (500 per account at the time of checking); Sidekick Lite is included on Standard and Pro, with larger Sidekick tiers on Enterprise or as paid upgrades.

AI pros

  • Very easy to start with templates and agents
  • AI built into automations
  • Vibe apps for no-code builders

AI cons

  • Credit-based usage needs monitoring
  • Advanced Sidekick tiers cost extra

Best fit: Business teams that want AI agents without technical setup.

#3

ClickUp

Best for choice of AI models

ClickUp Brain is a multi-model assistant inside ClickUp, with Super Agents for role-specific work, AI fields, meeting notes and enterprise search.

Key AI features

  • ClickUp Brain: chat, writing and Q&A across tasks and docs, with access to several leading AI models.
  • Super Agents and ambient agents for proactive, role-based automation.
  • AI fields, AI meeting notes and enterprise search.
How AI is pricedAI is a separate per-user add-on: Brain AI about $9/user/month ($7.20 billed annually) with 1,500 credits, or Everything AI about $28/user/month ($22.40 annually) with 5,000 credits, on top of a workspace plan.

AI pros

  • Broad AI feature set
  • Choice of AI models
  • Strong value on base plans

AI cons

  • AI add-on can roughly double the per-seat cost
  • Complex for new users

Best fit: Teams already standardising on ClickUp who want an all-in-one AI assistant.

#4

Asana

Best for AI-powered workflows across teams

Asana's AI includes smart features for everyday work, AI Studio for building AI-powered workflows without code, and AI Teammates positioned as agents that work alongside people.

Key AI features

  • Smart features: AI chat, summaries, status drafts and smart field completion.
  • AI Studio: a no-code builder for workflows with AI steps, such as triaging requests and routing work.
  • AI Teammates: agents that take on coordination work.
How AI is pricedSmart features and AI Studio Basic are included on paid plans with usage limits; higher AI Studio tiers and AI Teammates are paid. Usage is credit-based and pooled per account, so check limits for larger teams.

AI pros

  • AI tied to goals, portfolios and workflows
  • No-code AI Studio
  • Familiar, polished interface

AI cons

  • Credit pools can feel small for big teams
  • Advanced AI tiers cost extra

Best fit: Cross-functional teams that want AI to coordinate intake and handoffs.

#5

Smartsheet

Best for AI in spreadsheet-style project data

Smartsheet adds AI to its grid-based platform: Smart Assist for summaries and bulk actions, Smart Columns that process rows automatically, and AI dashboard building.

Key AI features

  • Smart Assist: summarise projects, identify risks and dependencies, take bulk actions and draft updates.
  • Smart Columns: summarise, translate and analyse sentiment as rows change.
  • AI dashboard builder, formula generation and data analysis.
  • MCP server and connections to external AI assistants (Business and above).
How AI is pricedSmartsheet lists its core AI tools across all paid plans, with connections to external AI tools from Business upwards.

AI pros

  • AI features included in paid plans
  • Great for data-heavy PMOs
  • Natural-language formulas

AI cons

  • Spreadsheet model is less visual
  • Agent capabilities are newer than some rivals

Best fit: PMOs and operations teams that run projects in sheets and dashboards.

#6

Jira

Best AI for software teams

Jira uses Atlassian's Rovo AI for search, chat, agents and an agent builder across Jira, Confluence and connected apps.

Key AI features

  • Rovo Search across Atlassian and connected third-party apps.
  • Rovo Chat for questions, summaries and creating work items.
  • Rovo Agents for development, IT and service workflows, plus Rovo Studio to build custom agents.
How AI is pricedAtlassian bundles its AI into paid Jira plans with a monthly credit allowance per user that rises from Standard to Premium and Enterprise; free plans do not include it.

AI pros

  • Deep integration with software workflows
  • AI included in paid plans
  • Works across Confluence and Jira Service Management

AI cons

  • Credit allowances are modest on Standard
  • Best value only inside the Atlassian ecosystem

Best fit: Software and IT teams already on Jira and Confluence.

#7

Notion

Best for AI docs and knowledge

Notion AI brings an agent, meeting notes and enterprise search into Notion's docs-and-databases workspace.

Key AI features

  • Notion Agent: carries out multi-step tasks across pages and databases.
  • AI Meeting Notes: transcription and summaries.
  • Enterprise Search across your workspace and connected apps.
  • Writing, autofill and Q&A on your workspace content.
How AI is pricedFull Notion AI is included on Business ($20/member/month, $16 billed annually) and Enterprise; Free and Plus offer only a trial of AI features.

AI pros

  • Excellent for project docs and wikis
  • AI included in Business plan
  • Flexible databases

AI cons

  • Lighter on scheduling and resource management
  • Full AI needs the Business plan

Best fit: Teams whose projects live in docs, specs and knowledge bases.

#8

Microsoft Planner

Best for Microsoft 365 and Copilot users

Microsoft Planner adds AI through Microsoft 365 Copilot, including the Planner Agent, which turns a task into a step-by-step plan your team can edit together.

Key AI features

  • Planner Agent: generates an AI outline of how to complete a task, stored in an editable Loop component.
  • Copilot assistance across Planner, Teams, Outlook and the rest of Microsoft 365.
How AI is pricedThe Planner Agent requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence plus a Planner basic or premium plan; it reached general availability in 2026.

AI pros

  • Works inside tools most offices already use
  • Strong security and compliance
  • Copilot across Microsoft 365

AI cons

  • Needs a separate Copilot licence
  • Less depth than dedicated work management platforms

Best fit: Organisations standardised on Microsoft 365 with Copilot licences.

#9

Motion

Best for AI auto-scheduling

Motion is an AI-first tool that automatically schedules tasks into calendars and offers AI 'employees' such as a project manager and executive assistant.

Key AI features

  • Auto-scheduling: plans tasks and projects into your calendar and re-plans when things change.
  • AI employees on higher tiers, including a Project Manager agent.
  • Task, project and calendar management with AI chat.
How AI is pricedPlans start at about $29/month for one user (AI Workplace) and $49/month for AI Employees Starter, with team tiers above; annual billing gives the lowest prices.

AI pros

  • Excellent automatic scheduling
  • AI agents for common roles
  • Good for individuals and small teams

AI cons

  • Less suited to complex, multi-team portfolios
  • Team pricing rises quickly

Best fit: Individuals and small teams who want AI to plan their day and deadlines.

What AI does in project management

  • Summaries and status updates: condense tasks, comments and meetings into updates for stakeholders; see project status reports.
  • Intake and triage: read requests, fill in fields, set priority and route work to the right team.
  • Planning help: break goals into tasks, suggest schedules and draft work breakdown structures.
  • Risk spotting: flag overdue, blocked or at-risk work early; see project risk management.
  • Agents: carry out multi-step processes such as assigning, chasing and updating work, with people approving key actions.
  • Search and Q&A: answer questions across projects, docs and connected apps.

How to choose an AI project management tool

  1. Start from the jobs you want AI to do (summaries, triage, scheduling, reporting, agents).
  2. Check which plan includes those features and how credits or actions are counted.
  3. Confirm security: data residency, training use, retention and admin controls.
  4. Pilot with one team on real projects for a month and measure time saved.
  5. Keep people in charge of decisions; let AI propose and automate the routine.

Frequently asked questions

What are AI project management tools?

AI project management tools are platforms that use artificial intelligence to plan, summarise, prioritise and automate project work, from drafting status updates to running agents that triage requests or flag risks.

What is the best AI project management tool in 2026?

Our top pick is Wrike, because its agents and Copilot work on full project, resource and portfolio data. monday.com is easiest for AI agents, ClickUp offers the widest choice of AI models, and Jira is best for software teams.

Are AI project management tools free?

Some AI features are free or included: Smartsheet and Jira include AI in paid plans, Wrike includes AI Essentials from its Team plan, and monday.com gives accounts a monthly credit allowance. Advanced agents usually cost extra.

How much do AI project management tools cost?

Pricing varies: some include AI in the plan, others charge per user (ClickUp's AI add-on starts around $9/user/month) or by credits and actions. Always check credit limits, because heavy agent use can raise costs.

Can AI project management tools replace project managers?

No. AI project management tools automate coordination such as summaries, triage and reminders, but people still set goals, make trade-offs, manage stakeholders and own decisions. See our guide to the future of project management.

What can AI agents do in AI project management tools?

Agents in AI project management tools can triage requests, assign work, draft status reports, summarise meetings, flag risks and update fields, usually with a person approving important actions.

Are AI project management tools secure?

Leading AI project management tools offer enterprise controls, but check where data is processed, whether it is used for training, data retention and admin controls before enabling AI for sensitive projects.

Which AI project management tool is best for small teams?

For small teams, ClickUp, monday.com or Motion are good AI project management tools; Wrike's Team plan includes AI Essentials if you expect to grow.

Which AI project management tool is best for enterprises?

Wrike, Smartsheet, Asana, monday.com and Jira all offer enterprise-grade AI project management tools with admin and security controls; Wrike and Smartsheet are strongest for PMOs.

What is MCP in AI project management tools?

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) lets external AI assistants securely read and act on data in AI project management tools; Wrike, monday.com and Smartsheet offer MCP servers.

How do I choose between AI project management tools?

List the jobs you want AI to do, check which plan includes them, test with real projects, compare credit limits and confirm security requirements before rolling AI project management tools out widely.
