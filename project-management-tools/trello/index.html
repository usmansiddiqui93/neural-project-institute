What is Trello?

Trello launched in 2011 and was acquired by Atlassian in 2017. It popularised digital Kanban boards with lists and cards, extended through Power-Ups and Butler automation.

Premium adds timeline, calendar, table and dashboard views, but Trello remains best for simple workflows.

Key features

Boards, lists and cards

Intuitive Kanban boards with checklists, labels and attachments.

Power-Ups

Add features and integrations to boards.

Automation

Built-in Butler automation with rules and buttons.

Views

Calendar, timeline, table, dashboard and map views on Premium.

AI

AI capabilities on paid plans.

Frequently asked questions

What is Trello?

Trello is a project and work management tool from Atlassian, launched in 2011. Trello is the simplest way to start managing work visually. Its cards and boards are intuitive and inexpensive, but it lacks the scheduling, resource and portfolio depth needed for complex projects.

How much does Trello cost?

Free; paid from $5/user/month. Plans: Free: $0; Standard: $5/user/month annual ($6 monthly); Premium: $10/user/month annual ($12.50 monthly); Enterprise: $17.50/user/month (annual). Prices checked 5 October 2026.

Does Trello have a free plan?

Yes (up to 10 collaborators per workspace).

Does Trello offer a free trial?

Free trial of Premium.

Is Trello good for small teams?

Yes. Individuals and small teams

Is Trello good for large enterprises?

It can work for larger organisations, but tools such as Wrike or Smartsheet offer more enterprise depth.

Does Trello have Gantt charts?

It offers a basic timeline, but not full Gantt scheduling with dependencies on all plans.

Does Trello support Agile and Scrum?

It supports Agile boards and some sprint features, but dedicated tools like Jira go further.

Does Trello have time tracking?

Limited, or via integrations.

What are the best Trello alternatives?

Top alternatives include Asana (for more structured project management), monday.com (for more views and automation), ClickUp (for far more features at low cost), Wrike (for full project and resource management). See our full list of Trello alternatives.

How is Trello rated by users?

4.4/5 on G2 from 14,063 reviews and 4.5/5 on Capterra from 23,624 reviews (checked 5 October 2026).

Who should not use Trello?

Teams managing complex schedules, budgets or many projects.
