Trello vs Basecamp (2026): Which Is Better?

Trello is best for simple kanban boards; Basecamp is best for simple team and client communication. We compare pricing, features, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings to help you choose.

By Neural Project InstituteUpdated 5 October 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Overall winner: Trello (7.8/10 vs 7.6/10), winning 3 of 7 categories.
  • Choose Trello for individuals and small teams that want simple, visual Kanban boards.
  • Choose Basecamp for agencies and small businesses that want calm, communication-centred projects with flat pricing.
  • Price: Trello Free; paid from $5/user/month; Basecamp Free (1 project); paid from $25/month flat.

Quick verdict

Trello is perfect for simple boards. When projects grow in complexity, teams typically move to Wrike, Asana or monday.com.

Basecamp is ideal for simple, communication-focused projects. Teams that outgrow it usually move to Wrike, Asana or monday.com for deeper project control.

Which should you choose?

Choose Trello if…

  • Individuals and small teams
  • Simple workflows such as content calendars or recruiting pipelines
  • Extremely easy to learn matters to you

Choose Basecamp if…

  • Agencies and consultancies working with clients
  • Small businesses wanting simple project communication
  • Very simple and calm to use matters to you

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Trello or Basecamp?

In our review, Trello scores 7.8/10 and Basecamp 7.6/10. Trello wins on ease of use, agile & software teams, value for money; Basecamp is better for collaboration, scalability & enterprise readiness.

Is Trello cheaper than Basecamp?

Trello: Free; paid from $5/user/month. Basecamp: Free (1 project); paid from $25/month flat. Compare total cost at your team size, including seat minimums and add-ons.

Do Trello and Basecamp have free plans?

Trello: Yes (up to 10 collaborators per workspace). Basecamp: Yes (1 project).

Which is easier to use, Trello or Basecamp?

Trello scores higher for ease of use in our review (Trello 10/10, Basecamp 9/10).

Which is better for Agile teams, Trello or Basecamp?

Trello is stronger for Agile and software teams in our scoring.

Which has better resource and portfolio management?

Neither (tie) scores higher for planning and resource management (Trello 4/10, Basecamp 4/10).

Which is better for large enterprises?

Basecamp scores higher for scalability and enterprise readiness.

How do Trello and Basecamp compare on G2 and Capterra?

Trello: 4.4/5 on G2 (14,063 reviews) and 4.5/5 on Capterra (23,624). Basecamp: 4.1/5 on G2 (5,489) and 4.4/5 on Capterra (14,447). Checked 5 October 2026.

Do Trello and Basecamp have Gantt charts?

Trello: partial. Basecamp: partial (plan-dependent).

Can I switch from Basecamp to Trello (or the reverse)?

Yes. Both support importing data, typically via CSV or built-in importers. Pilot with one project before migrating everything.
