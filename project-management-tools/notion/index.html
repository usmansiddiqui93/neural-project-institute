What is Notion?

Notion launched its current product in 2016 and became popular for its block-based pages and databases. Teams build wikis, meeting notes, roadmaps and project trackers in the same workspace.

Project management in Notion relies on databases with board, timeline and calendar views. This is flexible, but it requires design effort and lacks the dedicated controls of specialist tools.

Key features

Docs and wikis

Rich pages, nested structure and team wikis.

Databases and views

Table, board, timeline, calendar and list views.

Projects and tasks

Project templates with tasks, sprints and dependencies.

Notion AI

Writing, summarising and agent features, with full AI on Business.

Automations

Database automations and integrations.

Frequently asked questions

What is Notion?

Notion is a project and work management tool from Notion Labs, Inc., launched in 2016. Notion combines docs, wikis and databases into a flexible workspace that can be shaped into project trackers. It is excellent for knowledge management and lightweight projects, but lacks native time tracking, resource management and advanced scheduling.

How much does Notion cost?

Free; paid from $10/member/month. Plans: Free: $0; Plus: From $10/member/month; Business: From $20/member/month; Enterprise: Contact sales. Prices checked 5 October 2026.

Does Notion have a free plan?

Yes.

Does Notion offer a free trial?

Free plan available.

Is Notion good for small teams?

Yes. It suits small teams willing to invest some setup time; simpler options include Trello and Basecamp.

Is Notion good for large enterprises?

It can work for larger organisations, but tools such as Wrike or Smartsheet offer more enterprise depth.

Does Notion have Gantt charts?

Yes, Gantt or timeline views are available (plan-dependent).

Does Notion support Agile and Scrum?

It supports Agile boards and some sprint features, but dedicated tools like Jira go further.

Does Notion have time tracking?

Not natively; use an integration.

What are the best Notion alternatives?

Top alternatives include ClickUp (for docs plus richer project management), Wrike (for full project and resource management), Asana (for dedicated task management), Trello (for simple boards). See our full list of Notion alternatives.

How is Notion rated by users?

4.6/5 on G2 from 13,862 reviews and 4.7/5 on Capterra from 2,829 reviews (checked 5 October 2026).

Who should not use Notion?

PMOs needing resource, budget and portfolio controls; Teams wanting ready-made PM features out of the box.
