Best Notion Alternatives in 2026

Thinking of switching from Notion? We compared 9 leading alternatives on features, pricing, ease of use and G2 and Capterra ratings, so you can find the right fit for your team.

By Neural Project InstitutePublished 13 September 2026 · Prices and ratings checked 5 October 2026

Key takeaways

  • Choose Wrike if you want full project and resource management.
  • Choose Asana if you want dedicated task management.
  • Choose ClickUp if you want docs plus richer project management.
  • Choose Trello if you want simple boards.

Why look for Notion alternatives?

Notion is best for docs and projects in one workspace. Teams usually look elsewhere for these reasons:

  • No native time tracking or resource management
  • Complex project setups take effort to build
  • Performance can slow with large databases
  • Less suitable for large, structured portfolios

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Notion?

It depends on why you are switching. Choose Wrike if you want full project and resource management. Choose Asana if you want dedicated task management.

Why do teams look for Notion alternatives?

The most common reasons are: no native time tracking or resource management; complex project setups take effort to build; performance can slow with large databases.

Is there a free alternative to Notion?

Yes: Wrike, Asana, ClickUp, Trello, Basecamp, monday.com, Jira have free plans.

What is a cheaper alternative to Notion?

Trello (from $5/user/month) and ClickUp (from $7/user/month, annual) are among the lowest-priced paid options; Basecamp's flat pricing can be cheaper for large teams.

What is an easier alternative to Notion?

Trello and monday.com are the easiest tools to learn, followed by Asana and Basecamp.

What is the best Notion alternative for enterprises?

Wrike, Smartsheet and monday.com offer the strongest enterprise features in our review.

What is the best Notion alternative for software teams?

Jira is the standard for software teams; ClickUp is a flexible, lower-cost option.

Which Notion alternative has the best user ratings?

On G2, monday.com (4.7), ClickUp (4.6) and Notion (4.6) have some of the highest ratings among the tools we reviewed (checked 5 October 2026).

Can I migrate from Notion to another tool?

Most tools offer CSV import and many provide direct importers from popular competitors. Plan a pilot project, map fields and statuses, and migrate active work first.

How did you choose these Notion alternatives?

We compared the other nine tools in our top 10 on features, pricing, ease of use, scalability and published G2 and Capterra ratings, and ordered them by how well they address common reasons for switching.

How we review. Rankings and scores are the editorial opinion of the Neural Project Institute, based on features, pricing, ease of use, scalability and published user ratings.
