Best Notion Alternatives for Project Management in 2026

The best Notion alternatives for project management are Wrike for full project and resource management, ClickUp for docs plus richer project features at a low price, Asana for structured task management and Trello for simple boards. Notion remains one of the best docs-first workspaces, so many teams keep it as their wiki and move only their project tracking. Below we compare nine alternatives on scores, pricing, free plans and the features Notion does not include.

Key takeaways

  • Wrike (9.4/10) is the strongest Notion alternative when you need time tracking, workload views, Gantt charts and portfolio reporting in one platform.
  • ClickUp (8.9/10) is the closest like-for-like swap: docs, wikis and tasks in one workspace, with time tracking and Gantt charts from $7 per user/month (annual).
  • Asana (9.0/10) and monday.com (9.1/10) give you ready-made project management without designing databases yourself.
  • Trello, Basecamp, ClickUp, Asana, Wrike, monday.com and Jira all have free plans; Smartsheet has a 30-day trial instead.
  • You do not have to leave Notion completely: keeping it for documentation and adding a dedicated project tool is often the best answer.

Why teams look for Notion alternatives

Notion is an excellent workspace. It is rated 4.7/5 from 2,834 reviews on Capterra (checked 7 October 2026), and teams love it for documentation, wikis, meeting notes and flexible databases. The reasons people look elsewhere are almost always about project management, not about Notion being a poor product:

  • You build the project system yourself. Projects in Notion are databases with properties, relations and views that someone has to design and maintain. That is flexible, but dedicated tools give you tasks, dependencies, workload and reports ready to use.
  • No native time tracking or resource management. Notion has no timesheets, workload view or capacity planning. Agencies billing hours and teams doing resource allocation usually need these out of the box.
  • Scheduling is light. Notion's timeline view shows dates and dependencies, but not baselines or critical path analysis, which date-driven projects rely on.
  • Reporting is assembled by hand. Charts and filtered views can make a good dashboard, but there are no packaged portfolio, workload or burndown reports.
  • Performance with large databases. Capterra reviewers who discuss large amounts of content often describe Notion as sluggish, which becomes noticeable as project databases grow.
  • AI and plan costs. Notion AI is included only on the Business plan ($20 per member/month, annual) and Enterprise, and Custom Agents run on paid credits (checked 7 October 2026).

Do you need to leave Notion at all?

Often, no. Many teams get the best result by keeping Notion as the knowledge base (specs, handbooks, meeting notes) and running delivery in a dedicated project tool. Notion's Business plan includes connections to tools such as GitHub and Asana, and most project tools integrate with Slack, Google Drive and each other. Replace Notion completely only if you want a single tool and your alternative's docs are good enough for your team.

Each Notion alternative in brief

1. Wrike: best for full project and resource management

Wrike is our top-ranked tool and the most direct answer to Notion's project management gaps. It includes interactive Gantt charts with dependencies, critical path and baselines on paid plans, workload charts, time tracking, cross-project portfolio views, request forms, proofing and approvals, and dashboards. Notion is easier to start with and better for long-form documentation; Wrike is far stronger once you manage many projects and people. Wrike has a free plan, and paid plans start with Team at $10 per user/month (annual, 2 to 15 users) and Business at $25 (checked 7 October 2026).

2. Asana: best for dedicated task management

Asana gives you clean, ready-made task and project management: list, board, calendar, timeline and Gantt views, rules, forms and dashboards from Starter, with portfolios, goals, workload and time tracking on Advanced. You do not need to design databases, which is the main relief for teams coming from Notion. Notion still wins for wikis and documents. The free Personal plan is limited to two seats; Starter costs $10.99 per user/month annually (checked 7 October 2026).

Notion alternatives by use case

Best free Notion alternative

ClickUp's Free Forever plan has the broadest feature set, including docs, though Gantt is limited to a trial. Trello's Free plan is the simplest and allows 10 collaborators per Workspace. Jira is free for up to 10 users, Wrike's free plan covers basic task management on board and table views, Basecamp's free plan covers one project, and monday.com's covers up to two seats.

Cheapest Notion alternative

Trello Standard ($5 per user/month annual) and ClickUp Unlimited ($7) are the lowest per-user prices. For larger teams with few projects, Basecamp's flat pricing can cost less overall. All are cheaper than Notion Business ($20), although Notion Plus at $10 compares closely.

Best for start-ups and small teams

ClickUp suits small teams that want docs and projects together; Trello and Basecamp suit teams that want something simpler.

Best for agencies and client work

Wrike combines time tracking, workload, proofing and client-facing request forms. Basecamp is the simpler choice for client communication, with flat pricing and controlled client access.

Best for enterprises and PMOs

Wrike and Smartsheet score 10/10 for scalability in our review, with portfolio management, governance and advanced reporting. A project management office running many projects at once will find either far more structured than a Notion workspace.

Best for Agile and software teams

Jira is the standard for software teams. ClickUp is the alternative for teams who want sprints, docs and lower complexity in one tool.

Best for docs-first teams

ClickUp and monday.com have the strongest built-in docs among the alternatives, and Basecamp keeps docs and files inside each project. If documentation matters most, test the editor with your real content before you switch.

If you use Notion mainly for notes and wikis

Our list compares project management tools, because that is what we review. If you use Notion mostly as a personal notebook or company wiki, the right alternative may be a dedicated knowledge tool instead. Examples include Confluence (Atlassian's wiki, often paired with Jira), Microsoft Loop and OneNote for Microsoft 365 organisations, and local-first note apps for personal knowledge management. These sit outside our scored top 10, so check their current plans and features directly.

If you use Notion for both notes and projects, the docs features in ClickUp, monday.com and Basecamp may be enough to replace both. If they are not, keeping Notion for knowledge and moving projects is usually less disruptive.

How to choose the right Notion alternative

  1. Write down what Notion is not doing for you. Time tracking, workload, Gantt depth, reporting, performance or price: the answer points to different tools.
  2. Decide whether you are replacing Notion or adding to it. If your wiki works well, look for a project tool that integrates with Notion rather than one with strong docs.
  3. Shortlist two or three tools. Use the use-case section above and our head-to-head comparisons.
  4. Check the plan you would actually need. Many of the features that make an alternative attractive sit on mid or upper tiers.
  5. Run one real project in each trial. Rebuild a live project with its tasks, dates, owners and a status report, using the people who will work in it.
  6. Compare total cost. Include seat minimums, AI add-ons or credits, guests and any integrations you need.

How to migrate from Notion

Moving out of Notion is straightforward for simple databases and more work for complex, interlinked workspaces. According to Notion's help centre, you can export pages and databases as Markdown and CSV (pages as Markdown, databases as CSV), HTML or PDF, and export a whole workspace from Settings on desktop or web; the download link expires after seven days.

ClickUp offers a direct Notion importer on all plans. Its help centre says databases become folders and lists, Notion properties become text custom fields, and pages go into a ClickUp Doc. Some content does not transfer, including inline database comments and attachments and page history, and toggles and callouts arrive as plain text. Most other alternatives import CSV files, so export each Notion database to CSV and map its columns.

  1. Audit your workspace and archive anything no longer used.
  2. Agree how Notion properties map to the new tool's fields, statuses and owners.
  3. Export active project databases to CSV (or use a direct importer) and run a test import.
  4. Rebuild views, automations and dashboards in the new tool rather than copying old workarounds.
  5. Move one team or project first, then the rest once the pilot works.
  6. Keep Notion read-only for reference, or keep it as your wiki, until you are confident nothing is missing.
Tip

Notion relations and rollups do not survive a CSV export as live links. Plan to recreate them as task dependencies, subtasks or project links in your new tool.

A simple starting structure, such as our free project tracker template, can help you decide which fields you actually need before you import.

Our verdict

Notion is a superb place for documents and lightweight project tracking, and it earns its place in our top 10. When project work outgrows it, the right move depends on why. Choose Wrike if you need resource management, time tracking, portfolios and proofing in one platform. Choose ClickUp if you want docs and projects together at a low price. Choose Asana or monday.com for ready-made, easy-to-adopt project management. Choose Trello or Basecamp if you want something simpler than Notion, not more powerful.

Whichever you pick, try it on a real project before you commit, and consider keeping Notion as your wiki.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best alternative to Notion?

For project management, Wrike is our top pick because it adds time tracking, workload views, Gantt charts and portfolio reporting that Notion lacks. ClickUp is the closest like-for-like option, combining docs and projects at a lower price, and Asana is best for dedicated task management.

What is the best Notion alternative for project management?

Wrike (9.4/10) is the most complete. monday.com (9.1) and Asana (9.0) are easier to roll out, and ClickUp (8.9) gives the most features per dollar. All four include project features that Notion requires you to build yourself.

Is there a free alternative to Notion?

Yes. ClickUp, Trello, Wrike, Asana, monday.com, Jira and Basecamp all have free plans, with limits. ClickUp's Free Forever plan is the closest to Notion because it includes docs; Trello's Free plan is the simplest.

What is cheaper than Notion?

Trello Standard ($5 per user/month, annual) and ClickUp Unlimited ($7) are cheaper than Notion Plus ($10) and Business ($20). Basecamp's flat pricing, from $25/month, can be cheaper for larger teams (prices checked 7 October 2026).

Is ClickUp better than Notion?

For project management, ClickUp offers more built-in features, including time tracking, workload views and Gantt charts with baselines. Notion is stronger and simpler for documents and knowledge management. See our ClickUp vs Notion comparison.

Is Asana better than Notion?

Asana is better for structured task, goal and portfolio management with little setup; Notion is better for docs and flexible, custom systems. See Asana vs Notion.

Which Notion alternative has time tracking?

Wrike, ClickUp, Asana (Advanced plan), monday.com (Pro plan) and Basecamp include native time tracking. Notion has none, so teams rely on integrations or manual number fields.

Which Notion alternative is easiest to use?

Trello is the easiest tool in our review, followed by monday.com, Asana and Basecamp. All are quicker to set up for projects than a custom Notion database.

What is the best Notion alternative for enterprises?

Wrike and Smartsheet score highest for scalability and enterprise readiness, with portfolio management, governance and advanced reporting. monday.com and Asana also offer strong enterprise plans.

What is the best Notion alternative for software teams?

Jira is the standard for Agile software teams, with backlogs, sprints and developer integrations. ClickUp is a flexible, lower-cost option that also includes docs.

Can I import my Notion data into another tool?

Yes. Notion exports pages as Markdown, databases as CSV, and whole workspaces as HTML or Markdown and CSV. ClickUp has a direct Notion importer on all plans, and most other tools can import CSV files.

Should I replace Notion or use it alongside a project management tool?

If Notion works well as your wiki, keeping it and adding a dedicated project tool is often the least disruptive option. Replace it fully only if the new tool's docs meet your needs.

Is there a Notion alternative for Microsoft 365 users?

Microsoft Planner is the natural project management choice for Microsoft 365 organisations, with basic Planner included in many business plans. For notes and wikis, Microsoft's own apps such as Loop and OneNote are the usual alternatives.
